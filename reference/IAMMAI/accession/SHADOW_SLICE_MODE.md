# IAMMAI Shadow Slice Mode

## 1. Purpose

This file defines Shadow Slice Mode as the first real accession posture after eligibility.

Its job is to make explicit how one eligible external slice may be mirrored under body law for bounded inspection, comparison, and pre-proof handling while remaining explicitly non-native, non-admitted, and non-vessel.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a sovereign authority layer
- a Contact Law file
- an eligibility file
- an accession proof file
- an admission decision
- a vessel contract
- a migration strategy memo
- a product integration handbook
- a roadmap
- a project-management surface

This file is additive only. It does not replace the constitutional core, the admissibility surfaces, the continuity surfaces, the standing-facing review surfaces, the vessel-family surfaces, [`accession/CONTACT_LAW.md`](./CONTACT_LAW.md), [`accession/ACCESSION_ELIGIBILITY.md`](./ACCESSION_ELIGIBILITY.md), or the master map in [`accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md`](./ACCESSION_LAYER.md).

## 2. Why This File Is Needed Now

After candidacy is distinguished, the body still needs a lawful non-native inspection posture before proof.

Without shadow, the body is pushed toward one of two bad readings. It either stays too abstract to inspect anything real, or it grants too much too early by treating candidacy as though it already implied belonging, proof, or admission.

Shadow is therefore needed so one eligible slice can be made inspectable under body law without merger or premature admission.

This file exists to make that posture legible without overbuilding it.

## 3. Existing Position in the Body

Shadow Slice Mode is the next sublayer already mapped in [`accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md`](./ACCESSION_LAYER.md).

It follows Contact Law and Accession Eligibility. It precedes proof, admission, bound vessel mode, and revocation.

This file therefore binds a mapped sublayer rather than inventing a new family. It stands at the point where candidacy becomes lawfully inspectable without yet becoming admitted.

## 4. What Shadow Slice Mode Answers

Shadow Slice Mode answers the bounded question:

**How may one eligible external slice be lawfully mirrored under body law without being admitted?**

This is a pre-proof, non-native handling question only.

It asks how one slice may be mirrored, inspected, compared, and held for discrepancy visibility under body conditions without being mistaken for proof completion, standing, nativeness, or vesselhood.

## 5. Shadow Is Not Vesselhood

The following distinctions must remain explicit.

- Eligibility is not shadow.
- Shadow is not proof.
- Shadow is not admission.
- Shadow is not vesselhood.
- Shadow is not standing.
- Shadow is not nativeness.

Shadow means only that one eligible external slice is being mirrored in bounded form for lawful non-native handling.

It does not create:

- native belonging
- authority
- standing-facing consequence by itself
- admission pressure by itself
- whole-system inheritance

Shadow is therefore a bounded mirror posture, not a promotion.

## 6. What Shadow Does

Shadow Slice Mode lawfully does the following in bounded form.

### Bounded Mirroring

It mirrors one eligible external slice only. It does not mirror an entire outside system by atmosphere or by convenience.

### Discrepancy Visibility

It makes discrepancies visible between the mirrored slice and the body’s own lawful distinctions, including source, derivative, presentation, role, and carrier relation.

### Lawful Comparison

It permits bounded comparison under body law without granting the slice native status. The point is to compare without merging.

### Pre-Proof Inspection

It permits bounded inspection before proof. It lets the body see what kind of slice is actually present, what remains legible, and what may or may not be lawfully provable later.

### Preservation Of Source Legibility

It keeps source legibility intact. The mirrored slice must not lose origin, source relation, or predecessor relation merely because it has been placed under shadow.

### Jurisdiction Distinction

It keeps jurisdiction distinction visible. The slice is mirrored under body conditions, but it is not thereby converted into a native body surface.

These functions are infrastructural. They let the body inspect without granting itself away.

## 7. What Shadow Does Not Do

Shadow Slice Mode does not lawfully do the following.

- It does not create nativeness.
- It does not create admission.
- It does not create vesselhood.
- It does not create authority.
- It does not create standing.
- It does not create whole-system inheritance.
- It does not erase source or jurisdiction distinction.
- It does not turn mirrored visibility into native visibility.

Shadow must also not be granted by founder ownership, usefulness, familiarity, urgency, or proximity. Those do not create hidden shadow privilege.

Shadow is therefore non-promotional. It is a bounded mirror posture only.

## 8. Shadow and Non-Native Relation

In Shadow Slice Mode, the external system remains outside while one bounded slice becomes lawfully inspectable.

Source remains source. Origin remains legible. The mirrored slice may be handled under body law for comparison and pre-proof inspection, but that handling does not convert it into native authority or native belonging.

Mirrored visibility is not native visibility. Carried or mirrored legibility does not become ownership, admission, or jurisdictional absorption.

Jurisdiction distinction must therefore remain visible at all times. Shadow that erases origin or treats the mirrored slice as though it had always belonged is unlawful shadow.

## 9. Shadow Outcomes

The lawful outcomes available from shadow posture include the following.

### Proceed To Proof Preparation

If the slice remains legible enough under shadow, it may proceed toward proof preparation.

### Remain In Shadow

If comparison or inspection still needs bounded continuation, the slice may remain in shadow without being promoted.

### Non-Progression

If the slice remains mirrored but does not justify further movement, it may simply stop at non-progression.

### Refusal

If shadow reveals collapse, illegibility, merger demand, hidden override posture, or other blocking conditions, the body may refuse further movement.

Shadow does not create accession momentum by right. A slice in shadow has no entitlement to continue.

## 10. What Shadow Slice Mode Does Not Grant

Shadow Slice Mode does not itself create:

- proof
- admission
- vesselhood
- standing
- authority
- jurisdictional belonging
- closure

It also does not grant:

- founder privilege
- usefulness privilege
- urgency privilege
- familiarity privilege
- whole-system promotion by one mirrored slice

A mirrored slice in shadow is still only a mirrored slice in shadow.
