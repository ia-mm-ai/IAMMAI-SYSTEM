# IAMMAI Accession Eligibility

## 1. Purpose

This file defines Accession Eligibility as the second threshold of the Accession Layer.

Its job is to make explicit when one bounded external slice may or may not become an accession candidate after contact has already been lawfully distinguished, but before shadow, proof, admission, or vesselhood are considered.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a sovereign authority layer
- a Contact Law file
- a shadow-mode file
- an accession proof file
- an admission decision
- a vessel contract
- a product strategy memo
- a partner qualification memo
- a roadmap
- a project-management surface

This file is additive only. It does not replace the constitutional core, the admissibility surfaces, the continuity surfaces, the standing-facing review surfaces, the vessel-family surfaces, [`accession/CONTACT_LAW.md`](./CONTACT_LAW.md), or the master map in [`accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md`](./ACCESSION_LAYER.md).

## 2. Why This File Is Needed Now

After lawful contact is distinguished, the body still needs a separate candidate threshold before shadow can begin.

Candidacy cannot remain vague or be granted by vibe, pressure, usefulness, willingness, urgency, familiarity, or relational closeness. If that threshold is not explicit, the body loses the ability to say "not a candidate" without drama and "candidate only" without premature promotion.

The body must therefore be able to decide whether one bounded external slice may proceed toward shadow at all, without laundering contact into candidacy or candidacy into later accession stages.

This file exists to make that threshold legible without overbuilding it.

## 3. Existing Position in the Body

Accession Eligibility is the next sublayer already mapped in [`accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md`](./ACCESSION_LAYER.md).

It follows Contact Law and precedes shadow, proof, admission, bound vessel mode, and revocation.

This file therefore binds a mapped sublayer rather than inventing a new family. It stands between first external threshold relation and any later candidate handling.

## 4. What Accession Eligibility Answers

Accession Eligibility answers the bounded question:

**Under what lawful conditions may one bounded external slice become an accession candidate at all?**

This is a candidate-threshold question only.

It asks whether one slice may proceed toward shadow as a candidate. It does not yet ask whether shadow should actually begin, whether proof should be assembled, whether admission should occur, whether vesselhood should be considered, or whether any standing consequence should follow.

## 5. Eligibility Is Not Admission

The following distinctions must remain explicit.

- Contact is not eligibility.
- Eligibility is not shadow.
- Eligibility is not proof.
- Eligibility is not admission.
- Eligibility is not vesselhood.
- Eligibility is not standing.

Eligibility means only that one bounded external slice may be treated as a candidate.

It does not create:

- shadow
- admission pressure
- proof entitlement
- authority
- jurisdictional belonging
- closure

Candidacy is therefore a bounded permission to proceed to the next threshold only. It is not a reward, not a promise, and not an upgrade into belonging.

## 6. Minimal Eligibility Conditions

For one bounded external slice to be eligible as a candidate, the following minimal conditions should hold in lawful bounded form.

### One Bounded Slice Can Be Named

The body must be able to identify one bounded slice rather than being asked to absorb a whole external system at once.

Whole-system aspiration is insufficient. Eligibility is slice-first.

### Bounded Source / Lineage Visibility Is Possible

The candidate slice must permit bounded source or lineage visibility sufficient for later inspection. This does not require total perfection, but it does require more than opaque usefulness or atmosphere.

### Role Distinction Is Possible

The body must be able to distinguish role relation clearly enough to proceed. Source, derivative, presentation, carrier, and operator relation must not be hopelessly fused into one unreadable surface.

### Source / Derivative / Presentation Relation Is Not Hopelessly Collapsed

Eligibility requires that the slice can be read without total confusion between what is source-bound, what is derivative, what is presentation, and what is merely envelope, convenience, or display.

### Bounded Inspection Is Possible

The slice must be inspectable in bounded form. If no bounded observation or later bounded shadow could even be meaningfully attempted, candidacy is not yet lawful.

### Total-Merger Demand Is Absent

Eligibility requires that the approach not demand whole-system admission, total inheritance, or pre-granted belonging as the price of inspection.

These conditions are minimal and infrastructural. They do not prove the slice. They only make candidacy legible enough to proceed.

## 7. Disqualifying Conditions

The following kinds of conditions block candidacy at eligibility level.

### Impossible Inspection

If the slice cannot be inspected in bounded form, candidacy is blocked.

### No Meaningful Archive or Lineage Visibility

If no meaningful archive, source relation, or lineage visibility is available for the slice, candidacy is blocked.

### Hidden Override Posture

If the slice carries hidden override behavior, hidden authority assumptions, or demands to bypass bounded inspection, candidacy is blocked.

### Whole-System Admission First

If the approach requires whole-system admission, whole-system inheritance, or total-merger treatment before one bounded slice can even be named, candidacy is blocked.

### Authority Confusion Too Severe For Bounded Candidacy

If source, derivative, presentation, carrier, and operator relation are so collapsed that the body cannot tell what kind of thing it is being asked to treat as a candidate, candidacy is blocked.

### Founder Privilege Or Proximity Dependency

If candidacy depends on founder ownership, familiarity, usefulness, urgency, or relational proximity as a substitute for bounded legibility, candidacy is blocked.

Disqualification is a lawful threshold result, not a moral drama. It means the slice is not a candidate under present bounded conditions.

## 8. Eligibility and Bounded Candidacy

To be eligible is to be a candidate only.

Bounded candidacy means the body may hold one slice as candidate matter for possible later shadow handling. It does not mean that shadow has begun. It does not mean proof has begun. It does not mean admission is likely. It does not mean vesselhood is in motion.

Candidacy may still later end in:

- refusal
- non-progression
- narrowed observation only
- later determination that shadow should not begin

Eligibility therefore preserves lawful next-step possibility only. It does not create accession momentum by itself.

## 9. What Accession Eligibility Does Not Grant

Accession Eligibility does not itself create:

- shadow
- proof
- admission
- vesselhood
- standing
- authority
- jurisdictional belonging
- closure

It also does not grant:

- founder privilege
- usefulness privilege
- urgency privilege
- familiarity privilege
- whole-system inheritance by aspiration

An eligible slice is only a candidate slice. That is all.
