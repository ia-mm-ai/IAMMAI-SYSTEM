# IAMMAI Accession Proof Slice

## 1. Purpose

This file defines Accession Proof Slice as the proof posture for one bounded shadowed external slice.

Its job is to make explicit how one bounded shadowed slice may or may not prove itself under body law without collapsing into admission, vesselhood, standing, or whole-system conversion.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a sovereign authority layer
- a Contact Law file
- an eligibility file
- a shadow-mode file
- an admission decision
- a vessel contract
- a migration strategy memo
- a product integration handbook
- a roadmap
- a project-management surface

This file is additive only. It does not replace the constitutional core, the admissibility surfaces, the continuity surfaces, the standing-facing review surfaces, the vessel-family surfaces, [`accession/CONTACT_LAW.md`](./CONTACT_LAW.md), [`accession/ACCESSION_ELIGIBILITY.md`](./ACCESSION_ELIGIBILITY.md), [`accession/SHADOW_SLICE_MODE.md`](./SHADOW_SLICE_MODE.md), or the master map in [`accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md`](./ACCESSION_LAYER.md).

## 2. Why This File Is Needed Now

After shadow, the body still needs a lawful proof posture before admission.

Without proof, shadow remains observational but cannot lawfully test whether one slice can actually stand under body law in bounded form. The body can see the slice, but it cannot yet determine whether the slice can remain lawful under its conditions without hidden carryover, merger, or counterfeit nativeness.

Proof is therefore needed so one bounded slice can be tried under body conditions without premature admission or merger.

This file exists to make that posture legible without overbuilding it.

## 3. Existing Position in the Body

Accession Proof Slice is the next sublayer already mapped in [`accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md`](./ACCESSION_LAYER.md).

It follows Contact Law, Accession Eligibility, and Shadow Slice Mode. It precedes admission, bound vessel mode, and revocation.

This file therefore binds a mapped sublayer rather than inventing a new family. It stands at the point where one mirrored slice may be tested under body law without yet being admitted.

## 4. What Accession Proof Slice Answers

Accession Proof Slice answers the bounded question:

**How may one shadowed external slice prove that it can stand under body law in bounded form without being admitted?**

This is a pre-admission proof question only.

It asks whether one slice can remain lawful under body conditions while keeping provenance, role distinction, source legibility, and jurisdiction distinction intact. It does not yet ask whether the slice is admitted, whether it becomes a vessel, or whether any standing consequence follows.

## 5. Proof Is Not Admission

The following distinctions must remain explicit.

- Shadow is not proof.
- Proof is not admission.
- Proof is not vesselhood.
- Proof is not standing.
- Proof is not nativeness.

Proof means only that one shadowed slice is being tested under bounded body conditions.

It does not create:

- admission
- native authority
- vessel status
- standing-facing consequence by itself
- whole-system inheritance

Proof is therefore a bounded test posture, not a promotion.

## 6. What Proof Tests

Accession Proof Slice legitimately tests the following in bounded form.

### Bounded Provenance

The slice must show bounded provenance under the new read. What is being tested must remain traceable rather than atmospheric.

### Source And Lineage Legibility

The slice must preserve source and lineage legibility strongly enough that the body can tell what remains source-bound, what remains carried, and what is being tested under the new conditions.

### Role Distinction

The slice must preserve role distinction. Source, derivative, presentation, carrier, and operator relation must remain distinguishable enough for lawful proof reading.

### No Hidden Authority Carryover

The slice must not depend on silent authority inheritance, hidden founder privilege, usefulness privilege, proximity privilege, or any other form of carryover by convenience.

### Anti-Merger Verification

The slice must remain non-merged while under proof. Proof tests whether the slice can stand under body law without collapsing source relation, jurisdiction distinction, or canonical versus derivative distinction.

### Lawful Outputs Under Body Conditions

The slice must be able to produce lawful outputs under body conditions in bounded form. What emerges under proof must remain typed, non-counterfeit, and non-sovereign.

### Reversible Failure

Failure under proof must remain reversible. A failed slice does not disappear into cleanup theater. Its failure remains lineage-visible rather than erased.

These are infrastructural proof questions. They do not yet settle whether the slice should be admitted.

## 7. What Proof Does Not Do

Accession Proof Slice does not lawfully do the following.

- It does not create nativeness.
- It does not create admission.
- It does not create vesselhood.
- It does not create authority.
- It does not create standing.
- It does not create whole-system inheritance.
- It does not erase source or jurisdiction distinction.
- It does not turn proof visibility into native visibility.

Proof must also not be granted special force by founder ownership, usefulness, familiarity, urgency, or proximity. Those do not create hidden proof privilege.

Proof is therefore non-promotional. It is a bounded test posture only.

## 8. Proof Outcomes

The lawful outcomes available from proof posture include the following.

### Proceed To Admission Consideration

If the slice proves capable of remaining lawful under body conditions in bounded form, it may proceed to admission consideration.

### Remain Non-Admitted

If the slice is still under proof or only partially clarified, it may remain non-admitted without forced promotion.

### Non-Progression

If the slice does not justify further movement, it may stop at non-progression without drama.

### Refusal

If proof reveals hidden authority carryover, collapsed role distinction, unlawful outputs, merger pressure, or other blocking conditions, the body may refuse further movement.

### Reversible Failure With Lineage Preserved

If proof fails, the failure remains reversible and lineage-visible. The slice does not gain nativeness by effort, and its failure does not need to be erased to keep the body tidy.

Proof does not create accession momentum by right. A slice in proof has no entitlement to continue.

## 9. What Accession Proof Slice Does Not Grant

Accession Proof Slice does not itself create:

- admission
- vesselhood
- standing
- authority
- jurisdictional belonging
- closure

It also does not grant:

- founder privilege
- usefulness privilege
- urgency privilege
- familiarity privilege
- whole-system proof by one slice

A proved slice is still only a proved slice awaiting later bounded decision.
