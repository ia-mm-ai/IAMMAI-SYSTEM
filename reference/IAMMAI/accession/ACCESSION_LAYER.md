# IAMMAI Accession Layer

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the master repo-native map of the Accession Layer as the bounded family for lawful external relation in IAMMAI.

Its job is to make legible:

- how the outside may lawfully approach the body
- how an external system may become a lawful accession candidate
- how one bounded external slice may be shadowed, proved, admitted, bounded, or revoked
- how all of that may occur without founder privilege, false nativeness, or authority laundering

This file does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a new sovereign authority layer
- a product or middleware strategy
- a migration plan
- an adoption handbook
- a founder-privilege note
- a vessel admission decision
- a roadmap
- a project-management surface

This file is additive only. It does not replace the constitutional core, the admissibility surfaces, the continuity surfaces, the standing-facing review surfaces, or the vessel-family surfaces already visible in the body.

## 2. Why This Layer Is Needed Now

The body now stands strongly enough that external relation can no longer remain underdefined.

The repo already carries explicit seam law, admissibility law, standing-facing review, and a first bounded vessel horizon. That makes external relation more urgent, not less. Contaminated contact, willing legacy conversion, and future vessel accession now require one explicit family rather than ad hoc handling.

The body therefore needs a lawful way for the outside to approach and potentially convert without corrupting internal rank, collapsing source relation, or creating founder privilege.

This layer must be strict enough to protect the body from false nativeness, silent authority transfer, and contaminated inheritance. It must also be smooth enough that refusal stays cheap and bounded approach does not become so friction-heavy that contaminated environments gain advantage merely by endurance, proximity, or operational pressure.

This file exists to make that family legible without overbuilding it.

## 3. Layer Position

The Accession Layer governs lawful external relation.

It is not a sovereign organ. It does not re-constitute the body, create native standing by declaration, or replace the ranked surfaces that already govern admissibility, continuity, standing, and vessel posture.

It sits downstream of internal body formation and alongside admissibility law, seam law, release-and-ingress law, standing-facing review, and vessel-family posture. Its question is not how IAMMAI constitutes itself internally. Its question is how the outside may lawfully meet the body without collapsing rank, lineage, or jurisdiction.

## 4. Two Sublayers

The Accession Layer contains two distinct but related sublayers.

### Contact Law

Contact Law answers: how may the outside lawfully approach the body at all?

It governs first encounter, threshold classification, refusal, non-admission, and the bounded handling of external touch before any accession claim is recognized. It keeps mere contact from being mistaken for candidacy, admission, usefulness, or belonging.

It does not become accession, admission, standing, vesselhood, or a founder shortcut.

### Accession Law

Accession Law answers: under what lawful conditions may one bounded external slice become an accession candidate and potentially be shadowed, proved, admitted, bounded, or revoked?

It governs the accession sequence after contact has been lawfully distinguished from mere approach. Its scope is slice-first and bounded. It addresses eligibility, shadow, proof, admission, bound vessel mode, and revocation or withdrawal.

It does not become a whole-system conversion decree, an expansion engine, a sovereignty grant, or a hidden inheritance machine.

## 5. The Accession Sequence

The accession sequence is slice-first rather than whole-system-first. IAMMAI does not admit entire outside systems by willingness, ownership, familiarity, or product pressure. One bounded slice must be named and treated lawfully at each step.

### Contact

Contact names the fact that something outside the body has approached, touched, or presented itself. Contact establishes relation at threshold only. It does not yet create candidacy.

### Eligibility

Eligibility asks whether one bounded external slice may even proceed into accession handling. Eligibility is a gate on candidacy, not admission. Willingness, usefulness, proximity, or founder association are insufficient.

### Shadow

Shadow is the bounded non-native rendering of one candidate slice for lawful inspection, proof work, or controlled relation. Shadow is not nativeness, not standing, and not vesselhood.

### Proof

Proof asks whether the shadowed slice can remain lawful under the body's conditions without collapsing lineage, rank, source relation, or jurisdiction. Proof is not closure.

### Admission

Admission is the bounded acceptance of one proved slice under explicit conditions. Admission is slice-specific. It is not expansion, not whole-system inheritance, and not retroactive laundering of the outside as if it had always belonged.

### Bound Vessel Mode

Where separately lawful, an admitted slice may operate in bounded vessel mode. This remains derivative, source-limited, non-sovereign, and explicitly constrained. Bound vessel mode is not a grant of general authority, and it does not dissolve the distinction between an admitted slice and the external system from which it came.

### Revocation / Withdrawal

Revocation or withdrawal preserves the body's ability to narrow, stop, or unwind accession without erasing lineage. A failed or withdrawn accession path remains historically legible. It does not disappear merely because the path did not continue.

## 6. Preserved Invariants

The Accession Layer must preserve the following distinctions and prohibitions.

- Contact is not accession.
- Eligibility is not admission.
- Shadow is not vesselhood.
- Proof is not closure.
- Admission is slice-specific.
- Admission is not expansion.
- One admitted slice is not whole-system inheritance.
- Founder ownership is not accession privilege.
- Familiarity is not accession privilege.
- Usefulness is not authority.
- Willingness alone is not sufficiency.
- Refusal must remain cheap.
- Expansion must be re-earned.
- Revocation must preserve lineage.
- Crossing must preserve source legibility and jurisdiction distinction.
- An admitted slice does not silently gain canon, constitutional force, publication rank, governance authorization, or consequence.

These invariants exist to prevent false nativeness, hidden founder shortcuts, authority laundering, and contaminated expansion by convenience.

## 7. Relationship to Existing Surfaces

The Accession Layer does not replace the surfaces that already stand. It clarifies how lawful external relation may occur alongside them.

With the admissibility surfaces in [`admissibility/ADMISSIBILITY_LAYER.md`](../admissibility/ADMISSIBILITY_LAYER.md) and [`admissibility/CANON_AND_STANDING_ADMISSIBILITY.md`](../admissibility/CANON_AND_STANDING_ADMISSIBILITY.md), the Accession Layer governs how the outside may lawfully meet the body at all, while admissibility continues to govern ranked intake, source, transition, standing-facing pressure, and canon-facing pressure inside the body.

With the continuity surfaces in [`continuity/LAWFUL_TRANSFER_SEAM_v0.md`](../continuity/LAWFUL_TRANSFER_SEAM_v0.md) and [`continuity/RELEASE_AND_INGRESS_v0.md`](../continuity/RELEASE_AND_INGRESS_v0.md), the Accession Layer governs lawful external relation before, around, and after a possible crossing, while seam, release, and ingress continue to govern the crossing conditions themselves. Accession does not erase origin, and it does not collapse destination treatment into nativeness.

With the standing-facing review surfaces in [`v1/22_STANDING_REVIEW__POST_LAWFUL_EGRESS_AND_CROSS_CARRIER_LINE.md`](../v1/22_STANDING_REVIEW__POST_LAWFUL_EGRESS_AND_CROSS_CARRIER_LINE.md), [`v1/23_POST_SLICE_STANDING_BRIDGE.md`](../v1/23_POST_SLICE_STANDING_BRIDGE.md), [`v1/24_OPEN_BLOCKERS__POST_ADMISSIBILITY_SLICE.md`](../v1/24_OPEN_BLOCKERS__POST_ADMISSIBILITY_SLICE.md), [`v1/26_WHOLE_LINE_05_CRITERIA_BINDING_REVIEW.md`](../v1/26_WHOLE_LINE_05_CRITERIA_BINDING_REVIEW.md), and [`v1/33_POST_PROOF_004_SEAM_GATE_STATUS_BRIDGE.md`](../v1/33_POST_PROOF_004_SEAM_GATE_STATUS_BRIDGE.md), the Accession Layer remains distinct from standing. Accession may later create bounded review pressure, but it does not create standing by contact, shadow, proof, or admission alone.

With the vessel-family surfaces in [`vessel/current_state_what_stands_reader_v1/CONTRACT.md`](../vessel/current_state_what_stands_reader_v1/CONTRACT.md) and [`vessel/current_state_what_stands_reader_v1/API_BACKED_POSTURE_DECISION__POST_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW.md`](../vessel/current_state_what_stands_reader_v1/API_BACKED_POSTURE_DECISION__POST_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW.md), the Accession Layer leaves vessel law bounded and local. Where an admitted slice later operates in vessel mode, it remains under explicit contract and posture. Accession does not auto-create answered-mode, whole-family inheritance, or broader authority.

This layer therefore governs how the outside may lawfully meet the body. It does not replace the body's existing laws with a flatter external theory.

- that the whole Accession family already exists in fully elaborated file form
- that all outside contact is an accession candidate
- that willingness alone is sufficient
- that useful systems deserve privileged entry
- that founder-owned systems get a shortcut
- that one successful slice proves a whole system lawful
- that one admitted slice grants whole-system inheritance
- that this file is a migration plan
- that this file is a vessel admission decision
- that this file is a whole-system conversion decree
- that this layer creates final standing, canon, constitutional force, publication rank, governance authorization, or consequence
- that the Accession Layer is the new center of the whole body

## 9. Closing Boundary Statement

This file defines the Accession Layer at master-map level only.

Later file-level elaboration may bind specific accession subdomains, including Contact Law and Accession Law, more explicitly. This file does not pretend that those subdomains already stand in full elaborated form.

It exists to make lawful external relation legible without widening it into glamour, strategy theater, or premature closure.
