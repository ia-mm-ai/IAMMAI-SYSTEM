# IAMMAI Regulation Guardrails

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the first bounded runtime-facing regulation guardrails for IAMMAI.

Its job is to explain what regulation must preserve in the current living body, where regulation is already partially present, what runtime pressure points now require explicit guardrails, what the body must not do under pressure, and how regulation remains subordinate to constitutional integrity and distinct from governance.

It does not yet try to define:

- code
- a control-theory derivation
- a replacement for runtime flow, contracts, or failures
- an automated enforcement mechanism
- a new runtime role
- a broader embodiment layer

It is a bounded runtime translation document.

## 2. First Principle

Regulation in runtime is subordinate to constitutional invariant.

Regulation exists to preserve lawful distinction under pressure.  
Regulation must not cosmetically smooth evidence.  
Regulation must not create truth, authority, or continuity by convenience.

In runtime terms, regulation is not a new sovereign layer. It is the guarded posture by which the living body keeps typed sequence, typed non-passage, and typed preservation from collapsing when repeated motion places more pressure on the existing runtime path.

## 3. Why Runtime Regulation Guardrails Are Needed Now

The body has now moved enough that regulation cannot remain theory-only forever.

Repeated runs, visible failure, controlled variation, sequential pressure, and continuity accumulation have already placed the current body under more than one class of bounded pressure. That means the repo now has a living runtime body whose lawful posture can be preserved or softened in practice.

These conditions make explicit runtime guardrails relevant because the pressure is no longer hypothetical. The body now needs a runtime-facing statement of what must remain preserved when motion continues to succeed, fail visibly, thicken continuity, and tempt convenience.

This still does not justify full regulation code or mechanism yet. The present need is narrower: make the runtime guardrails explicit before any deeper translation is attempted.

## 4. What Regulation Must Preserve in the Current Body

Regulation must preserve, at minimum:

- sequence integrity
- truth versus decision distinction
- hold versus softening distinction
- visible non-passage
- canonical versus derivative separation
- continuity remaining subordinate to preserved artifacts
- no history rewrite

More concretely, regulation must keep validation from being treated as truth, governance from being treated as mere continuation of validation, HOLD from being cosmetically restated as harmless delay, derivative summaries from outrunning canonical runtime truth, and continuity from becoming thicker than preserved runs can actually support.

## 5. Where Regulation Is Already Partially Present

The current body already behaves in regulation-like ways at several important points:

- stopping at validation rather than fabricating downstream artifacts
- keeping failure visible rather than smoothing it into apparent completion
- preventing continuity emission on non-passing runs
- preserving append-oriented history rather than rewriting prior turns
- keeping the slice class bounded during controlled variation and pressure testing

These behaviors do not amount to a complete regulation system. They do show that the runtime already contains lawful distinction-preserving behavior that should now be guarded more explicitly.

## 6. Current Pressure Points Needing Explicit Guardrails

The most relevant pressure points now are:

- cumulative run pressure
- continuity thickening across successful turns
- temptation to widen scope when the body is holding
- temptation to soften evidence under hold
- dependence on operator elegance or interpretation
- drift signal families if they begin to recur

These pressure points matter because they are the places where a lawful body that is otherwise working can still start to soften its distinctions under success, repetition, or the desire for cleaner continuity.

## 7. Regulation vs Governance

Governance is explicit lawful force.

Regulation is not authorization. Regulation preserves lawful motion under pressure. Governance may participate in a regulated body because governance affects whether certain motions may proceed, but regulation is not governance-by-another-name.

This distinction matters especially now. Runtime guardrails must not turn regulation into a hidden second force-bearing layer that appears to authorize, deny, hold, or finalize by itself.

## 8. Regulation vs Runtime Flow / Failure Docs

Runtime documents already describe motion, contracts, failure, and handoff.

Regulation guardrails do not replace those documents. They define what must remain preserved when those motions are stressed.

This file is therefore not a duplicate of the runtime layer. It tightens the runtime layer’s lawful posture by stating what must not be softened, collapsed, or cosmetically repaired when the existing flows, contracts, failure surfaces, and handoffs are placed under more pressure.

## 9. Guardrail Statements

The current runtime body should observe the following guardrails:

- do not jump from signal to decision under pressure
- do not treat hold as permission to bleach evidence
- do not let repeated success justify widening the slice class
- do not let continuity outrun preserved artifacts
- do not hide non-passage to preserve appearance of coherence
- do not replace canonical truth with derivative summary
- do not rewrite history in order to restore neatness

These guardrails are intentionally bounded. They do not create new runtime powers. They preserve the lawful posture of the powers and records already present.

## 11. Stop Conditions / Escalation Signals

The following repeated pressure patterns would indicate that regulation may need further translation later:

- recurring manual rescue becoming necessary
- the same seam recurring across multiple pressure levels
- repeated ambiguity around hold versus softening
- repeated continuity pressure beyond current bounded posture
- repeated boundary-softening under success accumulation

These are not yet machine-decidable final verdicts. They are escalation signals that the current bounded runtime posture may no longer be enough without deeper regulation translation.

Later work may refine drift handling, stability claims, and possible regulation embodiment. Any further translation must remain subordinate to constitutional integrity and to actual evidence from the living body rather than to abstract preference for tighter control.
