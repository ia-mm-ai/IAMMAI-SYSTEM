# IAMMAI Release and Ingress v0

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the first bounded continuity note for lawful release from source-side and lawful ingress at destination-side around a transfer seam in IAMMAI.

Its job is to explain what release means before a seam crossing, what ingress means after arrival at a destination boundary, and what each side must preserve so transfer does not collapse lineage, jurisdiction, or canonical distinction.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a runtime contract
- a schema
- a full cross-system jurisdiction stack
- a source-to-destination implementation blueprint
- a generic interoperability theory

It is a bounded continuity-facing document.

## 2. First Principle

Lawful release and lawful ingress are subordinate to constitutional invariant.

Not everything may leave.  
Not everything that arrives may stand.  
Release and ingress must not collapse lineage, jurisdiction, or canonical distinction.

In IAMMAI terms, the seam alone is not enough. What approaches the seam from source-side and what arrival may mean at destination-side must each remain bounded and explicit.

## 3. Why Release and Ingress Need Articulation

The seam governs the crossing itself.

Release governs what may lawfully approach the seam from source-side. Ingress governs what lawful arrival may mean at destination-side.

Without release and ingress articulation, seam logic risks floating in abstraction. Something may appear to cross lawfully while the source side never made bounded release explicit, or while the destination side silently treats arrival as native standing, origin, or authority.

## 4. Lawful Release

Lawful release is source-side permissibility for crossing.

Release does not equal transfer completion.  
Release does not erase what remains canonical at source.  
Release must preserve source legibility, type legibility, and bounded relation.  
Release must not silently export authority, origin erasure, or collapsed derivative summaries.

Release is therefore a source-side bounded condition for approach to the seam, not a claim that destination standing has already been achieved.

## 5. What May Be Released

At a high level, the following may be released where lawful and bounded:

- bounded references
- continuity relations
- carried artifacts where lawful
- explicit transfer metadata

## 6. What Must Remain Source-Bound

At a high level, the following must remain source-bound:

- source canon
- source-native standing
- source authority not explicitly transferable
- source origin legibility
- source lineage references where required

Release may permit crossing of a carried relation or artifact, but it does not relocate the source canon itself.

## 7. Lawful Ingress

Lawful ingress is destination-side admissibility of arrival.

Arrival does not imply native standing.  
Arrival does not imply native origin.  
Destination may receive as candidate, derivative, or bounded carried relation only where lawful.  
Ingress may refuse or non-admit without fabricating continuity.

Ingress therefore names what lawful arrival may mean after crossing, not what the destination may pretend has always belonged there.

## 8. What Ingress Must Not Do

Ingress must not allow:

- automatic upgrade of arrival into native standing
- silent authority inheritance
- erased source relation
- fabricated continuity
- untyped jurisdiction shift
- derivative surface silently promoted to canonical destination standing

## 9. Relation to Seam and Handoff

The seam governs crossing.

This file governs what may lawfully approach the seam and what lawful arrival may mean after crossing.

It does not replace `runtime/HANDOFF_SEQUENCE.md`. Internal handoff remains distinct from cross-boundary release and ingress. Handoff coordinates motion within a runtime body. Release and ingress bound what may leave one jurisdictional boundary and how arrival may be treated at another.

## 10. Anti-Collapse Rules

A conforming release and ingress posture must not allow:

- release without source legibility
- release that silently exports authority by convenience
- ingress that treats arrival as native standing by default
- ingress that rewrites source
- fabricated continuity to smooth transition
- silent jurisdiction inheritance

## 11. Closing Boundary Statement

This file defines only release and ingress as bounded continuity conditions around the seam.

It does not replace the constitutional core, continuity layer, seam note, registry, or runtime layers. Fuller cross-system jurisdiction stack remains outside this file.
