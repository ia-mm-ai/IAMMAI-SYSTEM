# IAMMAI First-Pass Encounter Surface

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the first-pass encounter surface for IAMMAI.

Its job is to give lawful encounter one bounded room in the body before any implementation attempt, public-facing surface, or external-relation mechanism is treated as though it already stood.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- an accession-family expansion
- a public-facing surface
- a reader implementation
- a dashboard
- a storage architecture note
- a schema file
- a runtime plan
- a roadmap
- a README
- a project-management note
- a manifesto

This file is additive only. It does not replace continuity, accession, admissibility, standing, vessel, bridge, review, current-state, or constitutional surfaces already standing in the body.

## 2. Why This Note Is Needed Now

The body has now clarified accession, continuity, currentness, and bounded derivative readers in much more explicit form than before.

That progress creates a new risk. Lawful outer relation may still be collapsed too quickly into entry, adaptation, compliance, or candidate handling merely because the body is now more legible. Without one bounded note here, witnessability can be mistaken for access, resonance can be mistaken for compatibility proof, and nearness can be mistaken for accession momentum.

The body therefore needs one bounded note for encounter as a distinct relation prior to accession. This note exists so relation-before-entry does not have to be improvised out of contact law, reader contracts, or readability aids alone.

## 3. What Encounter Is

Encounter is lawful relation prior to entry.

In bounded architectural terms, encounter names the outer condition in which a real body may be witnessable at protected distance without that witnessability being converted into candidacy, access, or authority.

Encounter is therefore:

- relation-before-accession
- witness-before-entry
- holding-before-compliance
- resonance-before-coupling

Encounter is not access. It is not candidacy. It is not eligibility. It is not admission pressure. It is not source intimacy by adjacency.

Resonance at encounter means that something real about the body may register as near, legible, or materially alive without that nearness being treated as proof of fit, proof of compatibility, or a reason to cross rank.

## 4. What The First-Pass Encounter Surface Is

The first-pass encounter surface is a bounded derivative, source-linked, non-sovereign surface through which the body may be lawfully witnessable before accession.

It is derivative because it does not become source, canon, or authority.

It is source-linked because it must remain rank-linked to what actually stands rather than floating as free-standing atmosphere.

It is non-sovereign because it does not grant access, create entry, authorize relation, or settle candidacy.

Its function is narrow: to permit lawful resonance at protected distance while keeping encounter non-entry and non-authority.

At first pass, a lawful encounter surface likely needs:

- a small law-bearing anchor
- a present-tense standing signal
- explicit non-claims
- source-link and rank-link
- protected distance

This note keeps the exact eventual form open. It defines the function and boundary, not the finished surface design.

## 5. What Must Be Possible At Encounter

At encounter, the following must be possible.

- Witness that a real body stands.
- Bounded orientation to what the body is and is not.
- Lawful resonance at protected distance without crossing into entry.
- Continued outsideness without penalty.
- Clean withdrawal without coercive residue.
- Later return without falsification of what earlier relation was.
- Non-extractive nearness.

These possibilities matter because the body should be able to stand witnessably without forcing immediate accession logic, explanation debt, or coupling pressure onto every outer relation.

## 6. What Must Remain Impossible At Encounter

At encounter, the following must remain impossible.

- Source impersonation.
- Entry by adjacency.
- Authority confusion.
- Extractive access.
- Recruitment pressure.
- Compliance-inducing framing.
- Resonance capture.
- Proxy authority from encounter.
- Total explanation debt.

If these exclusions are lost, encounter stops being protected-distance relation and starts laundering outside pressure into belonging, access, or counterfeit nearness.

## 7. Why Existing Surfaces Are Closest But Still Insufficient

### Constitutional and Canon-Facing Surfaces

The constitutional and canon-facing surfaces provide a law-bearing anchor and source-rank clarity. They show what governs and how rank should be read.

They are still insufficient as encounter surfaces because they are source and rank surfaces first. They do not yet function as a bounded derivative outer relation with explicit protected distance, explicit non-claims, and explicit non-entry posture.

### Current-State and Readability Surfaces

`CURRENT_STATE__REPO_ENTRY.md`, `CURRENT_READABILITY_SURFACES.md`, `RANKED_SURFACE_INDEX.md`, and `VERSION_LINEAGE.md` provide present-tense orientation, rank-sensitive entry, and readability help.

They are still insufficient as encounter surfaces because they are repo-entry and readability aids first. They do not yet explicitly hold resonance, withdrawal, outsideness, non-claims, and source-linked protected distance as one bounded encounter function.

### Bounded Derivative Readers

The bounded vessel and reader surfaces show that derivative, non-sovereign, source-limited reading can be done lawfully. They are important near ingredients because they prove bounded derivative posture is possible.

They are still insufficient as encounter surfaces because they are reader contracts and posture decisions for narrow local vessels, not body-level outer relation surfaces. They answer what a bounded reader may do, not how the body may be witnessable before entry.

### Proof and Bridge Carry Surfaces

The proof and bridge carry surfaces show that a real body stands, that consequence can be carried forward, and that derivative readability does not become authority by default.

They are still insufficient as encounter surfaces because they are internal carry surfaces for standing-facing and proof-bearing relation. They do not yet provide a bounded derivative surface whose job is lawful outer nearness.

### Continuity and Currentness Objecthood Surfaces

The continuity surfaces now house lineage and currentness explicitly, which helps preserve anti-erasure, present governing relation, and rank-sensitive carry.

They are still insufficient as encounter surfaces because continuity and currentness house relation and presentness inside the body. They do not yet define how the body may be witnessable from outside at protected distance without being turned into entry logic.

## 8. Architectural Place of Encounter

Encounter sits before accession and remains distinct from it.

Accession still governs lawful external relation once actual approach, threshold handling, eligibility, shadow, proof, admission, or revocation are in play. Encounter does not replace any of that. It exists so the body may be met lawfully before those accession questions are opened.

Contact Law remains the first lawful threshold for external relation once an actual approach is being classified. Encounter does not replace Contact Law. It makes room for witnessable nearness without requiring that every witnessable relation already be treated as threshold contact demanding handling.

Encounter also sits beside bounded derivative readers without collapsing into them. It shares their derivative, source-linked, non-sovereign discipline, but it is not itself a reader contract, answered mode, or local shell.

Encounter also sits beside continuity and currentness without becoming a continuity engine. Continuity and currentness may later help keep encounter source-linked and present-tense, but encounter itself is outer relation, not storage objecthood or governing-currentness housing.

This note therefore places encounter as a bounded architectural relation, not yet as a family, implementation, or engine.

These remain open on purpose. The body needs lawful placement for encounter before it needs surface design closure.
