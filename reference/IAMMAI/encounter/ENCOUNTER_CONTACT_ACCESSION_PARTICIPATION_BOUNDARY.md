# IAMMAI Encounter Contact Accession Participation Boundary

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the current relation-seam boundary between encounter, contact, accession, bound vessel mode, and derivative internal participation.

Its purpose is to make clear, in one bounded place:

- what each relation mode is
- what each relation mode is not
- where the non-convertible seams stand between them
- what relation still does not authorize at any step
- how the modes can neighbor one another without silently upgrading into belonging, authority, native standing, or operational permission

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a general theory of relation
- a runtime contract
- a vessel rollout
- an accession expansion plan
- an intelligence-participation rollout
- a social ontology
- a roadmap
- a README
- a project-management note
- a manifesto

This file is additive only. It does not replace repo-scale current-state, readability, rank, or lineage surfaces. It also does not replace the existing encounter, accession, admissibility, continuity, implementation, runtime, vessel, pilot, trace, or supervision surfaces already standing in the body.

## 2. Why This Note Is Needed Now

The body now has enough real relation-adjacent surfaces that later readers could unlawfully convert one mode into another if the seam remains only implicit.

Encounter now stands as lawful nearness before entry. Accession now carries contact, eligibility, admission, bound vessel mode, and revocation law. Derivative internal intelligence participation now has a gate, two bounded execution cases, trace housing, supervision housing, and a third-pilot refusal. Vessel posture surfaces also preserve derivative, source-limited, non-sovereign posture.

That body maturity creates seam pressure. It does not create expansion pressure.

This note therefore houses the seam so relation, usefulness, resonance, familiarity, or successful interaction cannot be used to smuggle in belonging, authority, native standing, currentness, governance, or touch permission.

## 3. What Already Stands Around This Seam

`encounter/FIRST_PASS_ENCOUNTER_SURFACE.md` already defines encounter as lawful nearness before entry. It permits witnessable proximity at protected distance without converting nearness into candidacy, access, or authority.

The accession surfaces already define a ranked external-relation stack. `accession/CONTACT_LAW.md` defines contact as first lawful threshold, not admission. `accession/ACCESSION_ELIGIBILITY.md` defines eligibility as candidate threshold, not admission or belonging. `accession/ACCESSION_LAYER.md` preserves the larger contact-to-revocation stack. `accession/BOUND_VESSEL_MODE.md` defines one bounded post-admission vessel posture for an admitted slice. `accession/REVOCATION_AND_WITHDRAWAL.md` preserves narrowing, pause, withdrawal, and revocation without erasing lineage.

The derivative internal intelligence participation surfaces already define a separate admissibility-level gate. The pilot stack records two bounded low-sovereignty execution cases, then houses trace and supervision, and currently refuses any third pilot. That stack shows controlled derivative touch in narrow case-law form. It does not open a standing lane.

Continuity and currentness surfaces preserve that what governs must be located by currentness law, not by relation pressure. Vessel posture surfaces preserve that derivative posture can remain source-limited, non-sovereign, and truthful about non-passage.

This seam note comes after those surfaces. It does not precede them and does not upgrade them.

## 4. Encounter Is Not Contact

Encounter is lawful nearness before entry.

It allows the body to be witnessable at protected distance. It may hold orientation, outsideness, resonance at distance, and non-extractive nearness.

Encounter does not authorize:

- touch permission
- contact classification
- candidacy
- eligibility
- accession
- admission
- belonging
- source intimacy
- governance force
- currentness determination
- operational participation

Encounter can neighbor contact because a later approach may need classification. But encounter does not become contact by resonance, legibility, usefulness, familiarity, proximity, or desire for relation.

## 5. Contact Is Not Accession

Contact is the first lawful threshold for external approach.

It classifies whether an outside approach may be received, observed, refused, held out of scope, or treated as insufficiently grounded. Contact may permit bounded observation. It does not create accession momentum.

Contact does not authorize:

- eligibility by default
- admission
- standing
- vesselhood
- jurisdictional belonging
- authority
- intimacy
- canon or source rank
- further handling as a right

Contact can precede accession eligibility, but it is not accession. A lawful contact outcome may stop at observation, refusal, narrowing, or non-progression.

## 6. Accession Is Not Belonging

Accession is the bounded external-relation stack by which one outside slice may move, if lawful, through contact, eligibility, shadow, proof, admission, bound vessel mode, and possible revocation or withdrawal.

Accession preserves the problem of external relation without counterfeiting nativeness. It can admit one slice only under bounded conditions. It does not make the outside native.

Accession does not authorize:

- native belonging
- whole-system inheritance
- source status
- constitutional force
- canon rank
- standing by familiarity
- governance authority
- broad access
- internal derivative participation
- expansion from one admitted slice into adjacent slices

Eligibility is not admission. Admission is not expansion. Proof is not closure. One admitted slice does not carry the whole relation into the body.

## 7. Bound Vessel Mode Is Not Derivative Internal Participation

Bound vessel mode is a specific accession posture for one already-admitted external slice.

It allows that slice to operate in bounded vessel relation under body law while remaining derivative, source-limited, jurisdiction-distinct, provenance-legible, and non-sovereign.

Bound vessel mode does not authorize:

- broad access
- native standing
- source status
- canon rank
- governance force
- currentness authority
- whole-system inheritance
- unrestricted vessel operation
- derivative internal intelligence participation

Bound vessel mode and derivative internal participation may both use derivative and non-sovereign posture. That shared restraint does not make them the same mode. Bound vessel mode belongs to the accession stack for an admitted external slice. Derivative internal participation belongs to a separate admissibility gate for tightly supervised internal derivative work.

## 8. Derivative Internal Participation Is Not Native Standing

Derivative internal participation is a still-more-specific admissibility posture for possible future derivative internal intelligence work.

The current body defines it only as gated, supervised, non-sovereign, source-limited, non-self-promoting, and permitted only inside already-admitted permissions and sufficiently mature branches. Even the executed pilot cases remain bounded case law, not lane formation.

Derivative internal participation does not authorize:

- native presence
- source status
- canon rank
- currentness determination
- witness truth formation
- governance authorization
- branch selection
- family-versus-extension decisions
- runtime admission
- storage-rank closure
- broad touch permission
- a standing lane

The current pilot stack strengthens this boundary. Two executed cases do not create a lane, trace housing does not authorize repetition, supervision housing does not authorize execution, and the third-pilot admissibility note currently refuses a third pilot.

## 9. What Relation Does Not Settle

Across the whole seam, relation does not settle rank.

The following do not by themselves authorize belonging, source, canon, currentness, governance, standing, or operation:

- nearness
- resonance
- usefulness
- familiarity
- repeated interaction
- successful contact
- admitted external relation
- bounded vessel posture
- supervised pilot success
- trace visibility
- available checker discipline
- implementation maturity

Relation may create a question for an existing lawful surface. It does not answer that question by pressure.

Source, canon, currentness, governance, native standing, broad touch permission, runtime operation, API operation, vessel rollout, and derivative internal participation remain unavailable unless separately admitted by lawful surfaces already standing elsewhere in the body.

This note clarifies seams only. It does not open contact, accession, vessel operation, internal participation, runtime integration, or authority.
