# IAMMAI Regulation In Play v0

## 1. Purpose

This file defines how regulation is already present, observable, and testable in the current IAMMAI body.

Its job is to identify where regulation is already happening in the current architecture, runtime, embodiment, continuity, and controlled-variation posture, what the body is already doing that counts as regulation, what that regulation is preserving, where it is still thin, and what it does and does not yet require in runtime or code terms.

It does not yet try to define:

- code
- a control-system derivation
- a behavioral manual
- a replacement for runtime or governance
- a full formal model of regulation
- a new artifact family

It is a bounded architectural translation document.

## 2. First Principle

Regulation is already partially present in the current body.

Regulation must be identified through lawful function, not metaphor.  
Regulation remains subordinate to constitutional invariants.  
Regulation in play is not yet full formalization of regulation.

In IAMMAI terms, the question is not whether the repo has a separate regulation engine. The question is whether the living body already exhibits lawful motion-preserving behavior that keeps distinction intact under pressure, repetition, and visible non-passage. It does.

## 3. Why `Regulation In Play` Is Needed

Regulation should not remain only theory now that the body has moved.

The current body already exhibits regulation-relevant behavior. It already keeps typed role boundaries under motion, preserves visible non-passage instead of smoothing it away, holds canonical and derivative surfaces apart, and constrains continuity emission to preserved lawful runs rather than mere narrative sequence.

It is useful to identify present regulation before introducing deeper motion formalization because the repo already contains a living bounded body. Naming where regulation is already active helps prevent later dynamics work from pretending regulation begins only when a more elaborate theory or mechanism is introduced.

## 4. Where Regulation Is Already Present

Regulation is already visible in the current body at the following points:

- visible non-passage rather than false continuity across runtime and embodiment judgment surfaces
- role separation under runtime motion across Interface Gateway, Validator, Witness Emitter, Governor, State Engine, and Registry / Store
- bounded validation before governance where the selected move requires lawful force
- HOLD, denial, failure, blocked movement, and validation non-passage remaining distinct rather than collapsing into one generic interruption
- canonical versus derivative separation, so display or summary does not silently replace canonical truth
- append-oriented preservation and anti-overwrite posture in registry and continuity handling
- controlled variation remaining within the same slice class rather than widening into new matter categories, broader access, or richer hidden ontology
- continuity emitted only for successful lawful runs whose canonical artifacts were actually preserved

These are not merely adjacent to regulation. They are current lawful functions by which the body already preserves distinction while moving.

## 5. What Current Regulation Is Preserving

Current regulation is already helping preserve:

- sequence integrity
- distinction between truth and decision
- distinction between success, failure, hold, denial, and non-passage
- bounded continuity
- anti-overwrite posture
- lawful body coherence under repeated runs

More concretely, current regulation helps keep threshold from collapsing into truth, governance from collapsing into validation, witness from collapsing into authority, derivative surfaces from collapsing into canon, and repeated execution from collapsing into broadened embodiment by convenience.

## 6. Where Regulation Is Still Thin

The current body still has only minimal or emerging regulation in several places:

- higher-pressure handling across more repeated runs
- richer witness coverage beyond the current thin witness family
- fuller threshold-state handling beyond the first proof seam
- deeper continuity chaining across multiple successful turns
- a more mature relation between lawful variation and drift

This thinness does not mean regulation is absent. It means the present body already regulates in bounded form, but it does so with a deliberately narrow slice and without yet carrying the full motion burden that later embodiments may introduce.

## 7. Regulation vs Existing Layers

Current runtime already expresses regulation wherever typed sequence, handoff boundaries, visible non-passage, and anti-collapse distinctions are preserved under live motion.

Current embodiment already expresses regulation wherever the first slice remains bounded, controlled variation stays inside the same slice class, and success or failure is judged by canonical integrity rather than by presentation quality.

Regulation is not identical to runtime flow documents because runtime flow describes how motion proceeds, while regulation asks what in that motion is already preserving lawful distinction under pressure.

Regulation is not identical to governance or validation because governance applies lawful force and validation performs bounded checking. Regulation is the broader motion-preserving relation within which those roles already participate without absorbing one another.

Continuity and regulation relate without collapsing into one another. Continuity preserves lawful relation across turns. Regulation is visible where that continuity is emitted only under bounded preserved conditions and is prevented from becoming false narrative continuity.

## 8. What `In Play` Does Not Mean

This file does not claim:

- full regulation theory is finished
- regulation already has code-level completeness
- regulation has become a sovereign layer
- the body is already fully mature in motion

It claims only that regulation is already partially active in the present body and can already be identified through the functions that preserve lawful distinction under live bounded execution.

## 9. Next Pressure Points

The next places where regulation will likely matter most are:

- controlled variation across more matters

These are the points where repeated motion will place greater pressure on the body’s ability to preserve distinction without widening, smoothing, or overwriting.

## 10. Summary

This document defines `regulation in play` for IAMMAI as the already-operative bounded regulation visible in the current living body wherever runtime, embodiment, controlled variation, and continuity preserve lawful distinction under repeated motion and visible non-passage.

Its purpose is to show that regulation is not merely a future theory layer. It is already partially present in the current body, even though its formalization remains intentionally incomplete and bounded.

**One-Line Definition:** `REGULATION_IN_PLAY_v0.md` defines the first bounded translation of regulation into the current IAMMAI body as the already-observable preservation of lawful distinction, visible non-passage, bounded continuity, and anti-overwrite coherence across live repeated motion without collapsing regulation into runtime, governance, or validation.
