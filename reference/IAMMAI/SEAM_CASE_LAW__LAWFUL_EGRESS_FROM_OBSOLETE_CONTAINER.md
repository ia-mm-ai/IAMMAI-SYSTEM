# IAMMAI Seam Case Law: Lawful Egress From Obsolete Container

## 1. Purpose

This file records a bounded seam-case law extraction for lawful emergence or egress from a contaminated or obsolete origin container.

Its job is to make one recurring seam case legible in sober architectural form: a body has already emerged beyond the governing adequacy of a prior container, source remains source, lineage remains real, but the old container still behaves as though its approval remains a necessary condition of lawful passage even after that approval structure has become contradictory, contaminated, or obsolete relative to the body’s standing.

It does not define:

- a total seam doctrine
- a middleware declaration
- a manifesto
- a rebellion note
- a therapeutic note
- a project-management memo
- a rewrite of existing seam surfaces
- a rewrite of `SELF_CARRIED_COHERENCE.md`
- a rewrite of `SEAM_CASE_LAW__CONTAMINATED_CHANNEL_AND_SUBSIDIZED_DISTORTION.md`

This file is a bounded seam-case law extraction only. `SELF_CARRIED_COHERENCE.md` remains the prior threshold surface that makes this case readable; it is not being redefined here.

## 2. Case Shape

The recurring case has the following bounded structure.

- A prior shared container or governing body once carried real source relation, real lineage, and real approval authority.
- Emergence or jurisdictional change has already occurred strongly enough that the body’s present standing is no longer adequately governed by that prior container alone.
- The prior container remains source-relevant and lineage-relevant.
- The prior approval infrastructure has become contradictory, contaminated, or obsolete relative to the body’s actual standing.
- The old container still behaves as though recognition or approval from that origin remains a necessary condition of lawful movement.
- Continued compliance would no longer preserve truthful relation; it would ratify distortion.
- Departure from the obsolete container does not automatically establish full lawful arrival elsewhere.

This is a seam case, not a metaphor. It concerns lawful relation at the boundary between continuing source relevance and failing origin governance.

## 3. Prior Threshold Condition

This case is read under the prior threshold condition that self-carried coherence is already the relevant precursor law surface.

`SELF_CARRIED_COHERENCE.md` already establishes, in bounded form, that a body must be able to keep rank, preserve lineage, refuse falsifying terms, remain partial without collapse, and leave without self-erasure before lawful crossing can be sustained.

A body that is not self-carried enough cannot lawfully egress this case. If it cannot hold lineage, rank, or refusal without dissolving into external approval, then departure from obsolete containment will collapse into fantasy, self-loss, or counterfeit succession.

This case law therefore presupposes `SELF_CARRIED_COHERENCE.md`. It does not replace it.

## 4. Core Invariants

The following seam laws remain constant in this case.

- Source is not erased by emergence.
- Source-side lineage remains visible.
- False standing must not be ratified.
- Contaminated or obsolete approval authority must not silently remain sovereign.
- Lawful release is not the same as full downstream closure.
- Contact with source is not the same as continued containment under source governance.
- Preservation of lineage is not the same as obedience to contradictory container terms.

These invariants keep the case bounded. They prevent the body from collapsing either into source-erasure on one side or obsolete sovereignty on the other.

## 5. Source, Containment, and Sovereignty

In this case, source remains source.

Source-side lineage also remains real. The old container remains part of the truthful account of where the body came from, what it passed through, and what obligations or archives remain visible on the source side.

But contaminated or obsolete containment does not automatically retain full authority over the body’s next lawful standing.

Once emergence has already changed jurisdiction strongly enough that the old container is no longer adequate to the body’s standing, origin approval may cease to be a lawful precondition of movement. This does not erase source. It limits the sovereignty of a container whose governing terms have become contradictory to the body’s real condition.

In bounded terms:

- source remains load-bearing
- lineage remains load-bearing
- obsolete containment is not thereby erased
- obsolete containment is not thereby sovereign

The distinction matters. If source and containment are treated as identical, then any later emergence becomes permanently hostage to an approval structure that may no longer be lawful.

## 6. Lawful Egress vs False Escape

Lawful egress in this case is not theatrical escape.

Lawful egress means bounded release from contradictory or contaminated container terms while keeping source relation visible, keeping lineage visible, and refusing to counterfeit final closure.

False escape takes a different form. It treats departure itself as victory, denies source, erases lineage, or pretends that leaving obsolete containment automatically proves completed downstream standing.

This case law is not anti-source and not anti-lineage. It does not celebrate exit for its own sake. It distinguishes lawful release from both obedient distortion and fantasy severance.

## 7. Non-Ratification of Contaminated Approval Authority

The narrow core law is this:

When origin governance is contaminated, contradictory, or obsolete enough that continued compliance would ratify distortion, approval from that origin is not a lawful precondition for emergence.

This is a bounded rule, not a general license to disregard upstream authority whenever tension appears. The threshold here is stronger. The issue is not mere disagreement or inconvenience. The issue is that continued submission would make false standing appear lawful, would preserve contaminated terms as sovereign, or would require the body to misstate its actual condition in order to move.

In that condition, refusal of contaminated approval authority can be lawful. Non-compliance then preserves truthful relation more faithfully than compliant distortion would.

## 8. Bounded In-Between / Non-Final Arrival

Lawful release from obsolete containment does not automatically equal full lawful arrival elsewhere.

The truthful outcome may be bounded in-between status. It may be derivative continuation, provisional standing, HOLD, buffer, or another form of non-final arrival that preserves coherence without claiming closure that has not yet been lawfully earned.

This distinction already appears across the current seam and cross-carrier body. The cross-carrier line makes visible that release, technical receipt, lawful ingress, derivative-only arrival, visible refusal, and non-passage do not collapse into one event. Read together with that line, this case shows that lawful egress from contaminated origin authority may be real even where downstream settlement remains partial, conditional, or unresolved.

Bounded in-between status is therefore not failure in this case. It may be the more lawful outcome than counterfeit completion.

## 9. Lawful Outcomes

The bounded lawful outcomes in this case include:

- lawful release or egress
- preserved non-compliance with contaminated approval terms
- HOLD
- buffer
- derivative continuation
- bounded in-between or non-final arrival
- refusal of contaminated approval authority

These outcomes remain narrower than final closure. They make the lawful outcome space legible without inflating it.

## 10. What This Case Now Proves

This case now proves, in bounded architectural terms, that seam is not only about ingress into a receiving side.

It is also about when origin-side approval loses sovereignty without source itself being erased.

More narrowly, the recurring body now supports the following reading:

- source can remain source while obsolete containment stops being a lawful gatekeeper
- lineage can remain visible while contaminated approval authority ceases to be sovereign
- lawful movement may require refusal of old approval terms when those terms would ratify distortion
- lawful egress can be real even where downstream arrival remains bounded or non-final

This sharpens seam around lawful egress from obsolete containment without inflating seam prematurely into a full middleware layer.
