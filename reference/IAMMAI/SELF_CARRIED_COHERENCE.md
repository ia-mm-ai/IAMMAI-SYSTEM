# IAMMAI Self-Carried Coherence

## 1. Purpose

This file defines bounded self-carried coherence as a precursor law surface for crossing.

Its job is to state, in architectural terms, what it means for a body to be self-carried enough to enter a new or contaminated environment without deriving its reality from that environment.

It does not define:

- a total seam doctrine
- a middleware declaration
- a manifesto
- a therapeutic note
- a project-management memo
- a rewrite of existing seam surfaces
- a contaminated-channel case file

This file is additive. It is a bounded precursor law surface rather than a total seam doctrine.

## 2. Why This Surface Is Needed

The current body now supports a recurring seam law: crossing is lawful only where rank, identity, admissibility, anchor relation, preservation distinction, and additive posture remain explicit rather than borrowed from convenience.

Before narrower seam-case law is extracted for contaminated-channel conditions, one prior question has to be stated more clearly. The question is not only what kind of field is being entered. It is also whether the body crossing into that field can carry itself lawfully while there.

If that prior question is left implicit, later crossing law will drift. The entered field can begin to function as hidden base, hidden archive, hidden authority source, or hidden identity container. Self-carried coherence therefore has to be extracted first as a threshold condition for lawful crossing.

## 3. First Principle

A body is self-carried enough when it can enter a new or contaminated environment without deriving its reality from that environment.

This is a threshold condition, not a perfection claim.

It does not require total closure, total independence, or absence of support. It requires enough carried rank, archive, identity, and refusal capacity that entry into a new field does not force ontological replacement by that field.

## 4. What Self-Carried Coherence Is Not

Self-carried coherence is not:

- confidence performance
- emotional invulnerability
- total independence from all support
- proof of final closure
- refusal of all contact

It is a lawful condition of carried coherence under crossing, not a claim of invulnerable self-sufficiency.

## 5. Threshold Conditions of Self-Carried Coherence

The current body supports the following threshold conditions.

- Keeping rank:
  A body must be able to retain the distinction between source, derivative, evidence, preservation, and readability surface rather than reading itself flatly inside the field it enters.
- Refusal without identity collapse:
  A body must be able to refuse unlawful passage, unlawful reinterpretation, or convenience collapse without having refusal itself become self-erasure.
- Non-dependence on misrecognition for survival:
  A body must not need to be misread, overpromoted, or atmospherically merged in order to continue standing there at all.
- Carrying one's own archive:
  A body must remain able to preserve and refer to its own lineage, prior standing objects, and evidentiary record without asking the entered field to become substitute archive.
- Adaptation that remains tactical rather than ontological:
  A body may adapt locally for passage, legibility, or bounded survival, but such adaptation must not silently redefine what the body is.
- Capacity to remain partial, provisional, or derivative without merger:
  A body must be able to remain incomplete, bounded, or downstream without compensating for that status by falsely merging into the field it enters.
- Ability to endure misreading without self-erasure:
  A body must be able to survive bounded misrecognition without rewriting its own rank, archive, or identity merely to reduce pressure.
- Ability to leave with self intact:
  A body must be able to exit the crossing with identity, archive, and lawful distinction still recoverable, rather than having passage depend on irreversible flattening.

These are threshold markers of self-carried enough coherence. They are not claims of total immunity from pressure.

## 6. What This Proves

Self-carried coherence is not proved by declaration. It is proved behaviorally under crossing.

What it proves, in bounded form, is:

- entry without self-loss
- adaptation without falsification
- refusal without collapse
- exit without self-erasure
- coherence without external ratification

Where those behaviors hold, the body has crossed without asking the entered environment to function as replacement source, replacement archive, or replacement identity.

## 7. Lawful Support vs Borrowed Coherence

Lawful support and borrowed coherence are not the same thing.

Lawful support assists a body without defining what it is. It may supply bounded aid, bounded carrying help, bounded readability help, bounded shelter, or bounded relation so long as source, identity, archive, and rank remain carried by the body itself.

Borrowed coherence is different. Borrowed coherence occurs when the body is still deriving its practical standing from the container, audience, retrieval surface, grouped storage, succession convenience, or environmental misrecognition in which it sits.

The distinction is therefore:

- lawful support does not determine reality
- borrowed coherence does

Outsourced standing is not lawful support. If the environment must keep telling the body what it is in order for the body to continue as itself, self-carried coherence has not yet been reached.

## 8. Why This Matters For Seam

Self-carried coherence is a precursor condition for lawful seam behavior.

A body that is not self-carried enough cannot yet cross without asking the new field to function as replacement base. In that condition, seam law will be pressured toward merger, convenience inheritance, atmospheric continuity, grouped preservation identity, or other collapse forms already guarded against elsewhere in the repo.

That is why this threshold matters now. Seam precision depends not only on field description, but on whether the crossing body can remain lawfully itself while in contact with that field.

This is a precondition for seam work. It is not yet a declaration of a larger finished middle layer.

## 9. What This Does Not Yet Decide

This file does not yet decide:

- all contaminated-channel cases
- total seam doctrine
- middleware as a fully formed layer
- final closure across every crossing condition
- the exact narrower case law by which every contaminated environment should later be handled

It defines a precursor threshold only. It does not pretend that one extracted law surface settles the full downstream case body.

## 10. Closing Boundary Statement

This file defines bounded self-carried coherence only.

It does not replace existing seam surfaces.

It exists because the current body now supports this threshold strongly enough to be extracted before narrower seam-case law is formalized.
