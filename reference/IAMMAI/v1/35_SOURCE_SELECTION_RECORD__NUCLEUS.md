# IAMMAI v1 Source Selection Record: Nucleus

## 1. Bounded Matter

The bounded matter is:

**which single source may be selected as the first operating source for later IAMMAI derivation without deriving its rank from anything that follows it.**

This matter does not ask which later surface is most complete, most readable, most recent, or most authoritative within its own downstream rank.

Selected source:

`nucleus.yaml`

Source class:

**first-source constitutional firmware**

Preserved relation:

**root configuration from which later IAMMAI surfaces may read, reference, embody, and conform without acquiring write authority over the selected source.**

## 3. Basis for Selection

`nucleus.yaml` is selected because it alone declares and carries, in one bounded source:

- no predecessor
- the preparation and standing-state regimes
- the ordered nine-phase execution vector
- orthogonal containment through `HOLD`
- the closed resolution family `FINALIZE / INVALIDATE / EVOLVE`
- transition, lineage, authority, and contribution-role integrity constraints
- a root boundary that permits downstream reference and conformance while denying retroactive rewrite

The source is selected for the relation it preserves, not because of filename position, retrieval convenience, recency, or repository adjacency.

## 6. What Selection Does Not Grant

This selection does not itself create:

- standing for every downstream surface
- automatic conformance
- authority by proximity
- inheritance by implication
- immunity from validation
- final closure

It selects one source for one relation only.
