# IAMMAI v1 Seam Declaration

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the lawful repo-scale seam between the frozen root `v0` source-side body and the new `v1/` admissible derivation line.

Its job is to declare the boundary between source-side preservation and later derivation so the opening of `v1/` does not imply silent inheritance, silent supersession, or retroactive rewrite.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a `v1` README
- an authority or inheritance map
- an artifact-boundary decision
- a runtime contract
- a schema
- a full cross-system jurisdiction stack

It is a bounded seam declaration.

## 2. First Principle

The seam is subordinate to constitutional invariant.

Opening `v1/` does not erase or rewrite `v0`.  
`v1/` does not gain standing by existence alone.

In IAMMAI terms, folder creation is not lawful derivation by itself. The seam exists so root remains what it is, and `v1/` remains only the newly opened line beside it until later articulation gives that line more definite standing.

## 3. Why This Seam Must Be Declared

Root remains historically real.

Later derivation must not pretend it always existed. Without a seam declaration, inheritance and supersession can become atmospheric and fraudulent. A cleaner downstream line can begin to appear as though it silently owns the root body, even where no explicit re-derivation, retention, or supersession has yet been stated.

## 4. Source-Side Body

Root remains the frozen `v0` source-side body.

Source remains source. Root is preserved with proof, drift, experiments, and history intact. Nothing in the opening of `v1/` cleans, reclassifies, or rewrites that source-side body by implication.

## 5. Receiving-Side Line

`v1/` is the new admissible derivation line.

It stands only through explicit articulation, explicit re-derivation, and fresh proof. It does not silently absorb authority from root merely because it exists beside it.

## 6. What May Cross

At a high level, the following may cross where later explicitly articulated:

- inherited constitutional or body law where explicitly retained
- source-bound evidence and lineage references
- surfaces later chosen for re-derivation

## 7. What Must Not Cross

At a high level, the following must not cross:

- silent authority inheritance
- silent semantic promotion
- retroactive cleanup of root
- fake continuity produced by editorial tidying
- assumption that all root surfaces automatically remain authoritative in `v1`

## 8. Seam Law

The governing seam rules are:

- no inheritance by implication
- no supersession by adjacency
- no standing by folder creation alone
- no source erasure by cleaner downstream articulation

Crossing from `v0` to `v1` must therefore be explicit, not atmospheric.

## 9. Closing Boundary Statement

This file declares the seam only.

Later files may articulate inheritance and artifact law. This declaration exists so the repo-scale transition from root `v0` to `v1/` is lawful and non-fraudulent.
