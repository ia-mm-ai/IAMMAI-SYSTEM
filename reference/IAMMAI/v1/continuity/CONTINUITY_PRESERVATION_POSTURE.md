# IAMMAI v1 Continuity Preservation Posture

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded v1 continuity-specific preservation posture.

Its job is to state how continuity is to be preserved under the current v1 line without redefining continuity by grouped storage convenience, without silently re-importing v0 run-centric posture, and without collapsing continuity body, execution relation, and preservation into one undifferentiated preserved object.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a seam declaration
- an authority or inheritance map
- an artifact-boundary decision
- a continuity schema
- a continuity emitter implementation patch
- a continuity validator code note
- a runtime flow map
- a storage topology design
- a project-management roadmap
- a generic README

It is a bounded continuity preservation posture note.

## 2. Why This File Is Needed Now

Continuity now has overview, emission contract, body schema, conformance posture, runtime flow, emission plan, and operator runbook.

Continuity preservation posture must therefore now be stated before continuity emitter or preservation code chooses it by convenience. The current continuity law already says enough to define what must remain distinct when the first continuity proof slice is preserved.

Current root continuity preservation behavior remains lineage evidence, not automatic v1 law. The root line shows a historically real preservation path shaped by run-centric continuity handling. That evidence matters. It does not settle the v1 continuity preservation posture.

## 3. First Principle

Preservation preserves what already stands.

Preservation does not create, redefine, or silently merge continuity law.  
Continuity body remains continuity body.  
Execution relation remains execution relation.  
Preservation remains preservation.

In v1 terms, preservation must preserve the result of earlier lawful continuity stages without rewriting those stages into one combined preserved object that hides what stood as continuity body, what traveled only as explicit execution relation, and what belonged only to preservation context.

## 4. Preservation Scope of the First Continuity Proof Slice

The first continuity preservation slice is bounded to the continuity turn as the in-scope continuity artifact.

This slice is intentionally small and decisive. It is continuity-specific rather than a reopening of generic preservation posture for the whole body.

Its purpose is to preserve the first bounded continuity proof slice under current v1 continuity law without allowing continuity-specific preservation convenience to define continuity itself.

## 5. Continuity Preservation Layers

The first v1 continuity proof slice distinguishes the following continuity preservation layers:

- continuity body preservation
- optional explicit execution-relation preservation
- preservation or conformance reading material where relevant

Continuity body preservation preserves the continuity turn as continuity body.

Optional explicit execution-relation preservation preserves the lawful case in which continuity body traveled together with explicit execution relation while remaining separable from it.

Preservation or conformance reading material preserves what is needed so later reading can still tell whether continuity body, execution relation, and preservation each stood or failed at their own layer.

These layers may be preserved in relation. They must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated preserved continuity thing.

## 6. Placement Posture

V1 continuity preservation must keep continuity body, execution relation, and preservation or conformance results separately readable.

Preservation may group related continuity outputs, but it must not collapse them into one undifferentiated preserved object. Preservation should support recovery of what stood at each earlier continuity stage:

- what stood as continuity body
- what, if anything, traveled as explicit execution relation
- what was preserved as continuity preservation context
- what conformance posture or fixture posture applied where relevant

This posture does not require final filename rules or final storage topology here. It requires only that the preserved continuity result remain layer-readable rather than convenience-flattened.

## 7. Reading and Retrieval Posture

Preserved continuity material must remain readable by layer.

Retrieval must not force the reader to confuse continuity body with execution relation or preservation context. Preservation success does not override earlier continuity body or execution-relation failure.

Refusal-path continuity material may remain preserved lawfully without being misread as accidental incoherence. If refusal, non-conformance, or bounded failure was the lawful result at a specific continuity stage, retrieval must preserve that reading rather than laundering it into generic preserved success.

## 8. Anchor and Predecessor Preservation

Preserved continuity must retain the difference between canonical artifact anchoring and predecessor succession.

Predecessor succession may support append-oriented continuity by making lawful succession explicit across continuity turns.

Predecessor succession must not silently replace canonical artifact anchoring. Continuity preservation must remain subordinate to preserved canonical reality rather than to generic event history, generic runtime logging, or pure sequence appearance.

Preservation reading must therefore keep visible:

- anchor relation to preserved canonical artifacts
- predecessor succession where lawfully present

These relations may coexist. They must not be flattened into one atmospheric preserved continuity signal.

## 9. Current-v0 / Desired-v1 Distinction

Current root continuity preservation and output posture remains historically real v0 lineage.

The v0 continuity line was run-centric and preservation-convenience-shaped. That root posture remains important as source-side evidence of how continuity was actually preserved above preserved canonical reality.

This v1 continuity preservation posture is the target posture that later emitter work must serve. This is not blame language. It is the explicit statement that v1 continuity preservation is being re-derived rather than silently inherited from the root line’s convenience path.

## 10. Anti-Collapse Rules

A conforming v1 continuity preservation posture must not allow:

- preservation-first definition of continuity
- combined preserved object that destroys recoverability of continuity body versus execution relation
- preservation context pretending to be continuity body
- predecessor-only preserved reading that severs continuity from canonical artifact anchoring
- preserved refusal-path continuity material misread as accidental drift
- reuse of v0 run-centric preservation convenience by default

It must also not allow preserved grouping to erase which earlier continuity stage or layer actually stood, failed, refused, or remained boundedly out of passage.
