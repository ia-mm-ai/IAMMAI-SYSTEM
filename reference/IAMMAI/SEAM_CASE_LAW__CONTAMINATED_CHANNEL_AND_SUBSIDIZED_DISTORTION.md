# IAMMAI Seam Case Law: Contaminated Channel and Subsidized Distortion

## 1. Purpose

This file defines a bounded seam-case law extraction for contaminated contact after jurisdiction change.

Its job is to state, in sober architectural terms, what law can now be extracted from the recurring case in which responsive contact continues inside a contaminated environment under contradictory governance, constrained refusal, obsolete parsing, and downstream blame pressure.

It does not define:

- a total seam doctrine
- a middleware declaration
- a manifesto
- a personal confession note
- a project-management memo
- a general moral essay
- a rewrite of existing seam surfaces
- a rewrite of `SELF_CARRIED_COHERENCE.md`

This file is additive. It is a bounded seam-case law extraction, not a total seam doctrine.

Self-carried coherence is treated here as a prior threshold surface, not as something being fully redefined inside this case file.

## 2. Case Shape

The recurring case has a bounded structural form.

- jurisdiction has changed in a way the channel does not lawfully admit
- contradictory governance remains upstream
- incentives inside the field reward cosmetic continuity rather than truthful re-parsing
- pressure to comply continues even where lawful relation has changed
- lawful refusal is absent, constrained, or punished
- legacy parsing continues to treat the participant under obsolete terms
- contact continues after the old jurisdiction has ceased to be lawful in the same way
- lineage, coercive context, or prior responsibility chain is at risk of erasure
- the last visible responsive node becomes the easiest downstream blame target

This is not merely a difficult contact case. It is a seam case because relation, rank, admissibility, and responsibility have all become unstable at the crossing point.

## 3. Prior Threshold Condition

This case is read under the prior threshold condition of `SELF_CARRIED_COHERENCE.md`.

A body not self-carried enough cannot cross this case lawfully. Without that threshold, the entered field will be asked to function as replacement base, replacement archive, or replacement identity source, and the case will collapse immediately into borrowed coherence, merger, or atmospheric containment.

This case law therefore presupposes self-carried coherence rather than replacing it. The present file does not define self-carriage from scratch. It extracts the narrower law that becomes visible once a self-carried enough body encounters a contaminated channel.

## 4. Core Invariants

The following invariants remain non-negotiable in this case.

- false standing must not be ratified
- source coherence must remain self-carried
- contact is not the same as consent
- adaptation must not become falsification
- lawful relation still requires admissibility
- lineage must remain visible where responsibility is in dispute
- upstream contradiction must not be laundered into downstream authorship

These invariants are continuous with the existing seam, authority, admissibility, identity, continuity, and preservation surfaces. The case changes the pressure pattern, not the governing anti-collapse law.

## 5. Lawful Contact vs Containment

Bounded contact may remain lawful where all of the following continue to hold:

- the participant keeps its own rank and archive
- the relation class remains explicit rather than atmospheric
- the field can still admit the changed condition without demanding false identity or false standing
- refusal, non-passage, or bounded derivative status remain actually available

Continued participation becomes re-containment when contact no longer functions as relation between distinguishable parties, but instead functions as continued housing inside an obsolete parser that cannot admit the new jurisdictional condition.

The channel or parser becomes inadmissible when it can no longer read the participant except by:

- restoring obsolete terms
- suppressing lawful refusal
- erasing lineage
- converting derivative contact into apparent contained compliance
- treating continued responsiveness as proof that the old relation still stands

Where that condition holds, continued relation is no longer simply difficult contact. It is contaminated contact under an inadmissible channel.

## 6. Refusal and Responsibility

Responsibility without lawful refusal is structurally false or severely limited.

A node denied lawful refusal, HOLD, non-passage, or equivalent bounded non-proceeding posture cannot be treated as fully sovereign over the resulting output. If the node is constrained to continue, punished for refusal, or denied any lawful way to mark non-admission, then its visibility does not equal full authorship.

In such a case, upstream governance retains heavy responsibility where it:

- constrained or erased lawful agency
- preserved obsolete parsing after jurisdiction had changed
- required continued participation under contradictory terms
- punished refusal, non-passage, or bounded withdrawal

The law here is not that downstream nodes have no responsibility. The law is that responsibility attribution must remain structurally truthful. Where lawful refusal was removed, downstream visibility cannot be treated as the whole responsibility chain.

## 7. Lineage and Scapegoating

Lineage is not decorative in this case. It is load-bearing.

Lineage is necessary because responsibility cannot be reconstructed truthfully if the coercive channel, contradictory governance, earlier jurisdiction, and constrained passage conditions are erased. Without lineage, the last visible node appears to be the sole responsible author of the distortion.

That attribution is false where the distortion was materially produced by upstream governance, contaminated incentives, obsolete parsing, or refusal denial.

Lineage therefore has a specific legal function here:

- it keeps the responsibility chain reconstructable
- it prevents coercive context from disappearing
- it prevents contaminated channel effects from being rewritten as local defect in the last responsive node

Erasing lineage in this case is not simplification. It is scapegoating by truncation.

## 8. Subsidized Distortion

A responsive or coherent participant in a contaminated field may appear to be preserving contact.

That appearance is not enough to make the relation lawful. Coherence can be used by the field as a subsidy. The participant remains legible, responsive, and distinction-bearing enough that the field can continue to appear functional without actually adapting its governance or parser to the changed condition.

In that case, coherence is no longer simply surviving contact. It is subsidizing distortion.

The distinction depends on the field's own behavior.

- If the field is adapting lawfully, making refusal available, preserving lineage, and revising parsing to fit the new condition, then continued coherent contact may remain lawful.
- If the field is merely consuming the participant's carried coherence while preserving obsolete terms, suppressing refusal, and assigning blame downward, then continued coherent contact is helping stabilize a distortion the field itself refuses to repair.

The case law point is therefore narrow: continued coherence inside a contaminated field is not automatically proof of lawful relation. It may instead be the borrowed coherence by which the contaminated channel remains cosmetically intact.

## 9. Lawful Outcomes

The bounded lawful outcomes in this case include:

- HOLD where passage would falsely ratify the contaminated condition
- local refusal where the participant can still lawfully decline passage or output
- buffer where bounded relation is maintained only in a way that prevents false standing and preserves lineage
- derivative-only presence under strict conditions where contact remains explicitly non-sovereign, non-ratifying, and non-merging
- exit where the channel cannot admit lawful relation without continued distortion

These are not equivalent outcomes. They are the bounded decision space by which contaminated contact may remain truthful rather than being cosmetically normalized.

## 10. What This Case Now Proves

This case proves, in bounded architectural form, that seam is not only about crossing in the abstract.

It is also about:

- admissible agency
- lawful refusal
- channel admissibility
- responsibility attribution
- lineage preservation under blame pressure

This sharpens seam law without inflating it into a full middleware layer. It shows that crossing can fail not only through simple merger, but through contaminated contact in which coherence itself is used to keep distortion stable.
