# IAMMAI Contribution Role Overview

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the first implementation-facing overview for contribution role inside the IAMMAI body.

Its job is to give contribution role one bounded room in the implementation layer so later schema and mechanism work do not have to proceed from ambient memory, generic authorship language, or flattened record planning.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- a runtime migration plan
- a schema
- a full contribution-role family system
- an identity system
- a governance engine
- a roadmap
- a README
- a project-management note
- a manifesto

This file is additive only. It does not replace implementation, runtime, continuity, admissibility, accession, standing, vessel, bridge, review, or current-state surfaces already standing in the body.

## 2. Why This Note Is Needed Now

The implementation layer already houses several record families explicitly. State, transition, witness, validation, and governance each already have their own bounded implementation-facing overview.

Contribution role does not yet have that same dedicated room, even though the visible body already treats contribution as a distinct preserved concern. `implementation/CANONICAL_RECORDS_OVERVIEW.md` names contribution role records as a core family. `runtime/REGISTRY_CONTRACT.md` already permits preservation of contribution records where relevant. `protocol/canon.json` already preserves contribution as a distinct anti-collapse concern with named role families and a reference object.

That means the next problem is not whether contribution role exists. The problem is that it remains comparatively underhoused in implementation-facing form. The body needs one bounded note here before later schema or mechanism work could be lawful without flattening contribution into something weaker.

## 3. What Contribution Role Is

In implementation-facing terms, contribution role preserves how a bounded contribution stands in relation to the body.

It is about semantic participation in material, not about who someone is metaphysically. It preserves role relation rather than personal essence.

At first pass, contribution role is concerned with bounded role relation such as:

- ORIGIN
- ELICITATION
- FORMALIZATION
- AUTHORIZATION
- RENDERING
- TRANSMISSION

These roles already stand in `protocol/canon.json`. They matter because the body distinguishes materially different ways something may enter, be shaped, be authorized, be rendered, or be transmitted without collapsing those differences into one authorship label.

In bounded implementation terms, a contribution role record is therefore about:

- what role type is being preserved
- what matter that role relates to
- what actor, artifact, or related governance action the role is linked to where relevant

This is first-pass objecthood and placement only. It is not yet final system design.

## 4. Why Generic Authorship Is Too Weak

`author` alone is too weak for this body because it collapses materially different semantic roles into one surface label.

If origin, formalization, authorization, rendering, and transmission are all stored as authorship, later readers cannot tell whether someone created content, elicited it, formalized it, authorized its use, rendered it into a surface, or merely transmitted it. That collapse would distort provenance, blur lawful force, and simplify co-produced material into a false singular source.

Generic `actor` language is also too weak by itself. An actor reference may be relevant, but actor alone does not preserve how that actor stood in relation to the material. Contribution role therefore needs more than identity mention and more than authorship shorthand.

## 5. Distinction From Neighboring Record Families

### 5.1 Governance Action

Governance action preserves attributable protocol-significant force, together with authority class, legitimacy basis, matter scope, and acted relation.

Contribution role preserves semantic participation in material. It does not by itself apply lawful force.

Flattening contribution role into governance would distort the body by treating participation in material as though it were automatically an act of authority. That is non-conformant. Authorization may appear as a contribution role type, but contribution-role authorization is still not identical to governance action as force-bearing record.

### 5.2 Witness Artifact

Witness preserves bounded trace of contact, observation, registration, or witnessed relation to protocol state.

Contribution role preserves how a contribution stood in relation to material.

Flattening contribution role into witness would distort the body by reducing semantic participation to mere contact trace. Witness can show that something was seen, received, checked, or registered. It does not by itself preserve whether the bounded relation was origin, elicitation, formalization, rendering, or transmission.

### 5.3 Validation Artifact

Validation preserves bounded condition checking: what was checked, under what condition set, with what outcome, and when.

Contribution role preserves participation relation, not condition evaluation.

Flattening contribution role into validation would distort the body by making role relation look like pass/fail checking. A validator may check something about contributed material, but that does not make the contribution role itself a validation artifact.

### 5.4 State and Transition Records

State records preserve what stands. Transition records preserve lawful movement between protocol-relevant states.

Contribution role preserves how bounded material participation relates to matter, artifact, actor, and related governance surface where relevant.

Flattening contribution role into state or transition would distort the body by forcing participation relation into standing-state or movement records. State and transition answer what stands and how it lawfully changed. Contribution role answers how participation in material should be preserved without becoming state or change by itself.

### 5.5 Generic Authorship or Actor Language

Generic authorship language compresses multiple semantic roles into one name. Generic actor language only names a participant without preserving the role relation.

Contribution role preserves the typed relation itself.

Flattening contribution role into generic authorship or generic actor language would distort the body by making co-produced material falsely simple and by making later provenance reconstruction dependent on narrative guesswork rather than explicit role preservation.

## 6. Implementation-Facing Placement

The current best bounded implementation-facing read is that contribution role already belongs inside record-family planning, but is underhoused relative to the other named families.

With `implementation/CANONICAL_RECORDS_OVERVIEW.md`, the placement is direct. That file already names contribution role records as a core record family and already states that a contribution role record should preserve role type and relation to matter, actor, artifact, and related governance action where applicable. This overview does not create that family. It gives that already-named family its own bounded implementation-facing legibility.

With `implementation/SCHEMA_MAP.md`, the placement is still open. The current schema-planning order does not yet schedule a contribution-role schema in the first set. This file does not override that order. It clarifies that contribution role is now implementation-facing enough to be placed deliberately later, rather than being omitted by ambient memory or flattened into some other schema family.

With `runtime/REGISTRY_CONTRACT.md`, the placement is also bounded. The registry may preserve contribution records where relevant, which means contribution role is not merely a readability convenience. At the same time, this note does not claim that final runtime admission, envelope shape, or preservation flow is already settled.

The safest current read is therefore:

- contribution role already stands as a real implementation-facing record-family concern
- it sits nearer canonical record planning than derivative readability
- its final schema and runtime path remain open

This is placement clarification only, not migration.
