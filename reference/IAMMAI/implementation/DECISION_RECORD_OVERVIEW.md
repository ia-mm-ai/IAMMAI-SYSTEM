# IAMMAI Decision Record Overview

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the first-pass implementation-facing overview for decision record inside the IAMMAI body.

Its job is to give decision one bounded room in the implementation layer so later schema and mechanism work do not have to proceed from ambient review prose, bridge carry, or generic status language.

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- an implementation-family master map
- a schema file
- a runtime migration plan
- a storage architecture memo
- a graph or database design
- a distributed systems plan
- a roadmap
- a README
- a project-management note
- a manifesto

This file is additive only. It does not replace implementation, runtime, continuity, admissibility, accession, standing, vessel, bridge, review, or current-state surfaces already standing in the body.

## 2. Why This Note Is Needed Now

The implementation layer already houses several record families explicitly. State, transition, validation, witness, governance, and contribution now have bounded implementation-facing rooms.

The body also already makes real bounded decisions. `accession/ADMISSION_DECISION.md` stands as an explicit accession decision surface. `vessel/current_state_what_stands_reader_v1/API_BACKED_POSTURE_DECISION__POST_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW.md` stands as an explicit bounded posture decision. The body therefore already has visible decision reality, not only abstract decision language.

Decision nevertheless remains comparatively underhoused as an implementation-facing record family. The state machine already distinguishes decision as a standing-state role, but the implementation layer does not yet give decision the same bounded record-facing room that other nearby preserved concerns now have.

The body therefore needs one bounded room for decision before later schema or mechanism work could be lawful without flattening decision into governance, currentness, bridge carry, or review atmosphere.

## 3. What Decision Record Is

In implementation-facing terms, a decision record preserves a bounded commitment, outcome, or posture determination for a scoped matter.

It is about a determination that now stands in bounded form. It is not merely discussion, and it is not yet applied effect.

At first pass, decision record is concerned with preserving, in bounded form:

- what matter or scope was being decided
- what commitment, outcome, or posture was determined
- what bounded basis or review posture that determination depended on
- where that determination stands as a visible decided surface

This keeps decision clear as decision. It preserves that decision is more than review mood and less than consequence by itself.

## 4. Why Generic Review Or Status Language Is Too Weak

Generic review prose or atmospheric current-state language is too weak because it does not preserve whether a bounded matter was actually decided, what was decided, and how that decision should be read in relation to later carry.

If decision remains only inside review prose, bridge prose, or general status speech, later readers can easily confuse:

- a review that informed a decision
- a decision that actually determined posture
- a bridge that later carried the consequence of that decision
- a currentness surface that later located what now governs

That collapse would make decision recoverable only by interpretation. The body is already too explicit elsewhere to leave decision at that weaker level.

## 5. Distinction From Neighboring Record Families

### 5.1 Governance Action

Governance action preserves attributable lawful force, including actor, authority class, legitimacy basis, matter scope, and acted relation.

Decision record preserves a bounded determination for a scoped matter.

Flattening decision into governance would distort the body by treating every decision as though its primary meaning were force-bearing authority. Some decisions may involve governance. Decision is still not identical to governance action as such.

### 5.2 State and Transition Records

State records preserve what stands. Transition records preserve lawful movement between protocol-relevant states or conditions.

Decision record preserves the bounded determination that a matter now stands in a decided posture or committed direction.

Flattening decision into state or transition would distort the body by losing the specific fact that a determination occurred as decision rather than as generic state presence or generic movement. The state machine already says decision is a standing-state role. That still does not by itself give decision explicit record-facing housing.

### 5.3 Currentness Record

Currentness record preserves what presently governs for one bounded scope, in what posture, on what basis, and against which visible non-current neighbors.

Decision record preserves the bounded determination itself.

Flattening decision into currentness would distort the body by confusing a decision with a later housing of present governing relation. Currentness is authority-locating. Decision is the determined posture or committed direction that may later become part of what currentness points to.

### 5.4 Bridge Carry

Bridge carry preserves bounded carried consequence from one already-standing surface back into another part of the body.

Decision record preserves the determination that is being carried or later relied on.

Flattening decision into bridge carry would distort the body by making carried consequence look identical to the underlying decided matter. A bridge may carry what a decision now means for another ranked area. It is not the same thing as the decision record itself.

### 5.5 Generic Review / Posture Prose

Generic review or posture prose preserves evaluation, analysis, caution, or bounded explanation.

Decision record preserves that a bounded matter has been determined in a specific way.

Flattening decision into generic review prose would distort the body by making later readers infer decision from tone, closing sentence, or surrounding atmosphere. Not every review or posture note is automatically a decision record, even when it informs one.

## 6. Implementation-Facing Placement

The current best bounded implementation-facing read is that decision record belongs inside record-family planning, but has not yet been given its own explicit room.

With `implementation/CANONICAL_RECORDS_OVERVIEW.md`, the current situation is partial. That file already preserves state, transition, resolution, governance, contribution, witness, validation, and lineage, and it already states that decision is a standing-state role through the state-machine-adjacent record model. But it does not yet give decision explicit record-family treatment in its own bounded terms.

With `implementation/STATE_MACHINE_OVERVIEW.md`, the placement pressure is strong. That file already defines decision as a distinct standing-state role: committed direction on the basis of preserved state, not truth itself and not applied effect. That means decision is already structurally real enough that implementation-facing record planning can no longer treat it as only background vocabulary.

With `implementation/GOVERNANCE_ACTION_OVERVIEW.md`, the placement boundary is also clear. Governance may perform, authorize, or preserve acts around decision, but governance is not identical to decision. Decision record therefore should not be absorbed into governance record planning.

With `implementation/SCHEMA_MAP.md`, the placement remains open. The current first schema set does not yet name decision record as a schema family. This note does not override that order. It clarifies that decision is now implementation-facing enough to be placed deliberately later rather than being left under ambient prose.

The safest current read is therefore:

- decision record is now a real implementation-facing concern
- it sits closer to state, transition, and standing-state record planning than to governance-only planning
- it may become a record family or a bounded record-family extension later
- this note does not force final schema or runtime admission

## 7. Relationship to Visible Decision Surfaces

`accession/ADMISSION_DECISION.md` shows that the body already treats bounded acceptance, refusal, and not-yet posture as explicit decision rather than as generic surrounding review. It demonstrates decision reality in the accession layer without by itself settling implementation record shape.

`vessel/current_state_what_stands_reader_v1/API_BACKED_POSTURE_DECISION__POST_ALIGNMENT_REVIEW.md` shows that the body already treats bounded posture determination as explicit decision. It demonstrates that a derivative, local, non-sovereign branch can still carry a real decision surface without that decision becoming governance, currentness, or final standing.

`v1/34_POST_API_BACKED_POSTURE_BRIDGE.md` shows that the body already distinguishes a decision from its later carried consequence. The bridge carries the result of the API-backed posture decision back into `v1/`. That distinction is exactly why decision should not be collapsed into bridge carry.

These surfaces therefore demonstrate that decision is already real in the body. They do not yet settle the final decision-record envelope, schema shape, or runtime status.
