# IAMMAI Version Lineage

The current root tree remains the source-side v0 lineage body.

A later `v1/` may be opened as a new admissible derivation line beside root. If that line opens, it should stand as a later derivation rather than as a cleaned present that hides what the root tree actually became.

## Why Re-Derivation May Be Needed

Re-derivation may become necessary where the body shows class-level drift such as:

- derivation drift between doctrine, schema, runtime, and emitted artifact shape
- unstable enforcement surfaces
- fixture-classification ambiguity
- explanatory surfaces overtaken by the body’s actual emitted state

These are lineage reasons for a new derivation line, not reasons to rewrite the root tree into a tidier memory.

## What Remains Source-Bound From v0

The following remain source-bound from v0:

- the root tree as the v0 lineage body
- the constitutional source artifacts and their governing priority
- the visible proof, drift, experiments, and bounded corrections that actually formed this line
- the source-side legibility of how the body reached its present root state

## What May Later Be Re-Derived in v1

The following may later be re-derived in a v1 line where lawful and needed:

- explanatory surfaces
- schema and runtime alignment surfaces
- enforcement and admissibility surfaces
- fixture classification and repo-organization surfaces

Such re-derivation belongs in a later line beside root, not as a rewrite of what v0 was.

Freeze what held. Re-derive what drifted.
