## 4. What A First Lawful Pilot Action Would Need To Be

A first lawful pilot action would need to be:

- additive
- derivative-only
- reviewable
- traceable
- revocable
- low-sovereignty
- branch-local
- non-authority-bearing
- non-architecture-deciding

It should also be small enough that failure or overreach can be stopped without creating counterfeit continuity or symbolic inflation.

That means the first action should not be source-writing, branch-selection, architecture-shaping, currentness-determining, governance-bearing, or runtime-shaping work. It should sit at the lowest lawful end of the already-visible derivative task set.

## 7. Mandatory Supervision, Refusal, Trace, And Review Boundaries

Even this one action would require the following.

- explicit human selection of the task
- explicit human selection of the target branch and target schema surface
- explicit supervision surface rather than ambient oversight
- refusal if rank, scope, branch maturity, or authority conditions are not met
- refusal if the requested work drifts from one invalid fixture into schema revision, checker revision, currentness inference, governance implication, or branch expansion
- traceability of the proposed output as one derivative candidate artifact
- explicit human review before any acceptance, storage, or further derivative use
- no self-chaining
- no self-expansion from one action into broader permission

The participant would therefore remain unable to choose a second task, choose a neighboring branch, or promote the result by saying the first task succeeded.
