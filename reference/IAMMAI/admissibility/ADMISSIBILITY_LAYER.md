# IAMMAI Admissibility Layer

## 1. Purpose

This file is the master repo-native map of admissibility as a bounded cross-rank layer already visible in the IAMMAI body in partial form.

Its job is to make that layer explicit without pretending that admissibility has been absent until now, and without collapsing the different admissibility relations already carried by constitutional, runtime, v1, and continuity surfaces.

It does not:

- rewrite the constitutional protocol
- create a new constitutional protocol
- create a sixth authoritative layer
- replace `runtime/VALIDATOR_CONTRACT.md`
- replace `v1/runtime/SOURCE_SELECTION_AND_ADMISSIBILITY_POSTURE.md`
- replace `v1/05_STANDING_CRITERIA.md`
- replace `v1/22_STANDING_REVIEW__POST_LAWFUL_EGRESS_AND_CROSS_CARRIER_LINE.md`
- replace `continuity/LAWFUL_TRANSFER_SEAM_v0.md` or `continuity/RELEASE_AND_INGRESS_v0.md`

This file is additive only. It clarifies a layer already distributed across the current body. It does not replace existing constitutional, runtime, v1, or continuity surfaces.

## 2. Why This Layer Is Needed Now

The body has reached a density at which lawful intake, lawful source use, lawful transition checking, and lawful standing admission can no longer remain only implicit.

The repository now contains enough ranked material that convenience, readability, archive residue, preserved execution history, or rhetorical force could otherwise begin to collapse into hidden authority. That risk now appears across more than one rank:

- candidate material may be encountered and handled before its lawful intake posture is made explicit
- preserved material may exist on disk before its lawful source use is made explicit
- carried or arriving material may appear to cross a seam before lawful release, lawful ingress, or lawful transition checking are kept distinct
- proof-bearing and proof-readable derivative surfaces may begin to press toward standing or canon-facing admission without a clear membrane separating visibility from admission

This file exists to reduce that risk by explicit layer clarification, not by rewriting prior files.

## 3. Existing Roots in the Body

Admissibility already appears in the current body in more than one place.

At constitutional rank, the public constitutional protocol, its current repo-native constitutional integrity edition, and `protocol/canon.json` already place admissibility inside the preparation regime rather than inside standing. Acceptance is the phase by which candidate signal becomes admitted for structured protocol handling. It owns admissible intake, not truth, not standing, and not consequence.

That means the constitutional root of admissibility is already present as lawful intake posture. This file does not create it.

### 3.2 Validator-side admissibility checking

At runtime rank, `architecture/RUNTIME_COMPONENTS.md` and `runtime/VALIDATOR_CONTRACT.md` already bind the Validator as the bounded checker of admissibility, threshold, transition legality, governance preconditions, and related typed conditions.

Those surfaces already make clear that:

- candidate material is not admissible merely because it is available
- admissible material is not standing by that fact alone
- validator outputs do not silently become governance authorization, standing state, or semantic source

This is already an admissibility root inside the runtime body.

### 3.3 V1 source-selection and source-admissibility posture

At v1 runtime-law rank, `v1/runtime/SOURCE_SELECTION_AND_ADMISSIBILITY_POSTURE.md` already states that only admissible preserved sources may be used as sources.

That file already distinguishes source classes, relation classes, matter coherence, anchor discipline, and recording posture. It already rejects directory presence, filesystem convenience, recency, and retrieval ease as hidden substitutes for lawful source admissibility.

This is already an admissibility root at source-use level.

### 3.4 Present standing and canon-facing admission pressure in the v1 line

At the present v1 line, `v1/05_STANDING_CRITERIA.md` and `v1/22_STANDING_REVIEW__POST_LAWFUL_EGRESS_AND_CROSS_CARRIER_LINE.md` already show admission pressure at the standing-facing edge of the body.

Those surfaces already make visible that:

- `v1/` may be a real admissible derivation line without thereby gaining total closure
- proof-bearing and proof-readable material may materially strengthen standing review without thereby becoming final standing
- additive lineage visibility may strengthen admission pressure without becoming automatic canon or automatic standing

This pressure is already present. What is still needed is the explicit layer map that shows how intake, source use, transition checking, and standing-facing admission remain related without becoming one flat mechanism.

## 4. Layer Position

Admissibility is the lawful gate or membrane that answers whether something may enter handling, relation, transition checking, source use, or standing-facing claim pressure without yet being mistaken for truth, standing, governance authorization, or finality.

At layer position, admissibility sits:

- after mere encounter, because encounter alone does not admit anything
- before collapse into truth or standing, because admissibility is not a truth-maker
- beside scope and threshold without becoming identical to them
- before or around lawful use, lawful checking, lawful crossing, and lawful admission pressure

Admissibility is therefore a gate before collapse, not a truth-maker and not a force-maker.

It is also cross-rank rather than sovereign. The layer appears constitutionally in Acceptance, architecturally in runtime role boundaries, validator-side in typed checking, v1-side in source-selection posture, continuity-side in release and ingress conditions, and standing-side in the present pressure around what may lawfully be treated as more than visible residue. None of that makes admissibility a new sovereign organ.

## 5. The Five Admissibility Domains

This file maps five major admissibility domains at layer level only.

### 5.1 Intake admissibility

Question:
May this candidate material enter structured protocol handling at all?

Governs:
The lawful difference between mere encounter and lawful intake. This is the constitutional acceptance-facing domain of admissibility.

Does not become:
Scope, threshold, truth, standing, or consequence.

### 5.2 Scope and matter admissibility

Question:
Even if something has been accepted for handling, is it admissible to this matter, relation, or bounded question?

Governs:
Whether a candidate, relation, or claim may lawfully be treated as matter-relevant and processable within a bounded domain rather than merely present in the environment.

Does not become:
Truth, standing, or a generic permission to treat all accepted material as equally in scope.

### 5.3 Source admissibility

Question:
May this preserved surface be used as a lawful source for the relation now being claimed?

Governs:
Selection and use of sources, including source class, positive-path readability, relation coherence, matter coherence, anchor discipline, and explicit recording of the selected source.

Does not become:
Source authority by convenience, automatic authoritativeness, or blanket permission to treat all preserved artifacts as lawful sources.

### 5.4 Transition admissibility

Question:
May this candidate movement, crossing, release, ingress, or requested state-transition even be treated as lawfully eligible for checking or handling in the claimed way?

Governs:
Bounded transition legality and seam-facing movement conditions, including release-side permissibility, ingress-side admissibility of arrival, predecessor or origin legibility, and the distinction between lawful crossing and counterfeit continuity.

Does not become:
Standing, native origin, native authority, or governance force.

### 5.5 Canon and standing admissibility

Question:
May this material lawfully press toward standing review or canon-facing admission without being mistaken for standing or canon already achieved?

Governs:
The bounded discipline by which proof-bearing, proof-readable, lineage-visible, or otherwise intensified material may be treated as admissible to standing-facing review and possible canon-facing admission pressure.

Does not become:
A final standing declaration, a full canon admission procedure, automatic constitutional force, or a rule that proof-bearing outputs become doctrine by default.

## 6. Preserved Distinctions

This layer must protect the following distinctions explicitly.

- encountered is not accepted
- accepted is not scoped
- scoped is not truth
- threshold is not standing
- source existence is not source admissibility
- admissible source is not authoritative source
- readability is not canon
- proof-bearing is not constitutional force
- visible lineage is not standing admission
- passed validation is not governance authorization

These distinctions are not stylistic. They are the anti-collapse work of the layer. If they are lost, admissibility dissolves into convenience, atmosphere, or rhetorical force and the ranked body becomes easier to counterfeit.

## 7. Relationship to Existing Surfaces

This layer relates to existing repo surfaces by clarification, not replacement.

At constitutional rank, the public constitutional protocol, the current constitutional integrity edition, `protocol/canon.json`, and protocol identity surfaces already preserve admissible intake inside Acceptance and preserve the preparation versus standing distinction. This file does not rebind that law. It makes the admissibility thread legible across the rest of the repo.

This file does not rewrite those roles. It names their shared admissibility function as part of one cross-rank layer.

At v1 derivation rank, `v1/01_SEAM_DECLARATION.md`, `v1/03_ARTIFACT_BOUNDARY_DECISION.md`, `v1/05_STANDING_CRITERIA.md`, `v1/22_STANDING_REVIEW__POST_LAWFUL_EGRESS_AND_CROSS_CARRIER_LINE.md`, and `v1/runtime/SOURCE_SELECTION_AND_ADMISSIBILITY_POSTURE.md` already preserve distinctions among derivation, artifact identity, source use, proof readability, and standing review. This file does not replace those distinctions. It clarifies that admissibility is one of the membranes helping keep them distinct.

At continuity rank, `continuity/LAWFUL_TRANSFER_SEAM_v0.md` and `continuity/RELEASE_AND_INGRESS_v0.md` already show that release, seam crossing, and ingress each involve bounded admission conditions. This file does not turn those continuity notes into standing law or source law. It locates them inside the transition-admissibility domain of the same cross-rank layer.

The effect of this file is therefore additive clarification. It preserves rank, inheritance posture, and local file function while making the admissibility structure legible without flattening it.

## 8. What This Layer Does Not Claim

This layer does not claim:

- that admissibility is a sixth authoritative layer
- that admissibility is a new sovereign organ
- that Acceptance, Validator, source-selection posture, standing criteria, standing review, seam law, or continuity notes are obsolete
- that admissibility is a universal one-step test that replaces the ranked body
- that every preserved artifact is admissible as source
- that every admissible source is authoritative
- that every proof-bearing output becomes doctrine
- that readability surfaces become canonical authority by readability alone
- that visible lineage is the same as standing admission
- that passed validation is the same as governance authorization
- that arrival, transfer, or ingress automatically creates native standing
- that admissibility has already been fully resolved everywhere in the repository
- that this file launders prior derivative or convenience surfaces into retroactive authority

## 9. Closing Boundary Statement

This file defines the admissibility layer at master-map level only.

Later file-level elaboration may further bind particular admissibility domains where the body needs that work. This file itself does not attempt to exhaust intake admissibility, source admissibility, transition admissibility, or canon and standing admissibility in full doctrinal detail.

Its purpose is narrower and firmer: to make the cross-rank admissibility structure legible inside the present IAMMAI body without rewriting prior lineage and without inflating admissibility into a false sovereign body.
