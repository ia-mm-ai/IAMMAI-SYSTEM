# IAMMAI Transition Admissibility

## 1. Purpose

This file defines transition admissibility as the bounded admissibility subdomain governing when a candidate movement, crossing, release, ingress, carried arrival, or requested state-transition may or may not lawfully proceed to checking or handling in the claimed way inside the IAMMAI body.

Its job is to clarify the lawful difference between candidate movement, transition admissibility, transition legality and checkability, carried arrival, native standing or origin claims, and the later domains that may follow after transition handling without letting those later domains collapse backward into transition admissibility itself.

It does not:

- rewrite the constitutional protocol
- rewrite `runtime/VALIDATOR_CONTRACT.md`
- rewrite `v1/01_SEAM_DECLARATION.md`
- rewrite `continuity/LAWFUL_TRANSFER_SEAM_v0.md`
- rewrite `continuity/RELEASE_AND_INGRESS_v0.md`
- rewrite `admissibility/SOURCE_ADMISSIBILITY.md`
- define a canon admission procedure
- declare standing
- define a runtime flow map
- define a transport or storage topology note
- act as a project-management note, README, or manifesto

This file is additive. It does not replace validator posture, seam posture, release and ingress law, or other ranked surfaces. It binds one admissibility domain more explicitly inside the body already standing.

## 2. Why This File Is Needed Now

The runtime and continuity roots for transition admissibility already exist.

The body now benefits from a clearer bounded file for this subdomain because the repository has become dense enough that carried movement, arrival visibility, later sequence, preserved proof, or readable residue could otherwise begin to counterfeit lawful transition posture if the distinction remains under-specified outside those roots.

That risk is now practical:

- a candidate crossing may appear lawful because it is visible or already preserved
- later sequence may begin to impersonate legitimacy
- readable or proof-bearing carried material may appear to have already completed lawful transition
- arrival may begin to impersonate native standing, native origin, or native authority by atmosphere alone

This file exists to reduce that risk by bounded clarification, not by rewriting the validator, seam, or continuity surfaces that already carry the root posture.

## 4. What Transition Admissibility Answers

Transition admissibility answers the bounded question:

**May this candidate movement, crossing, release, ingress, carried arrival, or requested state-transition even be treated as lawfully eligible for checking or handling in the claimed way?**

This is a transition question.

It is not yet:

- a final transition outcome
- a native standing claim
- a native origin claim
- a truth question
- a consequence question

Transition admissibility authorizes bounded checking or handling posture in lawful form. It does not by itself decide that lawful transition has already completed.

## 5. Candidate Movement, Admissible Transition, and Checked Transition

These must remain distinct.

### 5.1 Candidate movement

Candidate movement is a proposed or observed crossing, release, ingress, carried arrival, or requested state-transition before lawful admissibility has been granted.

Candidate movement may be visible, technically possible, sequence-later, preserved, proof-bearing, or locally convenient. None of those facts alone make it an admissible transition.

### 5.2 Admissible transition

Admissible transition is the bounded handling status produced when a candidate movement is lawfully eligible to proceed to checking or handling in the claimed way.

Admissible transition means the movement may be taken seriously as a transition candidate inside bounded handling. It does not mean the transition has already been validated as lawful in full.

### 5.3 Checked transition

Checked transition is a later state in which bounded legality or admissibility checks have actually been performed.

Checked transition remains distinct from:

- completed lawful transition
- native standing
- canon
- governance authorization
- consequence

If these distinctions are lost, candidate movement begins to counterfeit closure by mere motion.

## 6. Release, Ingress, and Origin Legibility

Transition admissibility depends on lawful release-side and ingress-side posture where those relations are in play.

Release-side posture matters because what approaches a seam must remain bounded before crossing. Not everything may leave, and permissibility for approach is not the same thing as completed transfer.

Ingress-side posture matters because lawful arrival must remain typed after crossing. Arrival may be received in bounded ways without being promoted into native standing, native origin, or native authority.

Origin legibility must therefore be preserved throughout.

Lawful ingress does not erase provenance.
Lawful crossing does not erase source relation.
Carried arrival does not become native standing or native authority by crossing alone.

Where transition admissibility is being considered, provenance must remain legible enough that later handling does not counterfeit native force.

## 7. Transition, Scope, Source, and Standing

Transition admissibility must remain distinct from adjacent domains.

- transition admissibility asks whether a candidate movement may lawfully proceed to checking or handling in the claimed way
- scope and matter admissibility ask whether material lawfully counts for the matter now at issue
- source admissibility asks whether a preserved surface may lawfully be used as source for the relation now being claimed
- standing and canon-facing admissibility ask whether visible material may lawfully press toward later review or admission

A movement may be transition-admissible while other domains remain unresolved.

For example:

- a carried arrival may be admissible to checking while source admissibility remains unresolved
- a requested transition may be admissible to handling while matter fit remains unresolved
- a crossing may be admissible without creating standing-facing pressure

Transition admissibility therefore does not settle scope, source, standing, or canon questions automatically.

## 8. Transition Admissibility and Later Review

An admissible transition may lawfully proceed toward later checking or handling without thereby settling later review questions.

Once a candidate movement is lawfully transition-admissible, later review may still determine that:

- legality checks do not pass
- source posture does not support the claimed use
- scope or matter fit does not support the claimed use
- threshold has not been met
- standing-facing or canon-facing pressure is not warranted
- governance authorization is absent

Transition admissibility therefore opens bounded transition posture. It does not close later review.

This is why admissible transition can be real and still remain provisional with respect to threshold, standing, canon, governance, and consequence.

## 9. What Transition Admissibility Does Not Grant

Transition admissibility does not itself create:

- native origin
- native standing
- native authority
- truth
- final standing
- canon
- governance authorization
- consequence

It also does not convert visibility into legitimacy. Technical possibility, preservation, sequence order, proof-bearing status, and readability may matter to why a movement is noticed, but they do not automatically make it lawfully admissible as transition.

## 11. Anti-Collapse Rules

A conforming transition-admissibility posture must not allow:

- candidate movement is not admissible transition
- admissible transition is not completed transition by default
- lawful crossing is not native standing
- lawful ingress is not native origin
- carried arrival is not native authority
- visibility is not transition admissibility
- preservation is not transition admissibility
- sequence-later is not legitimacy
- proof-bearing is not native force
- readability is not native standing
- checked transition is not governance authorization
- admissible crossing is not canon
- movement permission is not consequence

These rules are not stylistic. They are the minimum protections that keep bounded movement posture from collapsing into native standing, native origin, governance force, or final closure by convenience.

## 12. Closing Boundary Statement

This file defines transition admissibility only.

Later files may elaborate other admissibility domains. This file does not attempt to solve source admissibility, standing review, canon-facing admission, governance authorization, or consequence in one step.

It exists to make lawful transition posture more explicit inside the current IAMMAI body without rewriting validator or seam law, and without collapsing carried movement into native standing, origin, authority, or consequence.
