# IAMMAI Derivative Internal Intelligence Participation Boundary

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the gate for any future derivative internal intelligence participation inside the IAMMAI body.

Its job is to answer, in bounded admissibility terms:

- why this question is now live
- what such a participant would and would not be
- what minimum branch maturity would need to stand before any first-pass touch could even be considered
- what kinds of derivative work it may likely touch in first pass
- what it must not touch
- what refusal, trace, supervision, narrowing, suspension, and revocation boundaries would be mandatory

It does not define:

- a constitutional rewrite
- an implementation plan
- a runtime contract
- a vessel rollout
- an agent framework
- a work queue
- a roadmap
- a README
- a project-management note
- a manifesto

This file is additive only. It does not replace repo-scale current-state, readability, rank, or lineage surfaces. It also does not replace existing admissibility, encounter, accession, continuity, vessel, runtime, or implementation surfaces already standing in the body.

## 2. Why This Note Is Needed Now

This question is now mature enough to house explicitly because the body no longer lacks the surrounding law needed to hold it in bounded form.

The admissibility membrane family now stands in explicit form. Encounter now gives lawful nearness before entry. Accession now gives bounded external relation, eligibility, contact, and revocation posture. Continuity and currentness now give clearer anti-collapse housing around lineage, present governing relation, and derivative status. Vessel posture surfaces now show that derivative, source-limited, non-sovereign operation can be named precisely without counterfeiting source or authority. Implementation now shows repeated branch-complete first-pass lines that can house bounded derivative work without losing rank.

That is enough maturity to define the gate.

It is not enough to say that the body is already through the gate operationally. This note exists because the future question can now be held explicitly without being left to intuition, not because broad internal intelligence participation is already ready.

## 3. What Already Stands That Makes The Question Real

The following body conditions now make this question lawful to name.

- The admissibility layer now stands as an explicit membrane family across intake, source, scope and matter, transition, and canon and standing. That gives a lawful place for a participation gate to be defined as a membrane question rather than as an implementation convenience.
- Encounter now stands as lawful nearness before entry. This matters because derivative internal intelligence participation cannot be treated as though mere contact already equals permission.
- Accession now stands as bounded external relation without fake nativeness. Eligibility, contact law, bound vessel mode, and revocation and withdrawal already show that relation can be lawful, narrow, and reversible without being source-native or sovereign.
- Continuity and currentness now stand in clearer object form. This matters because any future participant boundary must preserve that currentness locates what governs and does not become a derivative participant power.
- The vessel posture surfaces now show visible evidence for derivative, read-only, source-limited, non-sovereign posture and for truthful non-passage where answered or operative posture does not presently stand.
- The implementation area now shows branch-complete first-pass lines with schema presence, examples, invalid boundaries, checkers, and current-surfaces notes. That is evidence that the body can already house bounded derivative work in ranked form rather than as atmospheric assistance.

These conditions make the question real. They do not make the lane active by default.

## 4. What A Derivative Internal Intelligence Participant Would Not Be

Such a participant would not be any of the following.

- source
- witness
- currentness locator or currentness authority
- canon-bearing or standing-bearing authority
- governance force or governance authorizer
- branch-selection authority
- family-versus-extension decider
- runtime admission authority
- storage-rank closure authority
- autonomous subsystem

It would also not be a lawful basis for promoting its own outputs into source, canon, authority, or present governing posture.

This non-equivalence is the first boundary. If it cannot be preserved, the lane cannot be opened lawfully at all.

## 5. Current Best Read Of What Such A Participant Could Be

The current best bounded read is that such a participant could only be:

- derivative
- internal in the sense of acting inside a later admitted bounded work lane rather than from outside contact alone
- supervised
- non-sovereign
- source-limited
- allowed to act only inside already-admitted permissions

In first-pass terms, this would mean a participant whose work remains additive, reviewable, traceable, and rank-limited. It would not carry native authority. It would not determine what governs. It would not decide what stands. It would not choose its own scope. It would not self-expand from one branch into another.

Its lawful meaning would therefore be narrower than “intelligence in the body” and narrower than “vessel in operation.” It would be one derivative participant posture that remains subordinate to existing body law.

## 6. Minimum Branch Maturity Required Before Touch

Before any first-pass touch permission could even be considered, the relevant branch would need enough maturity that derivative participation would not depend on atmospheric judgment alone.

At minimum, the following should already stand.

- explicit objecthood or equivalent bounded branch-defining housing
- explicit placement, admission, or boundary clarification where the branch materially needs it
- explicit current branch state through a current-surfaces or comparable re-entry surface
- explicit anti-collapse and non-claims posture so the participant is not forced to guess what the branch is not
- a clear derivative task lane rather than a vague invitation to help
- supervision that is still directly available at the branch level
- a touched surface that can be handled additively rather than by rewriting standing material in place

The visible body supports this only as a first-pass threshold, not as a universal final rule. The important point is narrower: no branch should be touchable merely because it exists. It should be touchable only where its derivative lane is already mature enough to survive bounded participation without rank blur.

## 7. What It May Touch In First Pass

If such a lane were ever later admitted, the first-pass touch permissions should remain derivative-only, additive, and maturity-dependent.

The likely first-pass touch set is narrow.

- additive derivative notes
- additive schemas
- positive examples
- invalid fixtures
- bounded checkers
- bounded current-surfaces successor notes
- narrowly scoped propagation work inside already-derivative implementation-facing or similarly matured branch lanes

Even these should be lawful only inside sufficiently mature branches and only where the task lane is already visible in the body.

This is not a permission for broad internal developmental participation. It is a candidate permission set for bounded derivative work only.

## 8. What It Must Not Touch

The first-pass no-touch zone must remain explicit.

At minimum, such a participant must not touch:

- constitutional source surfaces
- canon identity
- witness truth formation
- currentness determination
- governance authorization
- admissibility decisions about source, standing, or canon force
- branch selection
- family-versus-extension decisions
- runtime admission
- storage-rank closure
- in-place rewrites of standing surfaces

It must also not promote its own outputs into authority by later reference, convenience, or repeated use. Derivative participation cannot lawfully manufacture the rank that it does not possess.

## 9. Mandatory Refusal, Trace, Supervision, And Revocation Boundaries

If such a lane were ever later admitted, the following boundaries would need to be mandatory rather than optional.

- refusal where rank, scope, branch maturity, supervision, or authority conditions are not met
- refusal where the requested work would force the participant to infer currentness, witness truth, governance force, canon standing, branch selection, or final storage rank
- clear traceability for every permitted output so later readers can tell that the work was derivative, scoped, and review-bound
- supervision that remains visible and available rather than implicit or assumed
- no self-promotion of derivative output into source, canon, currentness, or authority
- explicit narrowing, pause, suspension, or revocation if blur, overreach, or rank pressure appears

The revocation posture should remain closer to the explicit narrowing and withdrawal discipline already visible in accession than to silent continuation after drift. The important continuity is structural, not classificatory. This note does not convert the participant into an accession object or a vessel by saying so.
