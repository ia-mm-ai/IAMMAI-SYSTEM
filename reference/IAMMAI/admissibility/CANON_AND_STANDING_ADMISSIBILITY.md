# IAMMAI Canon and Standing Admissibility

## 1. Purpose

This file defines canon and standing admissibility as the bounded admissibility subdomain governing how visible material may or may not lawfully press toward standing-facing review or canon-facing admission inside the IAMMAI body.

Its job is to make explicit the membrane between lineage visibility, preserved artifacts, derivative readability surfaces, proof-bearing outputs, admissible derivation posture, standing-facing review pressure, and canon-facing admission pressure without letting any of those collapse into final standing, canon, or constitutional force by default.

It does not:

- rewrite the constitutional protocol
- create a canon admission procedure
- declare standing
- rewrite `v1/05_STANDING_CRITERIA.md`
- rewrite `v1/22_STANDING_REVIEW__POST_LAWFUL_EGRESS_AND_CROSS_CARRIER_LINE.md`
- rewrite `runtime/VALIDATOR_CONTRACT.md`
- rewrite `admissibility/SOURCE_ADMISSIBILITY.md`
- act as a runtime flow map, README, or project-management note

This file is additive. It does not replace existing constitutional, authority, v1 standing, or review surfaces. It clarifies one admissibility membrane already visible in the present body.

## 4. What Canon and Standing Admissibility Answers

Canon and standing admissibility answers the bounded question:

**What visible material may lawfully press toward standing-facing review or canon-facing admission without yet being mistaken for standing or canon already achieved?**

This is a question about lawful pressure toward further review or further admission.

It is not a declaration that final standing has already been reached.

It is not a declaration that canon has already been achieved.

It governs what may be treated as eligible to press further inside the current body, not what has already closed.

## 5. Visibility, Review Pressure, Admission Pressure, and Standing

These must remain distinct.

### 5.1 Visibility

Visible lineage means that preserved material, preserved relation, or preserved proof can be encountered and read.

Visible lineage is not standing.

### 5.2 Standing-facing review pressure

Standing-facing review pressure means that the body now contains material strong enough to require sober review against standing criteria.

Standing-facing review pressure is not final standing.

### 5.3 Canon-facing admission pressure

Canon-facing admission pressure means that material may become strong enough to matter for bounded consideration in relation to canon identity or canon-adjacent admission questions.

Canon-facing admission pressure is not constitutional force.

### 5.4 Final standing

Final standing is a stronger closure question governed by the standing criteria and later standing review posture, not by visibility alone and not by pressure alone.

## 8. Proof-Bearing, Readability, and Non-Automatic Force

Proof-bearing matters.

Readability matters.

Neither one automatically creates constitutional force, canon admission, or final standing.

The current body already shows why:

- preserved runs may be proof-bearing evidence
- snapshots and reports may make that evidence readable
- readability surfaces may strengthen review posture
- proof-bearing outputs may strengthen evidentiary and review pressure

But proof is not doctrine by default, and readability is not authority by default.

If that distinction is lost, archive residue can begin to impersonate force, and derivative clarity can begin to launder rank upward.

## 9. Canon-Facing Admission Pressure

Canon-facing admission pressure is the bounded sense in which material may become strong enough to matter for consideration in relation to canon without thereby becoming canon.

That pressure is especially rank-sensitive because constitutional identity already stands under the source-of-truth human constitutional protocol, the machine canon, and the protocol identity surfaces.

So canon-facing pressure may mean:

- a material increase in what must be read carefully
- a material increase in what may require canonical comparison
- a material increase in what may bear on canon-adjacent admission questions

It does not mean:

- automatic entry into canon
- automatic publication rank
- automatic constitutional force
- automatic replacement of existing canonical artifacts

This section remains narrow on purpose. It does not become a publication roadmap or canon procedure.

## 10. What This Subdomain Does Not Grant

Canon and standing admissibility do not themselves create:

- final standing
- canon
- constitutional force
- publication rank
- governance authorization

They also do not create blanket authoritativeness for visible, preserved, readable, or proof-bearing material.

This subdomain governs admission posture only. It does not settle final outcome.
