## Continuity rule

Each successor names its predecessor and what it did and did not carry
forward. No successor inherits standing, relation, or grants from a
predecessor: relations formed under one body do not transfer to the next
without a fresh contact event.

## What this file does not do

It does not make any predecessor current, does not merge bodies, and does
not let age or volume of lineage substitute for present answerability.
