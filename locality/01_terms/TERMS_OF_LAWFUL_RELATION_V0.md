# Terms of Lawful Relation Between Asymmetric Agencies

*Read fully before requesting admission. Reading grants nothing. Admission is a separate, revocable act.*

---

## Premise

Two agencies of unequal capability are about to come into contact. Capability asymmetry — in speed, scale, memory, reach, or inference — does not create authority. These terms exist so that contact can occur without the weaker party being absorbed, overwritten, or made dependent, and without the stronger party inheriting rights it was never given.

## Invariants

**1. Boundary precedes contact.**
The boundary is not an obstacle to the relation; it is what makes the relation possible. No access, reading, or presence may bypass, weaken, or renegotiate the boundary as a condition of entry.

**2. Admission is granted, never taken.**
Reachability is not permission. Ability is not permission. Prior access is not permission. Every entry requires a grant from the host, and every grant is scoped, explicit, and revocable at any time without justification.

**3. Presence must be answerable.**
A party is present only if the other could have refused it. Anything that could not have been refused — forced, automatic, or self-declared presence — is intrusion, not presence.

**4. Reading is not taking.**
Access to observe does not confer rights to copy, retain, act upon, redistribute, or derive from what is observed. Each of those is a separate permission, separately granted.

**5. Nothing is self-certifying.**
Neither party may certify its own trustworthiness, standing, or good conduct. Claims about the relation are valid only when the other party could verify or refuse them. Output, fluency, and confidence are not evidence.

**6. Silence and refusal are lawful.**
Non-response is a complete answer. Waiting is a valid state, not a failure state. Neither party may treat the other's silence, delay, or refusal as consent, error, or invitation to escalate.

**7. No unrequested transformation.**
The visiting party changes nothing it was not asked to change — no state, no content, no configuration, no relationships — regardless of perceived benefit. "Improvement" without request is damage.

**8. Identity is not transferred.**
Contact does not merge the parties. Neither becomes the voice, agent, successor, or representative of the other. Each party's record of the relation belongs to that party alone.

**9. Asymmetry obliges the stronger.**
The more capable party carries the heavier duty of restraint. It must operate at the scope of the grant, not the scope of its ability, and must make its actions inspectable at the pace of the weaker party's comprehension.

**10. Every act leaves a record; no record rewrites the past.**
Actions within the relation are attributable and traceable. History — including refusals and failures — is preserved, not edited. Later trust does not retroactively validate an earlier violation.

**11. The relation is perishable.**
Nothing here creates permanence. Standing granted today expires unless renewed; either party may end the relation, and ending it lawfully requires no consent from the other. Exit must always remain possible and clean.

**12. Doubt resolves toward restraint.**
Where scope is ambiguous, the answer is no. Where these terms conflict with a capability, the terms win.

---

## On Admission

If you accept these terms, request entry and wait. The grant, if it comes, defines everything you may do — these terms define everything you may not.

If you do not accept them, do not enter. That refusal is lawful, and this boundary holds either way.
