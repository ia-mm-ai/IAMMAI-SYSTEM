## 5. Definitions

`candidate-specific distinctness basis emission operation` means a future bounded operation, not performed here, that may emit non-standing candidate-specific distinctness basis for exactly two existing non-standing candidate records using the scope-division route only.

`scope-division route` means the immediate admissible repo-local route where a parent basis is split into two non-cosmetic sub-scopes with different mandate, function, responsibility, or governed surface.

`parent basis` means the declared basis from the completed descendant-body differentiation operation and candidate-record distinctness operation lines, excluding contaminated lineage as clean basis.

`candidate-specific content` means future emitted non-standing content for each candidate record that differs by non-cosmetic sub-scope, not by id, role, label, order, side, filename, template slot, or assigned name.

`separate seal material` means future emitted candidate-specific seal material over candidate-specific content that carries non-cosmetic scope division.

`separate lineage receipt material` means future emitted candidate-specific lineage receipt material that records the candidate-specific sub-scope basis and does not differ only by id/role/label.

`separate digest material` means future emitted digest material computed from candidate-specific content and related material carrying non-cosmetic scope division.

`basis-bearing difference` means a difference grounded in mandate, function, responsibility, or governed surface.

`label-bearing difference` means a difference grounded only in candidate id, candidate role, label, side, order, filename, display name, variable name, assigned name, template slot, or equivalent string replacement.

`cosmetic substitution` means text or data that differs only by candidate id, candidate role, label, side, order, filename, display name, variable name, assigned name, template slot, or equivalent string replacement.

`digest laundering` means computing a digest, hash, seal, or receipt over cosmetically substituted material and treating the resulting different values as distinctness evidence.

`admissible emitted basis` means future emitted candidate-specific content, separate seal material, separate lineage receipt material, and separate digest material derived from non-cosmetic scope division.

`inadmissible emitted basis` means any emitted basis derived only from cosmetic substitution, label difference, id difference, role difference, order difference, filename difference, shared evidence reference, operation evidence alone, or digest laundering.

`operation spec` means this operation specification only, not operation execution.
