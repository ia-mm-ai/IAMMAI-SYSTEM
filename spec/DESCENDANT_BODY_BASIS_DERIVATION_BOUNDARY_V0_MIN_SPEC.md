## 5. Definitions

`parent body-basis` means the standing `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` body-proof basis considered only as possible basis for a future descendant-body-basis derivation event.

`descendant body-basis` means a possible future body-basis that would have to be derived through a separately bounded future step with separate lineage receipt, separate sealing, separate standing check, traceability, non-synchronization, no hidden synchronization, no source transfer, and no authority transfer.

`standing body-proof basis` means the standing proof posture of `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` as body. It is preserved as basis only and is not a transferable source, authority, currentness, standing, relation frame, or runtime permission.

`derivation boundary` means this boundary specification. It defines requirements for a future derivation event without performing derivation.

`future derivation event` means a later event that is not created, scheduled, authorized, or executed by this file.

`separately bounded future step` means a future step requiring its own declared basis, checks, non-claims, and closure before it may stand.

`distinguishable body-bases` means body-bases whose distinction is not produced by file duplication, label duplication, aliasing, parallel naming, copied packet identity, cosmetic A/B naming, shared hidden state, hidden synchronization, source transfer, authority transfer, or parent-body monarchy.

`separate sealing` means each future descendant body-basis would need its own bounded sealing posture. This boundary creates no sealing.

`separate standing check` means each future descendant body-basis would need its own bounded standing check. This boundary performs no standing check.

`traceability` means future derivation would have to preserve declared basis, lineage, sealing, standing check, refusal posture, and non-claims in a recoverable form. This boundary creates no trace artifact.

`non-synchronization` means future descendant body-bases must not rely on hidden shared state, concealed update channels, implicit coordination, silent synchronization, or shared currentness.

`no hidden synchronization` means no concealed coupling may substitute for lawful body-basis distinction.

`no source transfer` means no source, source role, source authority, or source currentness moves from the parent body-basis to any future descendant body-basis by this boundary.

`no authority transfer` means no authority, currentness, standing, truth, action permission, relation permission, FIELD permission, or follow-on permission transfers by this boundary.
