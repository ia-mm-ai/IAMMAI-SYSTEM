## 4. Definitions

`distributed standing boundary conformance` means bounded verification that a selected distributed standing boundary result preserved its declared prerequisite basis, evidence, lineage, refusal/divergence posture, projection posture, summary/detail correspondence, and non-claims.

`selected distributed standing boundary result` means the declared distributed standing boundary artifact selected for conformance review. In the present execution line, the live selected artifact is `artifacts/integrity_host_v0_min_coexistence_distributed_standing_boundary/carrier_b_c_distributed_standing_boundary_001__distributed_standing_result.json`.

`conformance basis` means the declared conformance question, selected result identity, selected result path or artifact identity, selected result outcome, parseability where supplied, selected result sections, prerequisite basis sections, check records, statement, summary/detail correspondence, and explicit non-claims.

`conformance claim` means the bounded assertion that the selected distributed standing boundary result is conformant under this conformance specification.

`conformance refusal` means refusal to proceed with conformance because the selected result, conformance question, artifact identity, outcome, parseability, prerequisite basis, visibility posture, summary/detail correspondence, or non-claims are missing, malformed, hidden, contradictory, or collapse-shaped.

`prerequisite basis preservation` means the selected distributed standing boundary result preserved the prerequisite surfaces it claimed to preserve as prerequisite basis only, without letting any prerequisite surface create standing by itself.

`non-claim preservation` means required false postures remain present and false in the selected result.

`summary/detail correspondence` means summary, statement, and check projections correspond to the detailed basis rather than overriding it.

`conformance non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that conformance did not create permission, continuation, operation, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, carrier currentness, current carrier selection, winner/loser selection, source replacement, authority, truth, action, divergence resolution, evidence erasure, or distributed operation.

Conformance does not create permission.

Conformance does not create continuation.

Conformance does not create operation.

Conformance does not mutate the selected result.

## 5. Conformance Outcome Family

A future implementation may use this bounded outcome family:

- `DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_CONFORMANT`
- `DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_NOT_CONFORMANT`
- `DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_CONFORMANCE_BLOCKED`

`DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_CONFORMANT` means the selected distributed standing boundary result preserved required basis and non-claims under this conformance specification.

`DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_NOT_CONFORMANT` means the selected result was readable and identifiable as a distributed standing boundary result, but one or more conformance checks failed.

`DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_CONFORMANCE_BLOCKED` means conformance review could not lawfully proceed because required input was missing, malformed, hidden, summary-overwriting, non-claim-flipped, or collapse-shaped.

This outcome family records conformance only. It does not record a new distributed standing posture, closure of meaning, implementation permission, continuation, synchronization, full body transfer, or distributed operation.
