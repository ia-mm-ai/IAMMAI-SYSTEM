# Multi-Carrier Relation Conformance Closure V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines bounded closure for the meaning of `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANT` in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

The purpose is to prevent "relation conformance passed" from becoming distributed standing, currentness, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, synchronization, continuation, or permission for another physical carrier experiment.

Multi-carrier relation conformance closure records what a selected conformant multi-carrier relation result means and does not mean. It is a closure of meaning only.

Conformance closure is not conformance.

Conformance closure is not relation creation.

Conformance closure is not distributed standing.

Conformance closure is not currentness.

Conformance closure is not carrier hierarchy.

Conformance closure is not divergence resolution.

Conformance closure is not permission to continue.

Conformance closure records the meaning and non-meaning of a selected conformant multi-carrier relation result.

This file is a specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, distributed standing surface, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization layer, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`multi-carrier relation conformance closure` means the bounded recording of what a selected `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANT` result means and does not mean.

`selected conformant relation result` means one selected multi-carrier relation conformance result whose outcome is `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANT`.

`closure basis` means the selected conformance result, selected relation basis, selected carriers, selected carrier evidence, conformance checks, conformance statement, conformance non-meaning, visible refusal posture, visible divergence posture, and explicit non-claims that permit closure of meaning.

`closure claim` means the bounded assertion that the meaning of the selected conformant relation result has been recorded and bounded.

`closure refusal` means refusal to record closure because the selected conformance result is missing, malformed, not conformant, collapse-shaped, or because closure would create authority, permission, currentness, distributed standing, continuation, expansion, or another forbidden posture.

`closure statement` means the explicit statement of what the selected conformant relation result means in bounded form.

`conformance meaning` means the preserved meaning that the selected recognized multi-carrier relation cohered under bounded conformance checks.

`conformance non-meaning` means the preserved posture that conformance did not create distributed standing, currentness, authority, permission, source replacement, carrier hierarchy, carrier selection, divergence resolution, continuation, or expansion.

`closed relation-conformance posture` means a closure-recorded posture over one selected conformant relation result. It does not mean distributed standing.

`closure non-claim` means an explicit false claim that closure did not create or authorize a forbidden posture.

Closed relation-conformance posture does not mean distributed standing.

Closure of meaning does not mean completion of the body.

Closure does not authorize next work.

## 5. What Closure Means

Relation conformance closure means that a closure surface records that the selected `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANT` result means only:

- the selected recognized multi-carrier relation cohered under bounded conformance checks
- selected relation was preserved
- selected carriers were preserved
- selected evidence was preserved
- carrier roles were preserved
- local outcomes were preserved
- visible refusal was preserved
- visible divergence was preserved
- downstream evidence posture was preserved
- currentness participation remained participation
- no current carrier was selected
- no winning carrier was selected
- no losing carrier was invalidated
- no carrier hierarchy was created
- no distributed standing was created
- no source, currentness, authority, or permission was created
- no presence, threshold, truth, action, or consequence was created
- no continuation was authorized
- no additional carrier experiment was authorized
- no distributed operation was authorized

Closure may record this meaning. Closure may preserve the conformance posture as closed in meaning.

Closure does not add force to the relation, the carriers, the evidence, or the body line.
