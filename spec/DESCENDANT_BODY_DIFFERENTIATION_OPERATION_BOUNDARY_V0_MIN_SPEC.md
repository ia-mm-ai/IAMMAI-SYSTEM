`candidate record` means a future non-standing output record of a separately implemented differentiation operation, if that operation records successfully.

`non-standing descendant-body-basis candidate` means a possible future candidate record that has not yet passed standing, relation, crossing, FIELD, runtime, currentness, authority, output, action, derivative reception, synchronization, or follow-on checks.

`differentiation evidence` means separately emitted operation evidence sufficient to support candidate-record existence claims, not Markdown declaration alone.

`visible block` means a future operation result that refuses candidate production and records why no candidate records were produced.

`contaminated lineage` means `spec/DESCENDANT_BODY_BASIS_DERIVATION_EVENT_V0_MIN_SPEC.md` preserved unchanged for the unsupported existence-claim class.

`boundary` means this permission-to-define-future-operation boundary only, not permission to perform the operation.
