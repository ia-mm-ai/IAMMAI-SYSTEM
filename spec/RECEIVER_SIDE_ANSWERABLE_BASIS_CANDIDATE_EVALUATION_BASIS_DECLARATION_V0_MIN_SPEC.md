# Receiver-Side Answerable Basis Candidate Evaluation Basis Declaration V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines the shape and non-claims of one future separately prepared receiver-side answerable-basis candidate evaluation-basis declaration. It is bounded input shape for the completed candidate-evaluation operation machinery. It does not prepare, populate, record, validate, supply, admit, or execute a live declaration; invoke a resolver; evaluate the selected candidate; derive a dimension result; or authorize source-side fabrication of evaluation basis.

## 2. Scope

The declaration concerns one exact selected candidate, one completed candidate-evaluation boundary, one completed waiting evaluation operation, and exactly eight separate evaluation dimensions. It creates no resolver, test, artifact, terminal summary, live basis, populated dimension record, request, evaluation, candidate sufficiency, candidate insufficiency, candidate-level indeterminacy, receiver attestation, receiver answerable receipt, presence re-evaluation, identity, relation, coupling, FIELD machinery, runtime, API, public intake, authority, standing, or follow-on authorization.

## 3. Declaration Question

Given the selected candidate already supplied, received, recorded, and preserved; the completed boundary allowing evaluation consideration; and the completed operation recording `REQUIRES_EVALUATION_BASIS` with all eight dimensions `NOT_EVALUATED`, what bounded declaration shape may later carry separately prepared basis inputs without converting candidate material, source-side interpretation, evaluator reference, declaration, repository access, or desired outcome into established evidence or a pre-claimed result?

## 4. Required Answer

One future declaration may be separately prepared and supplied for exactly one selected candidate and eight dimensions. It may carry bounded basis inputs and explicit rule-input postures only. It must not carry caller-selected dimension results, candidate-level results, evaluator authority, identity, standing, or truth claims, or repository access as evidence. It becomes neither admitted operation basis nor an evaluation merely by existing, being ready for supply, or being supplied; only the existing operation resolver may separately admit it.
