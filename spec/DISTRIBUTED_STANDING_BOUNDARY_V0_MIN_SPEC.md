# Distributed Standing Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded distributed standing boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

It defines what it would mean for the body to stand across carriers without source collapse, carrier sovereignty, registry authority, current-carrier selection, winner/loser selection, synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, continuation, or distributed operation.

The purpose is to define what it would mean for standing to persist across carriers without allowing carriers, registries, receipts, propagation, currentness successor, divergence consequence, majority, recency, or synchronization to become source, truth, currentness, authority, or distributed operation.

Distributed standing is not synchronization.

Distributed standing is not distributed currentness.

Distributed standing is not carrier majority.

Distributed standing is not registry presence.

Distributed standing is not latest file.

Distributed standing is not carrier availability.

Distributed standing is not body transfer.

Distributed standing is not a second body.

Distributed standing is not carrier sovereignty.

Distributed standing is not current carrier selection.

Distributed standing is not winner/loser selection.

Distributed standing is not action.

Distributed standing is not truth.

Distributed standing is a bounded body-side standing posture across plural carrier evidence only if source lineage, currentness accounting, divergence consequence, standing propagation, carrier lifecycle, registry/persistence, continuity-turn lineage, relation/conformance/closure, and current-body conformance remain preserved without collapse.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, distributed standing execution, distributed standing claim, distributed operation, carrier registry implementation, persistence implementation, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, carrier election, consensus system, leader election, network topology, truth law, action law, consequence/action law, presence surface, threshold surface, body relevance medium, public launch surface, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`distributed standing` means a future bounded body-side standing posture in which standing may be said to persist across selected plural carrier evidence under declared basis while source-body lineage remains preserved and no carrier becomes source, current, sovereign, authority, winner, loser, or body by itself.

`body-side distributed standing posture` means a posture recognized by the body line under declared distributed-standing basis. It is not held by a carrier, created by a carrier, transferred by a carrier, or decided by a registry.

`distributed-standing basis` means the declared distributed standing question, selected body-side standing posture, selected carrier evidence identities and outcomes, source-body lineage, receipt/refusal/admission basis where applicable, divergence basis, divergence consequence basis, currentness successor basis, carrier continuity-turn basis, standing propagation basis, registry/persistence basis, lifecycle basis, relation/conformance/closure basis, current-body conformance v3 closure basis, visible refusal, visible divergence, blocked attempts, projection mismatch, detailed basis, summary projection, and explicit non-claims.

`distributed-standing claim` means a future bounded assertion that body-side standing posture persists across selected plural carrier evidence under declared basis and non-claims.

`distributed-standing refusal` means refusal to record distributed standing posture because required basis is missing, malformed, hidden, summary-overwriting, source-replacing, authority-creating, permission-creating, currentness-creating, carrier-sovereignty-shaped, synchronization-shaped, full-body-transfer-shaped, continuation-shaped, distributed-operation-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`plural carrier standing evidence` means selected evidence from more than one carrier, carrier line, carrier role, receipt attempt, refusal, returned evidence, admitted evidence, divergence surface, currentness participation surface, lifecycle posture, registry/persistence reference, standing propagation posture, continuity-turn posture, relation posture, conformance posture, or closure posture that may be considered as prerequisite basis.

`carrier-held evidence` means evidence held, carried, received, refused, returned, emitted, admitted, or referenced by a carrier. Carrier-held evidence is not source, currentness, standing, authority, or body by location or possession.

`source-body lineage` means the preserved lineage of the body-side standing posture and its upstream source surfaces. Source-body lineage must remain distinguishable from carrier-held evidence and registry references.

`carrier non-sovereignty` means the required posture that no carrier becomes sovereign, source, current, authority, winner, loser, or body by receiving, holding, refusing, returning, emitting, registering, or carrying evidence.

`distributed-standing non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that distributed standing did not create carrier currentness, select a current carrier, select a winning carrier, invalidate a losing carrier, replace source, create authority, create permission, create truth, authorize action, resolve divergence, erase evidence, hide refusal, hide divergence, hide blocked attempts, hide projection mismatch, authorize synchronization, authorize full body transfer, create a second body, authorize continuation, authorize distributed operation, or perform mutation/replay/merge.

`distributed-standing prerequisite` means a required basis surface or posture that may be cited before distributed standing could ever be reviewed. A prerequisite is not distributed standing by itself.

Distributed standing is not a carrier state.

Distributed standing is not registry membership.

Distributed standing is not sync.

Distributed standing is not body transfer.

Distributed standing is not currentness.
