# Carrier Continuity-Turn Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for carrier continuity-turn posture in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

The purpose is to prevent carrier-related work from becoming a flat list of artifacts where summaries, latest records, registry entries, or shallow projections override detailed basis, lineage, refusal, divergence, closure, conformance, lifecycle posture, standing propagation posture, or successor relation.

Continuity is not a flat list of records.

Continuity is not latest-file currentness.

Continuity is not summary projection.

Continuity is not registry presence.

Continuity is not distributed standing.

Carrier continuity-turn posture records how carrier-related artifacts preserve lawful relation across turns.

A turn is not authority.

A pass is not currentness.

A successor is not overwrite.

A summary is not the artifact body.

A projection mismatch is not erased by a successor.

Core rule: carrier continuity-turn posture preserves how carrier-related artifacts stand in sequence, including declarations, attempts, refusals, returns, admissions, divergence, currentness participation, relation, conformance, closure, lifecycle, registry/persistence reference, standing propagation, and successor projection. It does not create currentness, authority, permission, source replacement, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, distributed operation, or automatic successor pressure.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, generic continuity subsystem, memory subsystem, carrier registry implementation, persistence implementation, standing propagation implementation, cross-carrier currentness successor law, distributed standing, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, network topology, consensus system, leader election, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`carrier continuity-turn` means the bounded posture that situates one carrier-related artifact, pass, attempt, closure, or successor projection in lawful relation to other carrier-related artifacts without creating currentness, authority, permission, distributed standing, continuation, or overwrite.

`carrier turn` means one bounded carrier-related artifact or event-like posture in the present execution line, such as an experiment declaration, receipt attempt, returned evidence, admission as evidence, divergence recording, currentness participation, relation recognition, conformance, closure, lifecycle status, registry/persistence reference, standing propagation record, or projection successor.

`carrier pass` means one bounded execution or review pass that produces a carrier-related artifact. A pass may preserve checks and outcome. A pass is not currentness.

`turn basis` means the declared question, selected artifact identity, outcome where applicable, turn kind, selected carrier identities where applicable, predecessor/successor relation where applicable, detailed basis, summary projection, visible refusal, visible divergence, projection correspondence, and explicit non-claims that allow a continuity-turn posture to be recorded.

`pass basis` means the selected execution or review basis for one pass, including the inputs, checks, outcome family, and non-claims preserved by the pass. Pass basis is not authority by execution.

`predecessor artifact` means an earlier standing artifact selected as the artifact that a later artifact succeeds, closes, conforms, corrects, or references. Predecessor artifact does not become obsolete merely because a successor exists.

`successor artifact` means a later artifact that declares a bounded relation to a predecessor artifact. A successor artifact is additive and does not mutate, erase, or replace the predecessor.

`successor-by-projection` means a successor artifact or implementation created because the predecessor preserved detailed basis but projected some summary or statement field insufficiently. It corrects future projection without editing the predecessor.

`successor-by-closure` means a successor or later closure artifact that records bounded closure of meaning after prior checks, relation, or conformance. Closure is not continuation.

`successor-by-conformance` means a successor or later artifact that records that a selected prior relation, posture, or body line conforms under bounded checks. Conformance is not permission.

`blocked turn` means a turn whose requested posture, pass, or artifact production was blocked under a bounded reason. A blocked turn may remain part of continuity as visible evidence of refusal or failure.

`refusal turn` means a turn preserving refusal, block, non-admission, non-receipt, or basis failure. Refusal turn does not invalidate the body and does not erase successful evidence elsewhere.

`projection mismatch` means detailed basis and summary projection disagree, or a summary under-reports a posture preserved in the detailed artifact body. Projection mismatch is a preserved condition, not permission to rewrite.

`detailed basis` means the artifact body section, nested basis, checks, raw basis, or referenced evidence in which the fuller posture is preserved.

`summary projection` means a compressed statement, summary, report field, or index view derived from detailed basis. Summary projection must not override detailed basis.

`turn lineage` means the preserved relation across turns, including predecessor artifact, successor artifact, successor reason, closure relation, conformance relation, blocked attempt, and projection correction where applicable.

`turn non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that the turn did not create currentness, source replacement, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, distributed operation, evidence erasure, hidden refusal, hidden divergence, hidden projection mismatch, predecessor mutation, summary authority, latest-turn currentness, mutation, replay, or merge.

A turn does not mean permission.

A successor does not erase predecessor.

A projection correction does not mutate the corrected artifact.

A summary projection does not override detailed basis.
