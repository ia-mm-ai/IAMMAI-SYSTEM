# Receiver-Side Answerable Basis Receiver Attestation Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines one minimum boundary for determining whether receiver-attestation consideration may be allowed for the exact selected receiver-side answerable-basis candidate after that candidate has lawfully reached `RECEIVER_SIDE_ANSWERABLE_BASIS_CANDIDATE_SUFFICIENT`.

It defines consideration only. It does not create, establish, support, admit, validate, or record receiver attestation. It does not execute a receiver-attestation operation or preselect a later attestation result.

Candidate sufficient is not receiver attestation. Captured material is not receiver attestation. Receiver-attestation consideration allowed is not receiver attestation. A boundary result is not operation execution.

## 10. Decision Rules

Receiver-attestation consideration may be allowed only when the exact selected upstream artifact and identities match; every required upstream field in Sections 5 and 6 is valid; the selected candidate is sufficient; the operation is completed and exhausted; bounded material is referenced without conversion into attestation or standing; all required false postures remain false; and no prohibited downstream or repeated-use request is present.

Receiver-attestation consideration must be not allowed when required upstream standing is valid but a bounded request lawfully declines consideration or does not select bounded material for this consideration. Not allowed is not candidate insufficiency, receiver refusal, invalid evidence, or failed attestation.

The boundary must block when the required upstream artifact is missing, unreadable, malformed, identity-mismatched, version-mismatched, failed, incomplete, unexhausted, not recorded, or does not record the exact sufficient result. It must also block result preclaims, direct attestation creation, receipt or presence conversion, identity or standing conversion, repeated-use posture, downstream authorization, repair, scan, discovery, or contaminated-lineage validation.
