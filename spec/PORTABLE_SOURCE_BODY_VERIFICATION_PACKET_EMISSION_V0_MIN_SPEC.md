## 4. Definitions

`packet_transfer` means movement or copying to another carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`source_transfer` means transfer of source standing or source material. It does not occur by this spec.

`source_receipt` means receipt of source standing or source material. It does not occur by this spec.
