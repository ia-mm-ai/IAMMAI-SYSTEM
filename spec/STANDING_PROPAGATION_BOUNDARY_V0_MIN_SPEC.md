# Standing Propagation Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for standing propagation posture in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

The purpose is to prevent a standing surface, standing result, standing closure, carried standing-related packet, standing-related receipt evidence, standing-related admission evidence, standing-related relation evidence, standing-related lifecycle evidence, or standing-related registry reference from becoming distributed standing, currentness, authority, permission, source replacement, carrier hierarchy, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, continuation, distributed operation, or operational command.

Standing propagation is not distributed standing.

Carried standing is not currentness.

Received standing evidence is not standing-on-receiver.

Returned standing evidence is not admission.

Admitted standing evidence is not source.

Registered standing reference is not standing.

Standing may be carried as evidence or reference before it can ever stand across carriers.

Core rule: standing propagation may preserve a standing surface or standing-related artifact as carried evidence, returned evidence, admitted evidence, lifecycle evidence, relation evidence, currentness-participation evidence, or registry/persistence reference. It may not create distributed standing, currentness, source replacement, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, distributed operation, or automatic standing across carriers.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, distributed standing surface, standing propagation implementation, carrier registry implementation, persistence implementation, repository synchronization layer, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, carrier fleet management, network topology, consensus system, leader election, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

This file does not implement standing propagation.

This file does not authorize standing propagation execution.

This file does not create distributed standing.

This file does not authorize repository synchronization or full body transfer.

## 4. Definitions

`standing propagation` means the bounded description of how one standing surface, standing result, standing closure, carried standing-related packet, or standing-related artifact is preserved when carried through, received by, refused by, returned from, admitted from, related across, lifecycle-recorded, or registry-referenced through carriers.

`source standing surface` means the upstream standing surface, standing result, standing closure, or standing-related artifact selected as the source standing basis for a propagation question. Source standing surface does not move merely because it is carried, received, admitted, related, lifecycle-recorded, or registry-referenced.

`carried standing surface` means a selected standing surface or standing-related artifact carried across a carrier boundary as evidence or reference. It remains carried posture unless a separate future boundary says otherwise.

`carried standing evidence` means evidence that a standing surface or standing-related artifact was packaged, transferred, received, refused, blocked, returned, admitted, related, lifecycle-recorded, or registry-referenced. Carried standing evidence is not distributed standing.

`received standing evidence` means evidence that a receiving carrier recorded receipt of a carried standing surface or packet under bounded receipt basis. Received standing evidence is not standing-on-receiver.

`refused standing evidence` means evidence that a carrier refused, blocked, or could not lawfully receive a carried standing surface or packet under visible basis. Refused standing evidence must remain visible and is not erased by another carrier's receipt.

`returned standing evidence` means standing-related receipt or refusal evidence returned from a carrier to the source carrier context or body-line context. Return does not mean admission.

`admitted standing evidence` means standing-related carrier-local or returned evidence admitted into the body line as evidence under a separate carrier-local emission admission boundary. Admission does not make the carried standing source, current, or distributed.

`standing reference` means a registry/persistence, lifecycle, relation, divergence, currentness-participation, receipt, admission, or integrity reference to standing-related evidence. A standing reference is not standing by being listed.

`standing propagation basis` means the declared standing propagation question, selected standing surface or standing-related artifact identity, source standing basis where applicable, source carrier where applicable, receiving carrier where applicable, carried surface or packet basis where applicable, receipt/refusal/return/admission posture where applicable, divergence/currentness/relation/lifecycle/registry posture where applicable, preserved lineage, visible refusal, visible divergence, visible corruption or staleness where applicable, and explicit non-claims.

`standing propagation claim` means the bounded assertion that one standing surface or standing-related artifact has one recorded propagation posture under declared basis.

`standing propagation refusal` means refusal to record propagation posture because the request is missing, malformed, unsupported, lineage-breaking, evidence-erasing, hidden-refusal-shaped, hidden-divergence-shaped, source-replacing, currentness-creating, authority-creating, permission-creating, hierarchy-creating, distributed-standing-shaped, synchronization-shaped, transfer-shaped, continuation-authorizing, distributed-operation-authorizing, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`standing propagation non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that propagation did not create source, currentness, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, distributed operation, evidence erasure, hidden refusal, hidden divergence, hidden corruption, hidden staleness, repair by overwrite, registry-standing, receipt-standing, admission-standing, mutation, replay, or merge.

`standing propagation posture` means one declared bounded status from the minimal posture set in this specification.

Standing propagation posture does not mean distributed standing.

Carried standing surface does not mean standing on the receiving carrier.

Standing reference does not mean standing.

Standing propagation participant does not mean body participant.

## 6. What Standing Propagation Means

Standing propagation posture may mean:

- a standing surface or standing-related artifact was carried to a carrier
- a standing surface or standing-related packet remained source-standing-preserved upstream
- a carrier received standing-related evidence
- a carrier refused or blocked standing-related evidence
- a carrier returned standing-related evidence
- returned standing evidence was admitted as evidence under a separate admission basis
- divergence involving standing-related evidence was recorded
- currentness participation involving standing-related evidence was bounded as eligible or excluded
- relation involving standing-related evidence was recognized
- relation conformance involving standing-related evidence passed under bounded checks
- relation closure recorded meaning only for standing-related relation evidence
- lifecycle status preserved standing-related evidence as lifecycle evidence
- registry/persistence preserved standing-related reference evidence
- standing-related evidence remains visible and lineage-preserved
- source standing remains upstream unless separately bounded otherwise
- future reliance may be permitted, excluded, or blocked only under declared basis

Standing propagation records evidence posture over standing-related material.

It does not convert that posture into force.

## 25. Closing Boundary Statement

Standing propagation may record how a standing surface or standing-related artifact is carried, received, refused, returned, admitted, related, lifecycle-recorded, or registry-referenced across carriers. It preserves lineage and evidence posture while refusing source replacement, currentness, authority, hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, or distributed operation.

It closes the question:

`What posture does standing-related evidence have when carried across carriers?`

It does not open the question:

`Does the body now stand across carriers?`
