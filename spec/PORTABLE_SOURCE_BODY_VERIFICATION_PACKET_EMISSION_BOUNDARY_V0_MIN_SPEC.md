## 4. Definitions

`packet_emission_boundary` means a boundary over local packet-artifact basis for one future packet-emission step. It is not packet emission.

`packet_emission` means future local or outward emission of the packet, if separately resolved and recorded.

`emitted_packet` means a future emitted packet artifact, if separately resolved and recorded.

`packet_transfer` means future movement or copying to another carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`copy_to_another_device` means future copying to a distinct carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`source_transfer` means a future source-transfer act. It does not occur by this spec.

`source_receipt` means a future source-receipt act. It does not occur by this spec.

`reception_authorization` means a future authorization for reception. It does not occur by this spec.

`second_carrier_receipt` means receipt by a distinct device, environment, or carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`second_carrier_execution` means execution by a distinct device, environment, or carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`external_result_artifact` means a future result artifact produced outside the current carrier. It does not exist by this spec.

`cross_carrier_evidence_review` means future review of evidence across carriers. It does not occur by this spec.

`packet_artifact_basis` means the clean local packet artifact and terminal summary.

`emission_basis` means future selected basis for packet emission.

`emission_readiness` means a future readiness posture. It is not emission authorization and does not occur unless separately recorded.

`emission_authorization` means a future authorization posture. It is not transfer authorization and does not occur by this spec.

`emission_as_transfer` is blocked.

`emission_as_source_transfer` is blocked.

`emission_as_source_receipt` is blocked.

`emission_as_cross_carrier_proof` is blocked.

`emission_as_runtime` is blocked.

`hidden_repo_state` means any dependency not declared in the packet artifact or emission basis.

`repo_local_availability` means a file exists locally. It is not packet-emission authority.

`selected_basis_reference_shape` means emission-boundary selected basis remains bounded and reference-shaped unless later emission result explicitly admits bounded emitted material.

`raw_full_prior_artifact_body` means a full prior artifact body. It must not be embedded or returned unless a later emission result explicitly admits bounded copyable material.

`packet_emission_boundary_non_claim` means explicit false posture preventing emission boundary from becoming emission, transfer, receipt, second-carrier execution, cross-carrier proof, source, authority, currentness, runtime, final completion, continuation, reusable permission, or follow-on work.
