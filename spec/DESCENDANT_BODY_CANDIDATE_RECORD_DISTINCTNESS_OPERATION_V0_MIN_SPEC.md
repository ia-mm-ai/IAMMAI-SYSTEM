## 4. Operation Question

## 5. Definitions

`candidate-record distinctness operation` means a future bounded operation, not performed here, that may compare exactly two declared non-standing candidate records from the completed descendant-body differentiation operation result.

`declared candidate record` means one of the two result-contained non-standing candidate records emitted by the completed descendant-body differentiation operation result.

`distinctness result` means a future emitted result object from a separately implemented resolver.

`distinctness evidence` means candidate-specific evidence used to support inspectable difference between candidate records beyond candidate_record_id and candidate_role.

`candidate-specific content` means operation-declared content used to compare records beyond id, role, ordering, filename, display name, or shared evidence reference.

`separate seal material` means candidate-specific seal material that differs between the two candidate records and is not copied, shared, or identical.

`separate lineage receipt material` means candidate-specific lineage receipt material that differs between the two candidate records and is not copied, shared, or identical.

`separate digest material` means candidate-specific digest or hash material computed from candidate-specific content.

`DISTINCTNESS_SUPPORTED` means a future result where distinctness evidence supports inspectable difference between the two candidate records beyond id and role.

`NOT_DISTINCT` means a future result where distinctness evidence is absent, duplicated, shared, cosmetic only, identical, insufficient, or fails candidate-specific comparison.

`cosmetic distinction` means difference limited to candidate_record_id, candidate_role, id suffix, role label, filename, display name, ordering, list position, or assigned label.

`contaminated lineage` means `spec/DESCENDANT_BODY_BASIS_DERIVATION_EVENT_V0_MIN_SPEC.md` preserved unchanged for the unsupported existence-claim class.

`operation spec` means this operation specification only, not operation execution.
