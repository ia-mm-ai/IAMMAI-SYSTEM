## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_conformance_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether the recorded source-body reception boundary chain conforms to its own prior recorded constraints. It is conformance only.

`selected_receipt_exhaustion_result` means one source-body reception receipt / exhaustion result whose outcome is `SOURCE_BODY_RECEPTION_RECEIPT_EXHAUSTION_RECORDED`, whose failed check count is zero, and whose receipt / exhaustion remained recognition-record accounting only.

`reception_family_boundary_chain` means the recorded chain consisting of request declaration, identity preservation, receiving-context role, eligibility / admissibility, non-capture / non-adoption / non-currentness, recognition, and receipt / exhaustion for one source-body reception request.

`reception_boundary_chain_conformance` means the bounded determination that the recorded reception-family boundary chain preserved its own prior constraints and non-claims.

`source_body_reception_conformance` means chain conformance of the recorded source-body reception boundary family. It is not source receipt, reception authorization, adoption, authority, currentness, public readiness, final completion, or closure.

`conformance_recorded` means the conformance boundary has recorded that the chain conformance question has been reviewed and passed. It does not mean closure.

`conformance_not_closure` means conformance does not close the reception chain, record closure, or claim that reception closure has passed.

`conformance_not_source_receipt` means conformance does not receive source, record source receipt, create source receipt, or treat receipt / exhaustion of recognition accounting as source receipt.

`conformance_not_authorization` means conformance does not authorize reception, source receipt, operation, adoption, validation, invalidation, publication, continuation, or follow-on work.

`conformance_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that conformance did not authorize reception, receive source, record source receipt, create source receipt, create governance, make receiving context source/authority/current/receiver/adopter/validator/invalidator/operator, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, standing propagation, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, closure, mutation, replay, or merge.

`chain_overread_risk` means the risk that a recorded chain is treated as more than the sum of its bounded boundary results.

`conformance_by_existence_risk` means the risk that the mere existence of recorded artifacts is treated as proof of conformance without a dedicated conformance boundary.

`closure_overread` means treating conformance as reception closure or proof that reception closure has been recorded.

`completion_overread` means treating conformance as final completion, public readiness, operation permission, standing completion, or follow-on authorization.

Conformance means the recorded reception-family boundary chain is checked against its own prior constraints.

Conformance does not mean reception is authorized.

Conformance does not mean source was received.
