## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_identity_preservation_boundary` means a bounded review surface that preserves the identity of one selected source-body surface for one declared reception request. It is not source-body reception, reception recognition, reception authorization, source receipt, or final source-body identity.

`selected_source_body_surface` means the selected surface proposed in the declared reception request. It remains the selected surface for that request only. It does not become received source, adopted source, current source, authority, standing, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, or whole-body identity by preservation.

`selected_source_body_surface_identity` means the declared identifier, type, path or reference, identity basis, and lineage basis that distinguish the selected source-body surface from receiving context, closure artifacts, terminal summaries, declaration artifacts, latest files, carrier possession, registry references, and narrative convenience.

`source_body_identity_preservation` means preserving the selected source-body surface identity for later reception-family review without defining final source-body identity, whole-body identity, currentness, authority, adoption, or permission.

`source_body_lineage_preservation` means preserving the declared lineage basis for the selected source-body surface without replacing source lineage with receiving context, latest artifact posture, registry convenience, carrier possession, or terminal-summary context.

`receiving_context_non_source_posture` means the receiving context remains context only and is not source, authority, current, validation authority, invalidation authority, standing, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, publication flow, or follow-on authorization.

`source_replacement` means treating any non-source surface as replacing the selected source-body surface, including receiving context, distributed-operation closure artifact, terminal summary, request declaration artifact, latest file, latest turn, registry reference, carrier possession, or narrative convenience.

`source_validation_by_receiving_context` means treating the receiving context as validating the selected source-body surface. Identity preservation does not validate source by receiving context.

`source_invalidation_by_receiving_context` means treating the receiving context as invalidating the selected source-body surface. Identity preservation does not invalidate source by receiving context.

`latest_artifact_capture` means treating the latest visible artifact, latest file, latest turn, latest result, or latest summary as source by recency. Identity preservation blocks this capture.

`terminal_summary_capture` means treating the distributed-operation terminal summary as the selected source-body surface or as source replacement. The terminal summary may be context/evidence only where supplied.

`distributed_operation_closure_capture` means treating distributed-operation closure artifact as the selected source-body surface or as source replacement. Distributed-operation closure may be context/evidence only where supplied.

`carrier_possession_capture` means treating carrier possession, carrier visibility, carrier access, carrier custody, or carrier convenience as source identity, authority, currentness, or replacement. Identity preservation blocks this capture.

`registry_reference_capture` means treating registry presence, reference location, index entry, path convenience, or lookup success as source replacement or final identity. A registry/reference may identify a surface but does not replace it.

`whole_body_identity_inflation` means treating one selected source-body surface as the whole body, final source-body identity, currentness of all source-body surfaces, or complete body identity by default. Identity preservation blocks this inflation.

`identity_preservation_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that identity preservation did not recognize reception, authorize reception, receive source, define final source-body identity, inflate selected surface into whole body, replace source, validate source, invalidate source, convert receiving context into source/authority/current, convert closure or terminal summary into source, treat latest artifact or carrier possession as source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, mutation, replay, or merge.

Selected source-body surface means the selected surface proposed in the declared reception request.

Selected source-body surface may be a source-body surface for this declared request without becoming the whole body.

Identity preservation does not define final source-body identity.

Identity preservation does not create currentness.

Receiving context remains context only.

Distributed-operation closure artifact is context/evidence only where supplied.

Terminal summary is context/evidence only where supplied.

Declaration artifact is context/evidence only and does not replace source.
