## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_closure_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether the recorded and conformed source-body reception boundary chain may be closed as this bounded reception family only. It is closure only.

`selected_conformance_result` means one source-body reception conformance result whose outcome is `SOURCE_BODY_RECEPTION_CONFORMANCE_PASSED`, whose failed check count is zero, and whose conformance remained reception-chain conformance only.

`reception_family_closure` means closure of the recorded source-body reception boundary family for one declared source-body reception request. It is not source receipt, reception authorization, final completion, reusable permission, or follow-on authority.

`bounded_chain_closure` means sealing this specific recorded and conformed chain against further interpretation as permission, receipt, adoption, currentness, operation, publication, continuation, reusable permission, successor reception, or final completion.

`closure_recorded` means the closure boundary has recorded that the bounded source-body reception chain may be closed. It does not mean reception was authorized, source was received, source receipt was recorded, final completion was claimed, or follow-on work was authorized.

`closure_passed` means the closure checks passed for this bounded chain only. It does not mean source-body reception became operative or reusable.

`closure_not_final_completion` means closure does not claim final completion, public readiness, operation readiness, continuity completion, or system completion.

`closure_not_source_receipt` means closure does not receive source, record source receipt, create source receipt, or treat receipt / exhaustion of recognition accounting as source receipt.

`closure_not_authorization` means closure does not authorize reception, source receipt, adoption, validation, invalidation, operation, publication, continuation, another reception request, reusable permission, or follow-on work.

`closure_not_reusable_permission` means closure does not create a general reusable permission, a repeatable reception lane, or authority to treat future source-body reception requests as already allowed.

`closure_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that closure did not authorize reception, receive source, record source receipt, create source receipt, create governance, make receiving context source/authority/current/receiver/adopter/validator/invalidator/operator, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, standing propagation, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, reusable permission, another reception request, mutation, replay, or merge.

`closure_overread` means treating closure of this bounded chain as more than chain closure.

`completion_overread` means treating closure as final completion, public readiness, operation permission, standing completion, continuity completion, or system completion.

`continuation_overread` means treating closure as authorization to continue work, create a successor, schedule a future boundary, or open publication flow.

`reusable_permission_overread` means treating closure as reusable reception permission, successor reception authority, or permission for another reception request.

Closure means this recorded and conformed source-body reception boundary chain is sealed.

Closure does not mean reception is authorized.

Closure does not mean source was received.

Closure does not mean source receipt was recorded.

Closure does not mean adoption, authority, currentness, operation permission, public readiness, or final completion.

Closure does not authorize continuation or follow-on work.

Closure does not create permission to repeat the reception request.

Closure does not create general reusable reception permission.

## 20. Closing Boundary Statement

Source-body reception closure boundary may close the recorded and conformed source-body reception boundary chain. It preserves that this closure does not authorize reception, receive source, record source receipt, create source receipt, adopt source, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create authority, create currentness, create operation permission, create public readiness, claim final completion, authorize continuation, open publication flow, authorize follow-on work, create reusable permission, or authorize another reception request. Closure seals only this bounded source-body reception boundary family.

It closes the question:

`Can this conformed source-body reception boundary chain be closed?`

It does not open the question:

`Was the source received, authorized, adopted, current, complete, public-ready, operative, reusable, or permission-bearing now?`
