# Additional Physical Carrier Experiment Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for one additional physical receiving-carrier experiment in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

The purpose is to prevent "the body is now ready for another device" from becoming distributed standing, currentness, carrier hierarchy, repository synchronization, carrier registry, full body transfer, second-body creation, continuation, or unbounded carrier expansion.

The additional physical carrier experiment is a bounded declaration surface.

It may authorize one additional physical carrier to act as receiving carrier for one selected carried surface or packet.

It may authorize only receipt/refusal behavior on the additional carrier.

It may not authorize:

- copying the full repository as body transfer
- syncing folders
- running self-orientation on the additional carrier
- running current-body conformance on the additional carrier
- running relation, conformance, or closure on the additional carrier
- creating currentness
- creating distributed standing
- treating success as permission to expand

Core rule: an additional physical carrier experiment may be declared only as one bounded receipt/refusal experiment over one selected carried surface or packet. It does not authorize repository sync, body transfer, self-orientation on the additional carrier, conformance on the additional carrier, distributed standing, currentness, carrier hierarchy, or continuation by success.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, distributed standing surface, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization layer, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, multi-carrier lifecycle, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`additional physical carrier experiment` means one bounded declaration that permits one additional physical carrier to attempt receipt or refusal of one selected carried surface or packet under explicit basis and non-claims.

`source carrier for experiment` means the carrier from which the selected carried surface or packet is prepared or emitted for this experiment only. This role does not create universal source authority.

`additional receiving carrier` means the additional physical carrier selected to receive or refuse one carried surface or packet for this experiment. Additional receiving carrier does not mean body participant.

`selected carried surface or packet` means one selected standing surface, carried surface, or bounded packet chosen for the experiment, including its identity, basis, outcome or status where applicable, and integrity basis where applicable.

`experiment packet` means the bounded packet or surface prepared for transfer to the additional receiving carrier. It must preserve identity, source carrier role, receiving carrier role, purpose, integrity basis where applicable, permitted operations, prohibited operations, and non-claims.

`experiment receipt` means the additional receiving carrier records receipt of the selected carried surface or packet as carried evidence only.

`experiment refusal` means the additional receiving carrier refuses receipt or blocks the request under a visible reason, failed check, malformed basis, unsupported role, or other bounded refusal condition.

`experiment return` means receipt or refusal evidence is returned to the source carrier context or body-line context as returned evidence only. Return does not mean admission.

`experiment admission` means any later body-line admission of returned evidence under the carrier-local emission admission boundary. Admission is separate from this experiment declaration.

`experiment boundary` means this specification's limit on what one additional physical carrier experiment may declare, preserve, refuse, and explicitly not claim.

`experiment non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that the experiment did not create currentness, authority, permission beyond the declared experiment, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, or distributed operation.

`experiment refusal` also means refusal to declare the experiment when the requested declaration is malformed, insufficiently bounded, or collapse-shaped.

`experiment closure later, if required` means a future separate closure surface may record the meaning and non-meaning of an experiment result. This specification does not implement that closure.

Experiment success does not mean distributed standing.

Experiment declaration does not authorize continuation beyond the declared experiment.

## 9. Permitted Experiment Operations

- later divergence review only under the cross-carrier divergence boundary
- later currentness participation review only under the cross-carrier currentness boundary

These operations are limited to the declared experiment. They do not create standing permission for repeated operation.
