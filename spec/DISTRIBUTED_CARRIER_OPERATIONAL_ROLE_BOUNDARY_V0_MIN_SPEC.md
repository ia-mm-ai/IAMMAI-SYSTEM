# Distributed Carrier Operational Role Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the distributed carrier operational role boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

This boundary is the next lawful gate after source-body operational authority basis has been recorded for one eligible distributed operation matter.

The purpose of this boundary is to prevent source-body authority basis from becoming carrier participation, carrier authority, carrier currentness, carrier hierarchy, operation admission, operation authorization, operation execution, synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, consequence, or follow-on permission by implication.

This boundary reviews one eligible declared matter only.

This boundary may define and review possible carrier role shapes for future admission / transition review. It must not activate carrier roles. It must not assign carriers to live operation. It must not admit, authorize, execute, synchronize, transfer, continue, or produce consequence.

## 4. Definitions

`distributed carrier operational role boundary` means the bounded review surface that names possible carrier role shapes for one eligible distributed operation matter with source-body authority basis recorded, without activating any role or operation.

`selected eligible distributed operation matter` means the one declared matter that passed distributed operation eligibility and is carried into this role review through the selected source-body authority result.

`selected source-body authority basis` means the recorded source-body lineage and authority-reference basis that may support future admission / transition review. It remains basis only and is not an authority grant.

`carrier role shape` means a bounded descriptive posture for a carrier in relation to the selected matter, such as evidence, receipt, refusal, divergence, observation, or future-operation-requires-admission context.

`carrier role review question` means the declared question asking what carrier role shapes may be recognized as bounded context before any future operation admission.

`carrier role candidate` means a proposed carrier role shape that may be reviewed for support and forbidden inferences.

`carrier context role` means a non-operational role preserving a carrier as context, evidence, receipt, refusal, divergence, observation, or candidate context only.

`possible future operational role` means a role shape that cannot activate here and could only be inspected later by a separately declared and bounded admission / transition boundary.

`role activation` means converting a role shape into operational participation, operational force, authority, permission, currentness, hierarchy, or action. Role activation is not permitted here.

`role assignment` means assigning a carrier to a live operation or operational obligation. Role assignment is not permitted here.

`role refusal` means preserved evidence that a carrier refused, blocked, aborted, or could not take a role. It remains evidence/context only and must not be erased.

`role non-claim` means an explicit false posture preventing role review from implying activation, authority, permission, currentness, hierarchy, source replacement, operation, synchronization, transfer, continuation, consequence, final completion, public readiness, or follow-on work.

Role review does not activate roles.

Role review does not assign operation.

Role review does not create carrier authority.

Role review does not admit operation.

Role review only defines possible role shapes and forbidden inferences for a later admission / transition boundary.
