This file defines the bounded distributed operation closure boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

This is the next lawful gate after distributed operation conformance boundary.

The purpose is to close the recorded distributed operation boundary chain without claiming actual execution, actual receipt, executed-operation conformance, public readiness, final completion, reusable permission, autonomous continuation, or follow-on authority.

This boundary reviews one admitted operation context and its recorded boundary chain only.

Distributed operation closure boundary is not executed-operation closure.

Distributed operation closure boundary is not final system completion.

Distributed operation closure boundary is not public readiness.

Distributed operation closure boundary is not actual receipt.

Distributed operation closure boundary is not executed-operation conformance.

Distributed operation closure boundary only defines whether the recorded distributed operation boundary chain may be closed as a boundary chain.

This file is a closure boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, executed-operation closure, final system completion, public readiness, actual operation execution, actual output emission, actual operation receipt, executed-operation conformance, action authorization, consequence creation, repository synchronization, non-synchronized operation, full body transfer, second-body creation, current self-orientation v10, final governance, final continuity completion, final system identity, public launch surface, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`distributed_operation_closure_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether the recorded distributed operation boundary chain may be closed as a boundary chain only for one admitted operation context after boundary-chain conformance has been recorded. It does not claim executed-operation closure.

`selected_conformance_boundary_result` means one declared distributed operation conformance boundary result whose outcome is `DISTRIBUTED_OPERATION_CONFORMANCE_BOUNDARY_RECORDED`.

`boundary_chain_closure` means closure of the recorded distributed operation boundary chain as a boundary chain only. It is not closure of an executed operation, final system completion, public readiness, actual operation receipt, or follow-on authorization.

`executed_operation_closure` means closure of an operation that actually executed or was otherwise actually performed. This boundary does not claim executed-operation closure.

`closure_posture` means the declared posture for checking boundary-chain conformance basis, closure dependency, non-claim preservation, overread prevention, and bounded scope before boundary-chain closure is recorded.

`closure_refusal` means refusal to record boundary-chain closure when required basis is missing, malformed, not closed, overread, execution-shaped, receipt-shaped, conformance-inflating, public-readiness-shaped, final-completion-shaped, reusable-permission-shaped, continuation-shaped, mutation-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`closure_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that this boundary did not claim executed-operation closure, executed-operation conformance, actual operation receipt, public readiness, final completion, reusable permission, autonomous continuation, action, consequence, execution, emission, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, or follow-on work.

`closure_dependency_satisfied_for_boundary_chain` means the conformance-recorded chain has enough bounded basis to close the boundary chain while preserving that no executed operation, actual receipt, executed-operation conformance, public readiness, final completion, reusable permission, autonomous continuation, or follow-on work is created.

`final_completion` means any claim that the distributed operation, final governance, final continuity, final system identity, public launch readiness, or future-work posture has been finally completed. Boundary-chain closure does not claim final completion.

`public_readiness` means any claim that the system, operation, output, artifact, or standing surface is ready for public launch or public reliance. Boundary-chain closure does not create public readiness.

`closure_without_execution` means closure of the recorded boundary chain while preserving that operation execution did not occur.

`closure_without_consequence` means closure of the recorded boundary chain while preserving that no action was authorized and no consequence was created.

`closure_without_reusable_permission` means closure of the recorded boundary chain while preserving that no reusable permission remains or is created.

`closure_without_follow_on_authority` means closure of the recorded boundary chain while preserving that no follow-on work, successor, continuation, synchronization, non-synchronized operation, full body transfer, or second body is authorized.

Boundary-chain closure does not mean executed-operation closure.

Boundary-chain closure does not mean final system completion.

Boundary-chain closure does not mean public readiness.

Boundary-chain closure does not mean the operation executed.

Boundary-chain closure only closes the recorded distributed operation boundary chain.
