## 5. Definitions

`candidate-specific distinctness basis emission boundary` means this boundary only, not emission.

`candidate-specific distinctness basis` means prospective non-standing material that may be emitted later to support candidate-record distinctness only if separately bounded and recorded.

`non-cosmetic scope division` means a declared split of a parent basis into two bounded sub-scopes whose content differs by mandate, function, responsibility, or governed surface, not by candidate id, role, label, side, order, filename, or template slot.

`cosmetic substitution` means text or data that differs only by candidate id, candidate role, label, side, order, filename, display name, variable name, assigned name, template slot, or equivalent string replacement.

`digest laundering` means computing a digest, hash, seal, or receipt over cosmetically substituted material and treating the resulting different values as distinctness evidence.

`admissible future evidence class` means candidate-specific content, separate seal material, separate lineage receipt material, or separate digest material that is derived from non-cosmetic scope division.

`inadmissible future evidence class` means any candidate-specific content, seal, lineage receipt, or digest material derived only from cosmetic substitution, label difference, id difference, role difference, order difference, filename difference, shared evidence reference, operation evidence alone, or digest laundering.

`scope-division route` means the only admissible route for the immediate next repo-local candidate-specific distinctness basis emission operation.

`divergent receipt-history route` means a possible later route that is open but not authorized here.

`carrier separation route` means a possible later route that is open but not authorized here.

This boundary is permission to define a future operation shape only, not permission to emit evidence.

## 6. Boundary Thesis

Digest difference is not distinctness unless the digested material carries non-cosmetic candidate-specific basis.

Hash difference is not distinctness unless the hashed material carries non-cosmetic candidate-specific basis.

Seal difference is not distinctness unless the sealed material carries non-cosmetic candidate-specific basis.

Lineage receipt difference is not distinctness unless the receipt material carries non-cosmetic candidate-specific basis.

Candidate-specific content is not candidate-specific merely because it embeds candidate id, candidate role, label, side, order, filename, template slot, or assigned name.

Candidate-specific distinctness basis must be basis-bearing, not label-bearing.
