# Carrier-Local Emission Admission Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for admitting carrier-local emission into the body line as evidence.

Admission is not emission.

Admission is not return.

Admission is not possession.

Admission is not receipt.

Admission is not correspondence.

Admission is not self-orientation recognition.

Admission is not currentness.

The purpose is to prevent "this carrier emitted something" or "this returned artifact exists in the body repository" from becoming:

- source replacement
- currentness creation
- authority
- permission
- successor standing
- body formation
- multi-carrier law
- distributed standing
- presence
- threshold
- truth
- action
- consequence
- continuation

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, registry, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

The carrier role and emission boundary defines local carrier roles and the emission classes permitted under those roles.

Cross-carrier surface receipt proves that one carried surface can be received by a physical carrier as carried evidence.

Returned Carrier B receipt evidence and returned Carrier B refusal evidence have been preserved and recognized downstream.

Cross-surface correspondence has preserved bounded reading relation over returned receipt evidence and refusal-visible evidence.

Current self-orientation v7 mirrors the returned carrier evidence as downstream posture.

Current-body conformance v2 confirms that the v7-integrated posture coheres as one current body line.

However, carrier-local emission still does not self-admit into the body line.

Return is not admission.

Possession is not admission.

Receipt is not admission.

Correspondence is not admission.

Self-orientation recognition is not admission by itself.

Therefore a boundary is needed for when a returned or otherwise available carrier-local emission may be admitted as body-line evidence.

This boundary is still narrower than multi-carrier law. It does not define carrier relation, distributed standing, cross-carrier currentness, carrier registry, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, or successor carrier law.

## 4. Definitions

`carrier-local emission` means an operation-local output produced by a carrier under a declared carrier role. It remains local unless separately admitted as body-line evidence.

`returned emission` means a carrier-local emission has been brought back or made available to the body line. Returned does not mean admitted.

`body-line admission` means a body-side surface recognizes one carrier-local or returned emission as evidence under declared basis and preserved non-claims.

`admitted evidence` means a carrier-local or returned emission has been recognized by an admission surface as downstream body-line evidence. It does not become source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, or body.

`admission surface` means the bounded body-side surface that performs admission. It records the admission basis, checks, non-claims, outcome, and block posture where applicable.

`admission basis` means the declared carrier operation, carrier role, emission class, emitting carrier identity, emission identity, emission outcome, return basis where available, integrity evidence where applicable, admission purpose, and explicit non-claims.

`emission source basis` means the packet, carried surface, receipt basis, refusal basis, manifest, integrity evidence, or other bounded basis from which the carrier-local emission derives.

`return path` means the preserved path or context by which a carrier-local emission was brought back or made available to the body line. Return path may be absent where not applicable, but it must not be invented.

`admitted emission posture` means the emission is preserved as downstream evidence only. It is not governing, current, authoritative, permission-granting, successor-forming, body-forming, or continuation-authorizing.

`admission refusal` means a requested admission is refused because required basis is missing, malformed, unsupported, contradictory, or collapse-shaped.

`non-admission` means the carrier-local emission remains local or returned-but-not-admitted. Non-admission does not invalidate the source surface, the carrier, the receipt, the refusal, or other evidence.

## 5. Emission / Return / Admission Distinction

The boundary preserves three separate states:

### A. `EMITTED_LOCAL`

`EMITTED_LOCAL` means a carrier produced an operation-local output under a declared role.

It is not body-line evidence yet.

It does not create source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, or continuation.

### B. `RETURNED_TO_BODY_LINE`

`RETURNED_TO_BODY_LINE` means an emission was brought back or made available to the body line.

It is still not admitted by default.

Returned evidence may be preserved without becoming source or currentness.

Returned evidence may be refused without invalidating the source surface.

### C. `ADMITTED_AS_EVIDENCE`

`ADMITTED_AS_EVIDENCE` means a body-side admission surface recognized the returned or local emission as evidence under declared basis and explicit non-claims.

Admitted evidence remains downstream.

Admitted evidence does not become source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, signal by default, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The boundary also preserves:

- `NOT_ADMITTED`: the emission remains local or returned without admission.
- `ADMISSION_BLOCKED`: the requested admission is refused under a bounded block reason.

## 6. What Admission Means

Carrier-local emission admission means:

- one carrier-local emission has been admitted as body-line evidence
- the emitting carrier role is preserved
- the emitting carrier identity is preserved
- the emission class is preserved
- the emission identity is preserved
- the emission outcome or status is preserved
- the emission source or carried basis is preserved where available
- the return path or return context is preserved where available
- integrity evidence is preserved where applicable
- visible refusal evidence remains visible where applicable
- successful receipt evidence does not erase blocked receipt evidence
- admission remains downstream
- non-claims remain explicit and false
- later assessment may use the admitted evidence only if a later bounded surface separately permits that use

Admission records evidentiary availability. It does not convert evidence into force.

## 12. Relation to Carrier Role and Emission Boundary

The carrier role and emission boundary defines what a carrier may locally be and emit under a declared operation-local role.

This specification defines when such local emission may be admitted as body-line evidence.

Role recognition does not create admission.

Emission recognition does not create admission.

Admission requires separate basis and explicit non-claims.

Carrier role remains operation-local after admission.

Carrier emission remains downstream evidence after admission.

Admission does not create carrier relation, carrier registry, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, cross-carrier currentness, successor carrier law, or body formation.

## 13. Relation to Cross-Carrier Surface Receipt

Receipt is a carrier-local or returned emission pattern.

Cross-carrier surface receipt proves that one carried surface was received as carried evidence under the receipt boundary.

Receipt does not admit itself into the body line.

A receipt may later be admitted as evidence only under this boundary or a successor boundary that preserves the same non-collapse posture.

A blocked receipt may also be admitted as visible refusal evidence.

Successful receipt does not erase blocked receipt.

Blocked receipt does not invalidate the source surface or another carrier's later successful receipt by itself.

Receipt admission does not make the receiving carrier source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, standing participant, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

## 14. Relation to Cross-Surface Correspondence

Correspondence may compare admitted or selected surfaces under a declared correspondence question.

Correspondence does not admit carrier-local emission by itself.

Admission may provide evidence later used in correspondence.

Correspondence may preserve refusal visibility or non-claim alignment.

Correspondence may preserve mismatch without hiding divergence.

Correspondence cannot decide currentness, source, authority, permission, or admission by itself.

Correspondence cannot convert admitted evidence into signal, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, continuation, multi-carrier law, or distributed standing.

## 15. Relation to Self-Orientation

Self-orientation may mirror admitted carrier evidence if current posture materially requires it.

Self-orientation does not admit carrier-local emission by itself.

Self-orientation recognition is not admission.

Self-orientation successor is not forced by admission alone.

Self-orientation should not become the metabolizer of every carrier-local emission.

Self-orientation must preserve admitted carrier evidence as downstream posture only and must not treat it as current, governing, authoritative, permission-granting, successor-forming, body-forming, or continuation-authorizing.

## 16. Relation to Current-Body Conformance

Conformance may later test whether admitted carrier evidence coheres with the current body line.

Conformance does not admit carrier-local emission by itself.

Conformance over admitted evidence cannot turn admitted evidence into source, currentness, permission, authority, successor standing, body formation, signal, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

Conformance over admitted evidence does not create carrier relation.

Conformance over admitted evidence does not create distributed standing.

## 17. Relation to Cross-Carrier Currentness

This specification does not define cross-carrier currentness.

Carrier-local emission does not create currentness.

Returned evidence does not create currentness.

Admitted evidence does not create currentness.

Successful admission count does not create currentness.

Latest returned artifact does not create currentness.

Currentness across carriers remains open and requires a separate boundary.

## 18. Relation to Multi-Carrier Law

This specification is not multi-carrier law.

This specification handles one or more carrier-local emissions entering the body line only as evidence.

Multi-carrier relation law requires separate pressure and basis.

Admission of carrier-local emissions does not authorize multiple carriers to stand in relation by default.

Admission of carrier-local emissions does not create distributed standing.

Admission of carrier-local emissions does not create carrier registry state, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, or cross-carrier currentness law.

## 23. Closing Boundary Statement

Carrier-local emission may enter the body line only through a bounded admission surface that records it as evidence while preserving carrier role, emission identity, emission outcome, return basis where available, integrity evidence where applicable, and non-claims.

It closes the question:

`When may a carrier-local emission be admitted into the body line as evidence?`

`Do multiple carriers now stand in relation?`
