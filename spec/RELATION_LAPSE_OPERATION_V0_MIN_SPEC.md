## 1. Purpose

This specification defines only the shape of a future relation lapse operation downstream of the completed relation lapse boundary.

It does not execute the operation, record an operation result, lapse or dissolve relation, reverse or terminate relation, erase or mutate `relation_001`, invalidate or punish relation, create teardown logic or living relation state, preserve historical receipt, authorize presence boundary, establish presence, create identity, create FIELD machinery, runtime, API, currentness, authority, standing, coupling, output, action, derivative reception, synchronization, or follow-on work.

It creates no resolver, test, live artifact, operation result, or downstream authorization. It performs no repository scan, file discovery, repair, unsupported-claim validation, or upstream override.

## 2. Scope

The sole scope is a future bounded evaluation of relation lapse support for `relation_001` as historical relation record only after boundary allowance.

Relation lapse may be supported, authorized, performed, and recorded only by that future operation and only as `RELATION_LAPSE_WITHOUT_PUNITIVE_CONSEQUENCE_ONLY`. Relation lapse is not punishment, dissolution, erasure, teardown, living relation state, historical receipt preservation, presence boundary authorization, presence, identity, coupling, FIELD machinery, runtime, currentness, authority, follow-on authorization, or follow-on work.

No relation dissolution occurs here. No relation is reversed, terminated, erased, mutated, invalidated, or punished here.
