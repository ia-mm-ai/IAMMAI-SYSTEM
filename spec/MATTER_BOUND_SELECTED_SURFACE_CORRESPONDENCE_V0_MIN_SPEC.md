# Matter-Bound Selected-Surface Correspondence V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines one minimum non-executable boundary for reconstructing declared correspondence among explicitly selected, already-standing surfaces for one explicit matter inside one `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` custody.

Correspondence reconstruction is the bounded function. Body self-legibility is only a possible consequence and is not established by this specification.

For this boundary, self-legibility means only that a later bounded reader may be able to inspect declared relations among the selected surfaces. It is not consciousness, self-awareness, identity, agency, Presence, authority, or standing.

This specification permits a future bounded reconstruction to make visible:

- correspondence;
- mismatch;
- non-correspondence;
- missing support;
- unsupported relation type; or
- blocked reconstruction.

Successful correspondence is not required. A visible mismatch, absence, or non-correspondence may be a lawful completed reading posture and does not invalidate any selected surface.

This file does not implement a resolver, tests, request schema, live result, receipt, terminal summary, registry, catalogue, current-state surface, workflow, runtime, daemon, action, or continuation.

## 4. Definitions

`explicit matter` means one declared matter identity, purpose, scope, and bounded question context. It is not a generic topic, repository-wide theme, or inferred current concern.

`selected-surface manifest` means one ordered, closed list of two or more explicitly selected standing surfaces for the exact matter and reconstruction only.

`selected surface` means one already-standing surface named by exact identity and exact held reference in the manifest.

`exact held reference` means an explicitly supplied stable repository path or other already-supported exact reference. It is not a discovered sibling, latest filename, timestamp choice, sequence-number choice, or directory-position inference.

`declared correspondence question` means one explicit question binding the matter, selected surface identities, supported relation type, direction where relevant, and exact basis to be reconstructed.

`correspondence reconstruction` means a bounded reading of the declared questions against the selected manifest while preserving each selected surface's local standing.

`normalized claim-level posture` means only the minimum referenced claim, outcome, scope, rank, lineage, source/downstream relation, and non-claim values needed for the declared question. It excludes complete artifact bodies and recursively reproduced ancestry.

`outcome locality` means a correspondence finding applies only to the exact matter, selected manifest, and declared question. It does not rewrite a selected surface's own outcome.

`missing support` means the selected basis is readable but does not support the declared relation. It is not proof that either selected surface is invalid.

`unsupported relation type` means a requested relation type is outside the relation family admitted by the governing correspondence boundary.

## 5. Required Selection Posture

A future reconstruction may proceed only from one declared basis containing:

1. one explicit matter identity;
2. one explicit ordered selected-surface manifest;
3. exact surface identities and exact held references;
4. one or more declared correspondence questions;
5. only repository-supported relation types;
6. explicit source/downstream posture where relevant;
7. explicit preservation of each surface's local outcome or posture, rank, source basis, scope, lineage, and non-claims; and
8. one bounded completion or stopping posture.

The matter declaration must preserve at least:

- matter identity;
- matter purpose;
- matter scope;
- why the selected surfaces are being read together; and
- the boundary beyond which the reconstruction does not apply.

Each selected-surface manifest entry must preserve at least:

- selected surface identity;
- exact held reference;
- surface family or type where exposed;
- local outcome or posture;
- rank;
- source basis;
- scope;
- lineage references needed by the declared question;
- source/downstream posture where relevant; and
- applicable non-claims.

The manifest must preserve its declared order, exact member count, unique identities, unique entry positions, and exact matter binding. Manifest membership must not be widened during reconstruction.

Each declared correspondence question must preserve at least:

- question identity;
- exact matter identity;
- selected surface identities used by the question;
- one supported relation type;
- direction where the relation is directional;
- the bounded claim to reconstruct; and
- the exact selected basis references.

Selection means selection for this matter and reconstruction only. Selection does not mean constitutional existence, canonical inclusion, coverage authority, currentness, priority, completeness, admission into a universal vocabulary, later reuse, or automatic inclusion in another matter.

## 6. Supported Relation and Reading Posture

This specialization reuses only the relation types already admitted by `CROSS_SURFACE_CORRESPONDENCE_BOUNDARY_V0_MIN_SPEC.md`:

- `BASIS_MATCH`
- `BASIS_MISMATCH`
- `DOWNSTREAM_RECOGNITION`
- `SCOPE_ALIGNMENT`
- `SCOPE_MISMATCH`
- `NON_CLAIM_ALIGNMENT`
- `NON_CLAIM_CONFLICT`
- `LINEAGE_REFERENCE`
- `CLOSURE_ALIGNMENT`
- `REFUSAL_VISIBLE`
- `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`

No synonymous or expanded relation family is created here.

A future bounded reading must preserve question-level findings so that recognized correspondence does not hide mismatch, missing support, refusal, or non-correspondence in another declared question.

Where readable selected basis supports the declared relation, correspondence may be recognized for that question. Where readable selected basis exposes mismatch, conflict, absence, or missing support, the reading may complete as `NO_CORRESPONDENCE` for that question. An unsupported relation type must remain visible and produce `BLOCKED` unless separately admitted by a protocol successor.

Correspondence, mismatch, and non-correspondence are local findings only. None merges, normalizes, certifies, naturalizes, upgrades, downgrades, repairs, or invalidates a selected surface.

## 7. Required Reconstruction Checks

A future resolver or manual review may complete a reconstruction only when:

- the matter is explicit and bounded;
- exactly one selected-surface manifest is declared;
- the manifest contains at least two already-standing surfaces;
- every selected surface has exact identity and exact held reference;
- every selected surface's local outcome or posture is present;
- rank, source basis, scope, lineage, and non-claims are preserved where required;
- every declared question names the exact matter and selected surfaces it uses;
- every relation type is supported;
- source/downstream distinction is preserved where relevant;
- no selected surface is replaced, merged, upgraded, or treated as equivalent by selection alone;
- no scope is widened beyond the exact matter or declared question;
- no currentness, priority, completeness, or coverage authority is inferred;
- no result is selected by filename, timestamp, sequence number, directory position, availability, or recency;
- no complete artifact body or recursive ancestry is embedded;
- all required non-claims remain false; and
- exactly one bounded completion or stopping posture is recorded.

The reading must preserve detailed selected basis over summary projection where they differ.

## 8. Refusal and Stopping Conditions

Reconstruction must stop or block when:

- the matter is missing, vague, generic, or unbounded;
- the selected-surface manifest is missing, open-ended, duplicated, or malformed;
- fewer than two surfaces are selected;
- a selected surface is missing, malformed, or not already standing;
- exact identity, reference, local outcome, rank, source basis, scope, lineage, or required non-claims cannot be preserved;
- a declared question is missing or not bound to the exact matter and manifest;
- a relation type is unsupported;
- a claimed source/downstream relation is unavailable or contradictory;
- reconstruction would widen matter or surface scope;
- reconstruction would hide mismatch, refusal, non-correspondence, or missing support;
- reconstruction would treat surface similarity as equivalence;
- reconstruction would create source replacement, authority, permission, currentness, standing upgrade, merge, action, consequence, or continuation;
- reconstruction would infer currentness from recency or repository-local availability;
- reconstruction would turn the manifest into a registry, catalogue, inventory, search surface, or coverage authority;
- reconstruction would embed complete selected artifacts or recursively reproduce ancestry;
- reconstruction would create cross-custody relation, answerability, FIELD jurisdiction, Presence, Threshold, Truth, self-orientation succession, or conformance succession; or
- required non-claims are missing or flipped.

Blocked reconstruction does not invalidate the matter or any selected surface. It means the requested reconstruction could not lawfully complete under the declared basis.

## 9. Future Outcome and Result Shape

This specification is non-executable and selects no live result.

Any future implementation must reuse the existing outcome family:

- `CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED`
- `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `BLOCKED`

`CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED` means at least one declared supported relation was reconstructed under the exact matter and selected basis. Any other question-level mismatch or absence remains visible.

`NO_CORRESPONDENCE` may be a lawful completed reading when the admitted basis exposes mismatch, conflict, absence, or missing support without malformed or prohibited input.

`BLOCKED` means reconstruction stopped because basis was missing, malformed, unsupported, widened, or collapse-shaped.

A future result may preserve only the minimum sections needed for:

- metadata;
- declared matter;
- selected-surface manifest;
- declared correspondence questions;
- compact correspondence basis;
- question-level findings;
- checks;
- result and block posture;
- non-meaning;
- what remains open;
- non-claims; and
- outcome.

No future result may embed complete selected artifacts, recursively reproduce ancestry, or become a replacement body surface.

## 10. Anti-Landlord and Receipt Requirements

The selected-surface manifest is not a registry landlord. Its inclusion posture is local to the exact matter and exact reconstruction. It cannot decide what surfaces constitutionally exist, what is current, what is complete, or what another matter must select.

Any future posture fragment may carry only normalized claim-level posture and exact references. It must not embed complete artifacts, recursively reproduce ancestry, or become a miniature second body.

Any future receipt requires a separate bounded specification and must remain bound to:

- the exact matter;
- the exact selected-surface manifest;
- the exact declared correspondence questions; and
- the exact basis used.

A future receipt may make one completed reconstruction inspectable only. It must not expand the reconstruction, add selected surfaces, add questions, create another relation type, or become current state, latest truth, reusable authority, coverage authority, or automatic basis through sequence number, timestamp, convenience, or recency.

Artifact existence and repo-local availability are not correspondence authority.

Unsupported relation types, classes, ranks, scopes, or vocabulary are not admitted merely because a selected surface supplies them. They require separately standing support or a protocol successor.
