# Source-Body Operational Authority Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the source-body operational authority boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

Source-body operational authority boundary is the next lawful gate after distributed operation eligibility.

The purpose is to prevent eligibility from becoming authority, and to prevent source-body reference from becoming automatic operation permission.

This boundary evaluates one eligible declared distributed operation matter only.

It evaluates whether the eligible matter has a sufficient source-body authority basis to proceed toward a future admission / transition boundary.

It does not grant authority.

It does not create authority.

It does not admit operation.

It does not authorize operation.

It does not execute operation.

It does not decide carrier operational roles.

It does not authorize repository synchronization.

It does not authorize full body transfer.

It does not create a second body.

It does not authorize continuation.

It does not create consequence.

It does not create public readiness.

It does not claim final completion.

It does not schedule follow-on work.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not an authority grant.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not admission.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not operation.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not carrier authority.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not registry authority.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not currentness authority.

Source-body operational authority boundary is not distributed standing authority.

Source-body operational authority boundary only checks where operational authority could lawfully originate if a later admission boundary is ever separately declared and bounded.

This file is an authority-origin boundary specification only.

It is not an implementation, resolver, test, authority grant, operation admission, operation authorization, operation execution, distributed operation, carrier operational role boundary, synchronization / non-synchronization boundary, distributed operation transition authority, distributed refusal and abort law, distributed execution / emission boundary, distributed action / consequence boundary, distributed operation receipt / exhaustion, distributed operation conformance, distributed operation closure, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, current self-orientation v10, final governance, final continuity completion, final system identity, public launch surface, truth law, action law, consequence/action law, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`source_body_operational_authority_boundary` means a bounded review of whether one eligible declared distributed operation matter has a sufficient source-body authority basis for possible future admission / transition review. It is not an authority grant.

`selected_eligible_distributed_operation_matter` means one declared distributed operation matter whose selected eligibility result outcome is `DISTRIBUTED_OPERATION_MATTER_ELIGIBLE_FOR_REVIEW` and whose eligibility failed check count is zero.

`source_body_authority_basis` means the source-body lineage basis, source-body authority reference, current-body conformance v4 closure basis, current self-orientation v9 basis, distributed standing basis where applicable, divergence consequence basis where applicable, refusal/abort awareness, carrier context where applicable, and explicit authority non-claims used to review possible authority origin.

`operational_authority_origin` means the body-side source lineage and authority reference that could lawfully ground a later admission / transition boundary. It is not authority granted by this review.

`authority_review_question` means the declared question asking whether the eligible matter has enough source-body authority basis for future admission / transition review. It is not an admission question and not an operation question.

`authority_claim` means the bounded assertion that sufficient source-body authority basis has been recorded for possible later admission / transition review.

`authority_refusal` means refusal to record authority basis because the selected eligibility result, selected matter, source-body lineage, authority reference, closure basis, refusal/abort awareness, non-claims, no-carrier-authority posture, no-registry-authority posture, no-currentness-authority posture, no-admission posture, no-operation posture, or no-collapse posture is missing, malformed, failed, authority-granting, permission-creating, admission-shaped, operation-shaped, synchronization-shaped, full-body-transfer-shaped, continuation-shaped, consequence-shaped, final-completion-shaped, follow-on-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`authority_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that authority basis review did not grant authority, create authority, create permission, admit operation, authorize operation, execute operation, define carrier roles, synchronize repositories, transfer the body, create a second body, authorize continuation, create currentness, select carriers, replace source, create truth/action, create consequence, resolve divergence, erase evidence/refusal/block/projection mismatch, create public readiness, claim final completion, schedule follow-on work, mutate evidence, replay source, or merge carriers.

`sufficient_source_body_authority_basis_for_future_admission_review` means the selected eligible matter has enough declared source-body lineage and authority reference basis to be considered by a later admission / transition boundary, if that later boundary is separately declared and bounded.

`insufficient_authority_basis` means the eligible matter is readable but lacks adequate source-body authority basis for future admission / transition review.

`authority_basis_requires_additional_support` means the eligible matter is non-collapse-shaped, but the authority review cannot yet determine sufficient or insufficient basis because bounded source-body authority support is not specific enough.

`blocked_authority_review` means authority review cannot lawfully proceed because the request or selected eligibility result is missing, malformed, unreadable, failed, or collapse-shaped.

Authority basis review does not grant authority.

Authority basis review does not authorize operation.

Authority basis review does not decide carrier roles.

Authority basis review does not admit operation.

Authority basis review only decides whether the eligible matter has enough source-body authority basis to proceed toward a later admission / transition boundary.
