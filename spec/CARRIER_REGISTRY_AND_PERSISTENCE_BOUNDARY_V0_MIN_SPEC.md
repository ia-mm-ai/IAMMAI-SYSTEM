# Carrier Registry and Persistence Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for future carrier registry and persistence surfaces in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

The purpose is to prevent registry existence, record presence, persisted metadata, indexed carrier status, stored evidence references, or registry membership from becoming source, currentness, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, continuation, distributed operation, or operational permission.

Registry is not authority.

Persistence is not standing.

Indexing is not currentness.

Record presence is not permission.

Registry membership is not distributed standing.

A registry or persistence boundary may preserve references to what already stands elsewhere. It may not make those references stand by being listed.

Core rule: a carrier registry/persistence surface may preserve bounded carrier records, lifecycle status references, evidence references, relation references, divergence references, currentness participation references, and non-claims. It may not create source, currentness, authority, permission, hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, or distributed operation.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, database, persistence engine, carrier registry implementation, distributed standing surface, repository synchronization layer, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, carrier fleet management, network topology, consensus system, leader election, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

This file does not define a storage backend.

This file does not define a database schema as executable law.

This file does not implement registry or persistence.

## 4. Definitions

`carrier registry` means a bounded future surface that may index selected carrier identities, carrier labels where declared, carrier lifecycle references, evidence references, relation references, divergence references, currentness participation references, experiment references, integrity references, and non-claims. It is not authority, currentness, source, hierarchy, standing, or distributed standing.

`persistence surface` means a bounded future surface that may preserve references, record envelopes, artifact identities, artifact outcomes, carrier identities, lifecycle posture, relation posture, divergence posture, currentness participation posture, and non-claims for later reconstruction. It is not standing by duration or storage.

`registry record` means one bounded record in a future registry/persistence surface. A registry record is not source.

`carrier record` means a bounded record about one carrier identity, declared label, lifecycle posture, role reference, evidence reference, or relation reference. A carrier record is not currentness.

`carrier evidence reference` means a reference to receipt evidence, refusal evidence, returned evidence, admitted evidence, integrity evidence, corruption evidence, staleness evidence, or other carrier-local or body-line evidence. A persisted reference is not standing.

`lifecycle status reference` means a reference to a separately recorded lifecycle status or lifecycle transition. Registry membership is not distributed standing.

`relation reference` means a reference to a separately recognized multi-carrier relation, relation conformance, or relation conformance closure. It does not create relation.

`divergence reference` means a reference to a separately recorded visible divergence posture. It does not resolve divergence.

`currentness participation reference` means a reference to a separately bounded currentness participation posture. It does not create currentness.

`registry basis` means the declared registry question, selected record category, selected carrier identity where applicable, selected referenced artifact identity where applicable, selected referenced artifact outcome where applicable, preservation basis, and explicit non-claims.

`persistence basis` means the declared persistence purpose, selected references, artifact identities, artifact outcomes, carrier identities where available, posture references, paths or links where applicable, preservation rules, and explicit non-claims.

`registry refusal` means refusal to record a registry posture because the request is missing, malformed, unsupported, authority-shaped, currentness-shaped, hierarchy-shaped, distributed-standing-shaped, synchronization-shaped, evidence-erasing, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`persistence refusal` means refusal to persist or preserve a reference because persistence would mutate, overwrite, repair, erase, hide, decide, authorize, or otherwise upgrade the referenced posture.

`registry non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that registry did not create source, currentness, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, distributed operation, evidence erasure, hidden refusal, hidden divergence, hidden corruption, or repair by overwrite.

`persistence non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that persistence did not create standing, currentness, authority, permission, hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, distributed operation, or evidence mutation.

A registry record is not source.

A carrier record is not currentness.

A persisted reference is not standing.

Registry membership is not distributed standing.

Registry/persistence may preserve that a referenced artifact exists, what it identifies itself as, what outcome it reports, and what non-claims it preserves.

Registry/persistence may not make the referenced artifact stand by listing it.

## 12. Relation to Carrier Lifecycle Boundary

Lifecycle status may be referenced by registry/persistence.

Registry does not create lifecycle status by itself.

Persistence does not make lifecycle status current.

Registry may preserve lifecycle history only as references to separately recorded lifecycle artifacts.

Lifecycle remains bounded and downstream unless separately admitted elsewhere.

Carrier C's `CARRIER_REFUSED_OR_BLOCKED` lifecycle status may be referenced as lifecycle evidence only.

Referencing Carrier C lifecycle status does not create Carrier C authority, currentness, body participation, registry authority, or distributed standing.

## 13. Relation to Additional Physical Carrier Experiment Boundary

Experiment declarations may be referenced.

Experiment declaration does not create registry membership by itself.

Registry reference does not authorize physical execution.

Registry reference does not authorize repeated experiments.

Success or block from an experiment may be referenced without creating expansion permission.

The additional physical carrier experiment boundary remains declaration-only until a separate receipt/refusal execution surface exists.

Registry/persistence does not retroactively authorize the experiment or any later experiment.

## 14. Relation to Carrier Role and Emission Boundary

Carrier roles may be referenced only as operation-local roles.

Registry does not make roles permanent.

Registry does not grant emission rights.

Role history does not create hierarchy.

Carrier role records must preserve the declared operation, role scope, permitted emission class where available, and role non-claims where available.

Registry/persistence may not turn `SOURCE_CARRIER_FOR_PACKET`, `RECEIVING_CARRIER`, `RETURNING_CARRIER`, or `REFUSING_CARRIER` into universal source, current carrier, authority, successor, or body.

## 15. Relation to Carrier-Local Emission Admission

Admitted evidence may be referenced.

Registry does not admit evidence by listing it.

Returned evidence remains not admitted unless separately admitted.

Admission as evidence remains evidence only.

Registry/persistence may preserve admission identity, admission outcome, admitted evidence identity, carrier identity, return path where available, and admission non-claims.

Registry/persistence may not convert admission into source, currentness, authority, permission, truth, action, consequence, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, or continuation.

## 16. Relation to Cross-Carrier Divergence

Registry/persistence may reference divergent evidence only as divergent evidence, not as correctness, currentness, truth, action, carrier priority, or carrier hierarchy.
