Current-body conformance v3 closure records the meaning and non-meaning of one selected v3 `BODY_CONFORMANT` result. It is closure of meaning only.

Conformance closure is not conformance.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

Current self-orientation v8 mirrors the closed multi-carrier relation band as downstream posture only.

Current-body conformance v3 confirms that the v8-oriented body posture coheres as one current body line after the closed multi-carrier relation band.

The live v3 conformance result has outcome `BODY_CONFORMANT`, passed check count greater than zero, and failed check count zero.

Conformance passed is still not closure. A v3 `BODY_CONFORMANT` result proves bounded coherence of selected v8 posture under v3 checks, but it does not by itself record the meaning and non-meaning of that result.

`BODY_CONFORMANT` can be overread as permission for another carrier experiment, distributed standing, continuation, currentness, carrier hierarchy, or follow-on work.

Closure is needed to record that v3 conformance means bounded current-body coherence only. It preserves that v3 did not authorize follow-on work.

This remains narrower than distributed standing and narrower than physical carrier expansion.

## 4. Definitions

`current-body conformance v3 closure` means the bounded recording of what one selected current-body conformance v3 `BODY_CONFORMANT` result means and does not mean.

`selected v3 conformance result` means one declared current-body conformance pass v3 result whose outcome is `BODY_CONFORMANT`.

`closure basis` means the selected v3 conformance result, selected v8 orientation basis, selected v7 basis where exposed, selected current-body conformance v2 basis where exposed, selected multi-carrier relation basis, selected relation conformance basis, selected relation conformance closure basis, v3 conformance checks, v3 conformance statement, v3 conformance non-meaning, visible refusal posture, visible divergence posture, currentness participation posture, and explicit non-claims that permit closure of meaning.

`closure refusal` means refusal to record closure because the selected v3 conformance result is missing, malformed, not body-conformant, collapse-shaped, or because closure would create authority, permission, currentness, distributed standing, continuation, expansion, carrier hierarchy, or another forbidden posture.

`v3 conformance meaning` means the preserved meaning that current self-orientation v8 cohered as the present body mirror after the closed multi-carrier relation band, while preserving upstream current/governing basis and downstream-only relation-band posture.

`v3 conformance non-meaning` means the preserved posture that v3 conformance did not create distributed standing, currentness, authority, permission, source replacement, carrier hierarchy, carrier selection, divergence resolution, continuation, successor pressure, or expansion.

`closed v3 conformance posture` means a closure-recorded posture over one selected v3 `BODY_CONFORMANT` result. It does not mean distributed standing.

`closure non-claim` means an explicit false claim that closure did not create or authorize a forbidden posture.

Closed v3 conformance posture does not mean distributed standing.

Closure of meaning does not mean completion of the body.

Closure does not authorize next work.
