## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_recognition_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether one non-capture-passed source-body reception request / relation candidate may be recognized for later reception-family accounting. It is recognition only.

`selected_non_capture_result` means one source-body reception non-capture / non-adoption / non-currentness result whose outcome is `SOURCE_BODY_RECEPTION_NON_CAPTURE_PASSED` and whose failed check count is zero.

`reception_recognition` means the bounded posture that the source-body reception request / relation candidate is visible as recognized for later reception-family accounting. It does not authorize reception and does not receive source.

`bounded_reception_recognition` means recognition constrained by prior request declaration, identity preservation, receiving-context role, eligibility / admissibility, and non-capture results, with explicit non-claims against authorization, receipt, adoption, authority, currentness, validation, invalidation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, and follow-on work.

`reception_relation_candidate` means the declared relation under review between the selected source-body surface and the receiving context. Recognition may make this candidate visible for later accounting without making it authorized, received, adopted, current, operative, public-ready, conformed, or closed.

`recognition_posture` means the bounded status of recognition itself. It is not permission, receipt, source replacement, currentness, validation, governance, publication, conformance, closure, or final completion.

`recognized_for_accounting_only` means recognized so that later reception-family boundaries may inspect the relation if separately created. It does not schedule, authorize, or execute those later boundaries.

`recognition_not_authorization` means recognition does not authorize reception, source receipt, operation, adoption, validation, invalidation, publication, continuation, or follow-on work.

`recognition_not_source_receipt` means recognition does not receive source, create source receipt, exhaust reception, or make the selected source-body surface current in the receiving context.

`recognition_not_adoption` means recognition does not adopt source, authorize adoption, or treat the receiving context as adopter.

`recognition_not_currentness` means recognition does not create currentness for the selected source-body surface, receiving context, relation candidate, latest artifact, carrier context, registry reference, or narrative convenience.

`recognition_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that recognition did not authorize reception, receive source, create source receipt, create governance, make receiving context source/authority/current/receiver/adopter/validator/invalidator/operator, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, standing propagation, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, receipt / exhaustion, conformance, closure, mutation, replay, or merge.

`recognition_overreach` means treating recognition as any downstream force beyond bounded visibility for later reception-family accounting.

`authorization_overreach` means treating recognition as permission to receive, adopt, validate, invalidate, operate, govern, publish, continue, or perform follow-on work.

`source_receipt_overreach` means treating recognition as actual source receipt, source receipt recording, receipt / exhaustion, or source-body reception completion.

Recognition means the bounded reception relation / request may be made visible for later reception-family accounting.

Recognition does not mean authorization.

Recognition does not mean source receipt.

Recognition does not mean adoption.

Recognition does not mean source validation or invalidation.

Recognition does not mean currentness.

Recognition does not create operation permission.

Recognition does not create public readiness or final completion.
