## 5. What Continuity Memory Means Here

Continuity memory here means preserved lineage that lets the body remain itself across turns, packets, successors, and seams while preserving prior surfaces as prior surfaces.

Continuity memory is not:

- mutable storage
- overwrite
- one giant recap
- mere chronology
- narrative compression replacing the preserved body
- latest-file selection
- a current summary pretending to be the whole body

A remembered surface remains preserved. A later correction, refinement, narrowing, or adoption enters by successor relation, not by editing the earlier surface into never having stood.

Lawful continuity memory therefore requires at least three things together:

- lineage: what prior surface, run, packet, result, or derivative is being carried or succeeded
- successor relation: how later material lawfully relates to earlier material
- rank/currentness: which surface governs the present bounded question and why

Sequence alone proves only order. It does not prove lawful memory. A later artifact can follow an earlier artifact without lawfully succeeding it, governing it, correcting it, or replacing it.

## 6. What the Continuity-Memory Seam Means Here

The continuity-memory seam is the lawful boundary across which preserved body material is carried without being rewritten.

At this seam:

- successor relation must remain explicit
- currentness must be re-established through rank and standing, not through recency
- derivative carry must remain derivative
- source surfaces must remain source
- prior artifacts must remain preserved
- non-claims must continue blocking false closure

The seam is not source replacement, mutable overwrite, silent correction, chronology alone, storage sync, host replay, preserved-run merge, or final continuity completion.

The seam exists because the body must be able to continue without pretending that continuation is the same thing as editing its past.

## 7. Preserved Turns, Successor Relation, and Rank

Lawful continuity memory depends on preserved turns, successor relation, and rank together.

Preserved turns make prior material recoverable. They show that an earlier surface existed, what it claimed, what it refused, what it carried, and what it did not claim.

Successor relation shows how later material lawfully relates to earlier material. A successor may correct, refine, narrow, adopt, supersede, or carry forward within a bounded relation. It must not erase the fact that the predecessor stood or was emitted.

Rank determines which surface currently governs a bounded question. A later surface may have higher current rank for a particular use only when the line explicitly supports that posture.

Preservation without rank leaves currentness undecided. Rank without lineage becomes unsupported authority. Sequence without successor relation becomes chronology only. Currentness without lineage becomes recency fraud.

## 8. Currentness Without Recency Fraud

Currentness does not mean "latest file wins."

Currentness must be explicitly derived through lawful standing, rank, successor relation, and bounded selection surfaces. In this repo line, currentness appears through surfaces such as execution-authority resolution, current-governing packet, governing transition, governing re-resolution, successor-family adoption, effective-family resolution, effective-family consumption, current-work-input resolution, and downstream current-state surfaces that preserve their active inputs.

Currentness is not mutation. It is not overwrite. It does not erase prior preserved turns, prior current families, prior transition results, or prior derivative surfaces.

Present governing status must remain distinguishable from earlier preserved lineage. A current surface can govern a bounded present question while earlier surfaces remain preserved and readable as lineage.

Any memory model that chooses by lexical latest, file timestamp, naming convention, or operator recollection alone is outside this closure.

## 9. Derivative Carry Without Source Collapse

Derivative carry is lawful only if source remains source.

The current line can touch a current-state surface, transfer a carried derivative, receive it, participate with it, and permit bounded action from it. Those surfaces carry material forward, but they do not promote carried material into source, governing authority, final local identity, or continuity completion.

Transfer remains transfer lineage. Receipt remains receipt lineage. Participation remains participation lineage. Action permission remains action-permission lineage. The selected source surface remains source.

Carried derivative material must not silently replace the source it derives from. It must not become governing identity by use, by receipt, by participation, or by action permission.

Derivative carry is continuation support. It is not source promotion, replay, merge, standing upgrade, or completion.

## 10. Standing/Truth Memory vs Interpretation/Decision Surfaces

Lawful continuity memory must not collapse standing/truth memory into interpretation, decision, use, or action surfaces.

The current line now exposes downstream surfaces that can answer:

- what stands
- what remains open

Standing/truth memory preserves the chain of what has stood, what has been succeeded, what remains current for bounded purposes, and what non-claims still block false closure. Interpretation and decision surfaces read, select, use, or permit action from that memory under bounded checks.

If a downstream answer, receipt, participation result, or action-permission result were treated as the whole memory, the body would collapse preserved standing into one current decision layer. This spec refuses that collapse.
