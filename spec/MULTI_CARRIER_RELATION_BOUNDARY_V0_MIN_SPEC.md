# Multi-Carrier Relation Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded multi-carrier relation boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

It defines when multiple carriers or carrier-evidence surfaces may be recognized as standing in bounded relation to the body line.

The purpose is to prevent multiple carriers or multiple carrier-evidence surfaces from becoming:

- source replacement
- currentness creation
- authority
- permission
- carrier hierarchy
- successor standing
- body formation
- distributed standing
- presence
- threshold
- truth
- action
- consequence
- continuation

Relation is a bounded body-side recognition over selected carrier roles and selected carrier evidence. It is not a carrier-local claim.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by possession.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by emission.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by receipt.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by return.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by admission.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by correspondence.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by divergence.

Multi-carrier relation is not created by currentness participation eligibility.

Multi-carrier relation may be recognized only by an explicit body-side relation basis that preserves carrier roles, carrier evidence, admitted evidence, divergence visibility, currentness non-claims, lineage, and non-claims.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, distributed standing surface, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization surface, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

The carrier role and emission boundary defines local carrier roles and role-permitted emissions.

Carrier-local emissions may be admitted into the body line only as evidence.

The cross-carrier receipt boundary proves that one carried surface may be received by one physical carrier as carried evidence without creating source, currentness, authority, permission, successor standing, body formation, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The cross-carrier divergence boundary preserves visible mismatch without resolving it into truth, currentness, action, carrier correctness, carrier hierarchy, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The cross-carrier currentness boundary prevents carrier evidence from creating currentness by possession, receipt, return, admission, correspondence, divergence, timestamp, majority, success count, availability, label, or convenience.

The cross-surface correspondence boundary permits selected standing surfaces to be read together while preserving rank, source, scope, lineage, local outcome, and non-claims.

Returned receipt evidence and returned refusal evidence now stand as downstream evidence.

Physical receiving-carrier receipt evidence now stands as carried evidence.

Carrier-local receipt emission has been admitted as body-line evidence only.

Visible divergence has been recorded between returned blocked receipt evidence and returned successful receipt evidence.

Currentness participation eligibility has been recorded for admitted returned Carrier B receipt evidence without creating currentness.

Current self-orientation v7 mirrors carrier evidence as downstream posture.

Current-body conformance v2 verifies that the v7-integrated posture coheres as one current body line.

The body has now bounded the prerequisites for relation.

Before any distributed standing, registry, synchronization, or broader carrier lifecycle can be considered, the body must define when multiple carriers or carrier-evidence surfaces may stand in bounded relation.

This remains narrower than distributed standing.

## 4. Definitions

`multi-carrier relation` means a bounded body-side recognition that selected carriers or selected carrier-evidence surfaces stand in relation for one declared relation question. It is not currentness, hierarchy, distributed standing, synchronization, carrier sovereignty, or continuation.

`selected carrier` means a declared carrier identity or label selected for relation review. A selected carrier does not become source, current, authoritative, permission-bearing, successor, body, or body participant by being selected.

`selected carrier evidence` means a carrier-produced, carrier-returned, carrier-admitted, corresponded, divergent, receipt, refusal, or currentness-participation evidence surface selected for relation review.

`relation basis` means the declared relation question, selected carrier identities where available, selected carrier roles where available, selected evidence identities, selected evidence outcomes, source or carried basis where available, admission status where applicable, correspondence status where applicable, divergence status where applicable, currentness participation status where applicable, lineage, relation type, and explicit non-claims.

`relation claim` means the bounded assertion that selected carriers or selected carrier-evidence surfaces stand in one declared relation type for one declared relation question.

`relation refusal` means requested relation recognition is refused because required basis is missing, malformed, unsupported, hidden-divergence-shaped, source-replacing, currentness-creating, hierarchy-creating, distributed-standing-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`no multi-carrier relation` means selected carriers or selected carrier-evidence surfaces do not stand in relation for the declared question while each selected surface remains preserved in its own posture.

`relation posture` means the recorded body-side relation status of selected carriers or evidence. It may recognize bounded relation, no relation, or blocked relation, but it does not create currentness or distributed standing.

`relation evidence set` means the selected carriers and selected carrier-evidence surfaces preserved for the declared relation question.

`relation participant` means a selected carrier or carrier-evidence surface participating in a bounded relation set. Relation participant does not mean body participant.

`carrier relation non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that relation did not create source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, or continuation.

`body-side relation recognition` means relation is recognized by a bounded body-side surface under declared basis and non-claims. It is not carrier-local relation creation and not carrier sovereignty.

Relation participant does not mean body participant.

Multi-carrier relation does not mean carrier sovereignty.

Multi-carrier relation does not mean currentness.

Multi-carrier relation does not mean distributed standing.

## 5. What Multi-Carrier Relation Means

Multi-carrier relation means a body-side surface recognizes that selected carriers or selected carrier-evidence surfaces are in bounded relation for one declared relation question.

Relation may include:

- source carrier for packet related to receiving carrier
- receiving carrier related to returned evidence
- returned blocked evidence related to returned successful evidence
- admitted evidence related to divergence evidence
- currentness participation evidence related to body-side current posture
- carrier roles related through one declared operation chain
- evidence surfaces related through preserved lineage

Relation means:

- selected carriers or selected evidence may be read as one bounded relation set
- carrier roles remain operation-local
- evidence remains downstream
- returned states remain distinct
- admitted states remain distinct
- divergent states remain distinct
- currentness-participation states remain distinct
- visible divergence remains visible
- currentness non-claims remain false
- relation may support future conformance only if separately admitted

Relation records bounded relation posture. It does not convert relation into force.
