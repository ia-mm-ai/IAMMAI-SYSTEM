## 4. Definitions

`distributed_operation_conformance_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether the recorded distributed operation boundary chain conformed to its own recorded limits for one admitted operation context after receipt / exhaustion boundary accounting has been recorded. It does not claim executed-operation conformance.

`selected_receipt_exhaustion_boundary_result` means one declared distributed operation receipt / exhaustion boundary result whose outcome is `DISTRIBUTED_OPERATION_RECEIPT_EXHAUSTION_BOUNDARY_RECORDED`.

`boundary_chain_conformance` means review of whether the recorded boundary chain preserved its declared limits, scopes, non-claims, prior-boundary basis, and no-overread posture. It is not executed-operation conformance.

`executed_operation_conformance` means conformance of an operation that actually executed or was otherwise actually performed. This boundary does not claim executed-operation conformance.

`conformance_posture` means the declared posture for checking boundary-chain integrity, non-claim preservation, scope preservation, overread prevention, and closure dependency. It is not closure.

`conformance_failure` means a readable boundary-chain condition showing that a recorded boundary did not conform to its own declared limits. It does not erase the boundary chain and does not authorize repair.

`conformance_refusal` means refusal to record boundary-chain conformance when required basis is missing, malformed, failed, overread, closure-shaped, public-readiness-shaped, reusable-permission-shaped, continuation-shaped, mutation-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`conformance_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that this boundary did not claim executed-operation conformance, actual operation receipt, closure, public readiness, final completion, reusable permission, autonomous continuation, action, consequence, execution, emission, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, or follow-on work.

`boundary_chain_integrity` means that each selected prior boundary remains visible as its own boundary surface, with its own outcome, checks, scope, basis, statement, non-meaning, and non-claims preserved without replacement by summary or later narrative.

`non_claim_preservation` means that required false postures remain false across the selected receipt / exhaustion, action / consequence, execution / emission, admission / transition, refusal / abort, sync/non-sync, carrier role, source-body authority, eligibility, and matter declaration layers.

`overread_prevention` means that no layer is read as doing work it did not do. Admission is not execution. Execution / emission boundary is not execution or output emission. Action / consequence boundary is not action or consequence. Receipt / exhaustion boundary is not actual receipt, conformance, or closure.

`scope_preservation` means that each boundary remains bounded to its declared scope and does not expand into operation, authority, synchronization, continuation, public readiness, final completion, or follow-on work.

`prior_boundary_preservation` means that selected prior boundary results are preserved as prior basis only and are not mutated, replayed, merged, overwritten, summarized away, or upgraded into later force.

`closure_dependency` means the required future posture that closure remains separate and cannot be claimed by conformance boundary recording.

Boundary-chain conformance does not mean executed-operation conformance.

Boundary-chain conformance does not mean closure.

Boundary-chain conformance does not mean public readiness.

Boundary-chain conformance does not mean final completion.

Boundary-chain conformance only reviews whether the recorded boundary chain stayed inside its declared limits.
