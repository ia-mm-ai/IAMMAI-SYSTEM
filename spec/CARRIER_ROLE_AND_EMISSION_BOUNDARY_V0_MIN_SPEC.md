# Carrier Role and Emission Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded carrier role and emission boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

It defines:

- what a carrier is for this boundary
- what carrier roles may be held locally in a declared operation
- what each admitted role may emit
- what each admitted role may not emit
- what must not be inferred from carrier role or carrier-local emission

The purpose is to prevent the statement "this carrier emitted something" from becoming source, currentness, authority, permission, standing, successor, body, multi-carrier relation, distributed standing, or continuation.

A carrier role is local to a declared operation. It is not permanent identity.

A carrier-local emission is a local output permitted by a bounded carrier role. It does not enter the body line by being emitted. Body-line admission requires separate bounded return or admission law.

This file is a boundary specification only. It does not implement a resolver, test, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

Current self-orientation v7 mirrors the post-carrier-receipt body posture while preserving that post-conformance closure, cross-surface correspondence, cross-carrier receipt, returned Carrier B receipt evidence, and visible Carrier B refusal evidence remain downstream posture only.

Current-body conformance v2 proves that the v7-integrated posture coheres as one current body line without creating authority, permission, currentness, signal, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, successor standing, or continuation.

One receiving carrier proof now stands. Returned receipt evidence and refusal evidence stand as carried evidence only.

The body has not defined multi-carrier law. It has not defined distributed standing. It has not defined cross-carrier currentness or carrier participation law.

Before multi-carrier relation can be defined, carrier roles and carrier-local emissions must be bounded. Without this boundary, carrier-local emission could be misread as source, currentness, authority, permission, successor standing, body formation, carrier relation, or continuation.

Therefore one bounded carrier role and emission boundary is lawful now as a narrower precondition for any later carrier-relation work.

## 4. Definitions

`carrier` means a bounded environment that can hold, read, receive, copy, or emit artifacts. A carrier is not source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, or participant by default.

`carrier role` means the operation-local posture a carrier holds for one declared carrier operation. The role is bounded to that operation and does not become permanent identity.

`declared carrier operation` means one bounded operation in which a carrier role, selected carrier, selected surface or packet, purpose, permitted emission class, and non-claims are explicit.

`carrier-local emission` means an output emitted by a carrier under a declared carrier role. It remains local unless separately admitted into the body line.

`body-line admission` means a separate bounded process by which carrier-local emission or returned evidence may be recognized inside the current body line while preserving role, emission identity, basis, integrity evidence where applicable, and non-claims.

`source-carrier-for-packet` means the carrier from which one carried packet or carried surface is emitted for one declared operation only. This role does not create universal source authority.

`receiving carrier` means a carrier that receives one carried surface or packet under a declared receipt operation. It may record receipt or refusal but does not become source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, or distributed standing.

`holding carrier` means a carrier that only holds bytes or artifacts without lawful receipt. Holding is passive storage unless separately bounded.

`returning carrier` means a carrier that returns its local receipt or refusal evidence to the body line or source carrier context. Returning evidence does not admit itself.

`refusing carrier` means a carrier that refuses a carried packet, surface, receipt request, or malformed basis and preserves refusal evidence where available.

`candidate witness carrier` means a possible future carrier role that may attest to observation, holding, or verification without receiving as participant. It is not admitted by this specification.

`candidate comparison carrier` means a possible future carrier role that may hold multiple surfaces for comparison or correspondence only. It is not admitted by this specification.

`candidate emitting carrier` means a possible future carrier role that may emit new carrier-local surfaces beyond receipt or refusal. It is not generally admitted by this specification.

`stale carrier` means a possible future carrier role for a carrier holding a surface valid under prior basis but not current relative to a later admitted body line. It is not fully defined by this specification.

`successor carrier` means a possible future carrier role that would be allowed to continue a line or emit successor surfaces. It is explicitly unopened and not admitted in this version.

## 5. Carrier Role Invariant

Carrier role is operation-local.

Carrier role is not permanent identity.

Carrier role does not grant authority outside the declared operation.

The same physical or logical carrier may hold different roles in different declared operations.

A carrier may not use one role to claim another role.

Role transition requires a separate declared operation and bounded basis.

No carrier role creates source, currentness, authority, permission, successor standing, body formation, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, or continuation by default.

Carrier role must preserve non-claims. If role posture would flip a required non-claim, role recognition must be refused.

## 6. Admitted Carrier Roles in This Version

The admitted carrier roles in this version are minimal and operation-local.

### 6.1 `SOURCE_CARRIER_FOR_PACKET`

`SOURCE_CARRIER_FOR_PACKET` means the carrier from which one carried packet or carried surface is emitted for one declared operation.

It may emit:

- carried packet
- packet manifest
- carried surface hash or integrity evidence
- transfer declaration

It may not emit:

- universal source authority
- currentness
- permission
- body standing
- successor standing
- distributed standing
- multi-carrier law
- continuation authorization

The source role is local to the carried packet or carried surface. It does not make the carrier source for other operations.

### 6.2 `RECEIVING_CARRIER`

`RECEIVING_CARRIER` means the carrier that receives one carried surface or packet under a declared receipt operation.

It may emit:

- `CARRIED_SURFACE_RECEIVED`
- `BLOCKED` receipt
- receipt refusal reason
- receipt checks

It may not emit:

- body conformance
- self-orientation
- source claim
- currentness claim
- authority claim
- permission claim
- successor claim
- body claim
- distributed standing
- multi-carrier relation
- continuation authorization

Receipt by a receiving carrier remains carried-evidence posture only.

### 6.3 `HOLDING_CARRIER`

`HOLDING_CARRIER` means a carrier that only holds bytes or artifacts without lawful receipt.

It may emit:

- no body-standing emission by default
- passive storage status only if separately bounded later

It may not emit:

- lawful receipt
- currentness
- authority
- permission
- standing
- body relation
- successor standing
- distributed standing
- multi-carrier law

Holding bytes is passive storage. Passive storage is not receipt.

### 6.4 `RETURNING_CARRIER`

`RETURNING_CARRIER` means a carrier that returns its local receipt or refusal evidence to the body line or source carrier context.

It may emit:

- returned receipt evidence
- returned blocked receipt evidence
- returned evidence packet

It may not emit:

- altered receipt meaning
- source replacement
- currentness
- authority
- permission
- body standing
- successor standing
- admission into the body line by itself
- distributed standing
- multi-carrier law

Returned evidence must remain evidence until separately admitted.

### 6.5 `REFUSING_CARRIER`

`REFUSING_CARRIER` means a carrier that refuses a carried packet, carried surface, receipt request, or malformed basis.

It may emit:

- `BLOCKED`
- refusal reason
- failed checks
- preserved basis where available

It may not emit:

- invalidation of the source surface
- invalidation of other carriers' receipts
- authority from refusal
- currentness from refusal
- permission from refusal
- successor standing from refusal
- body formation from refusal
- continuation authorization from refusal

Refusal remains visible evidence. It does not erase a later successful receipt and does not invalidate upstream standing by itself.

## 10. Carrier-Local Emission vs Body-Line Admission

Carrier-local emission does not self-admit into the body line.

Returned evidence is not automatically admitted evidence.

Body-line admission requires a separate bounded return or admission surface.

An emission may be preserved as evidence without becoming source or currentness.

An emission may be refused without invalidating the source surface.

Body-line admission must preserve:

- declared carrier operation
- carrier role
- emission identity
- emission class
- selected carrier identity
- selected packet or surface identity where applicable
- selected packet or surface outcome/status where applicable
- integrity evidence where applicable
- source/downstream posture
- lineage where exposed
- non-claims

Body-line admission must refuse any emission that creates source, currentness, authority, permission, successor standing, body formation, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, signal by default, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, or continuation.

## 11. Relation to Cross-Carrier Surface Receipt

Cross-carrier surface receipt is one admitted emission pattern for receiving carriers.

Receipt proves that one carried surface was received as carried evidence under a bounded receipt basis.

Receipt does not create a general emitting carrier.

Receipt does not create multi-carrier law.

Receipt does not create distributed standing.

Receipt does not make the receiving carrier source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, or body.

Receipt does not authorize follow-on work.

Receipt does not decide carrier relation beyond the declared receipt operation.

## 12. Relation to Cross-Surface Correspondence

Cross-surface correspondence may later compare admitted carrier-produced surfaces under a declared correspondence question.

Carrier emission does not automatically create correspondence.

Correspondence does not automatically create carrier relation.

Correspondence cannot decide currentness, source, authority, or permission.

Correspondence may preserve alignment, mismatch, lineage reference, receipt alignment, or refusal visibility where separately declared.

Correspondence may preserve mismatch or refusal visibility without creating authority, currentness, permission, signal, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, successor pressure, or continuation.

## 13. Relation to Cross-Carrier Currentness

This specification does not define cross-carrier currentness.

Carrier role does not create currentness.

Carrier emission does not create currentness.

Returned evidence does not create currentness.

Successful receipt count does not create currentness.

Holding the latest local copy does not create currentness.

Currentness across carriers remains open and requires a separate boundary.

## 14. Relation to Multi-Carrier Law

This specification is not multi-carrier law.

It defines preconditions for later multi-carrier law by bounding carrier roles and emissions.

Multi-carrier law requires separate pressure and basis.

Carrier roles and emissions must be bounded before carrier relation can be defined.

This specification does not authorize multiple carriers to stand in relation by default.

This specification does not create distributed standing, carrier registry, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, carrier participation law, or cross-carrier currentness law.

## 15. Role Misuse / Refusal Conditions

Carrier role or carrier emission must be refused when:

- carrier role is undeclared
- carrier role is unsupported
- carrier claims permanent identity from local role
- carrier claims source from role
- carrier claims currentness from role
- carrier claims authority from role
- carrier claims permission from role
- carrier claims successor standing from role
- carrier claims body formation from role
- carrier emits outside permitted class
- receiving carrier emits self-orientation
- receiving carrier emits conformance
- holding carrier claims receipt
- returning carrier alters receipt meaning
- refusing carrier invalidates source surface
- carrier emission self-admits into body line
- carrier emission creates signal by default
- carrier emission establishes presence
- carrier emission establishes threshold
- carrier emission creates truth
- carrier emission authorizes action
- carrier emission creates consequence
- carrier emission creates multi-carrier law
- carrier emission creates distributed standing
- carrier emission authorizes continuation
- carrier emission uses latest-file recency as currentness
- carrier emission mutates, replays, or merges standing surfaces
- required non-claims are missing or flipped

Blocked role or emission does not invalidate the selected carrier, selected packet, selected surface, or other carrier evidence. It means the requested role or emission is not lawfully recognized under this boundary.

## 20. Closing Boundary Statement

Carrier roles are local to declared operations. Carrier emissions are local outputs permitted only by those roles. Neither carrier role nor carrier emission creates source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, or continuation.

It closes the question:

`What roles may a carrier hold locally, and what may each role emit?`

It does not open the question:

`Do multiple carriers now stand in relation?`
