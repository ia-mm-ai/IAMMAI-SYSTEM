## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_receipt_exhaustion_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether one boundedly recognized source-body reception request may have its recognition record receipted and exhausted for reception-family accounting. It is receipt / exhaustion of recognition accounting only.

`selected_recognition_result` means one source-body reception recognition result whose outcome is `SOURCE_BODY_RECEPTION_RECOGNITION_RECORDED`, whose failed check count is zero, and whose recognition remains bounded for accounting only.

`recognition_record_receipt` means the recognition result has been accounted for as received by the reception-family accounting chain. It does not mean source receipt.

`recognition_record_exhaustion` means the recognized relation's current accounting question is exhausted for the receipt / exhaustion layer only. It does not mean conformance, closure, final completion, or source receipt.

`recognized_reception_accounting_receipt` means the accounting chain has recorded receipt of the recognition record as an accounting object. It is not receipt of the selected source-body surface.

`recognized_reception_accounting_exhaustion` means the accounting chain has exhausted the recognition-record receipt question at this layer only. It does not exhaust conformance, closure, standing, operation permission, or final completion.

`source_receipt` means the source itself was received. This boundary does not record source receipt, create source receipt, or receive source.

`source_received` means the selected source-body surface has been received. This boundary preserves `source_received = false`.

`receipt_exhaustion_not_source_receipt` means receipt / exhaustion of the recognition record is not source receipt, source received, source receipt creation, or reception authorization.

`receipt_exhaustion_not_authorization` means receipt / exhaustion does not authorize reception, source receipt, adoption, validation, invalidation, operation, governance, publication, continuation, or follow-on work.

`receipt_exhaustion_not_conformance` means receipt / exhaustion does not claim, perform, or record reception conformance.

`receipt_exhaustion_not_closure` means receipt / exhaustion does not claim, perform, or record reception closure.

`receipt_exhaustion_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that receipt / exhaustion did not authorize reception, receive source, record source receipt, create source receipt, create governance, make receiving context source/authority/current/receiver/adopter/validator/invalidator/operator, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, standing propagation, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, conformance, closure, mutation, replay, or merge.

`source_receipt_overreach` means treating receipt / exhaustion of the recognition record as source receipt, source received, or source receipt creation.

`completion_overreach` means treating receipt / exhaustion as final completion, public readiness, closure, standing completion, or exhaustion of any boundary beyond recognition-record accounting.

`conformance_overread` means treating receipt / exhaustion as reception conformance or proof that conformance has passed.

`closure_overread` means treating receipt / exhaustion as reception closure or proof that closure has been recorded.

Recognition-record receipt means the recognition result has been accounted for as received by the reception-family accounting chain.

Recognition-record exhaustion means the recognized relation's current accounting question is exhausted for the receipt / exhaustion layer only.

Source receipt means the source itself was received; this boundary does not do that.

Source received must remain false.

`source_receipt_recorded` must remain false.

Receipt / exhaustion of recognition does not mean conformance or closure.
