## 4. Definitions

`second_carrier_receipt_boundary` means a boundary over packet-transfer basis for one future second-carrier receipt step. It is not second-carrier receipt.

`second_carrier_receipt` means a future bounded acknowledgment that a distinct receiving carrier received the bounded transferred packet material, if separately resolved and recorded. It does not occur by this spec.

`receipt_artifact` means a future artifact recording bounded second-carrier receipt, if separately resolved and recorded. It does not exist by this spec.

`receiving_carrier` means a distinct carrier, device, or context that may later acknowledge receipt. Receiving carrier is not source, authority, currentness, runtime, or final completion by possession alone.

`carrier_possession` means a carrier has a copy or path. Possession alone is not receipt, source, authority, currentness, cross-carrier evidence, or runtime.

`copy_presence` means a file or packet copy is visible somewhere. Copy presence alone is not receipt, authority, currentness, proof, or runtime.

`packet_transfer_basis` means the clean packet-transfer artifact and terminal summary selected as basis.

`transferred_packet_basis` means bounded transfer/copy posture basis only.

`receipt_as_execution` is blocked. Receipt cannot become second-carrier execution.

`receipt_as_external_result` is blocked. Receipt cannot become an external result.

`receipt_as_cross_carrier_evidence` is blocked. Receipt cannot become cross-carrier proof or evidence review.

`receipt_as_source_transfer` is blocked. Receipt cannot become source transfer.

`receipt_as_follow_on_work` is blocked. Receipt cannot authorize follow-on work.

`second_carrier_execution` means future execution by the second carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`external_result_artifact` means a future artifact recording an external result, if separately resolved and recorded. It does not exist by this spec.

`cross_carrier_evidence_review` means a future review of evidence across carriers. It does not occur by this spec.
