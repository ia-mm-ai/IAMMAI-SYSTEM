# Cross-Surface Correspondence Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for reading standing surfaces in correspondence within the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

Cross-surface correspondence is a bounded reading relation, not a capability. It permits selected standing surfaces to be compared or read together only while preserving rank, source basis, scope, lineage, outcome locality, and non-claims.

The purpose of this boundary is to prevent the statement "this surface relates to that surface" from becoming source replacement, authority transfer, currentness creation, permission, action, explanation ownership, merge, or continuation.

This file is a boundary specification only. It does not implement a resolver, test, relevance layer, router, event bus, workflow, registry, persistence layer, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, self-orientation successor, conformance successor, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 2. Status and Rank

This specification ranks below constitutional and reference authority surfaces, including the preserved `reference/IAMMAI/` surfaces.

This specification is additive. It does not replace:

- current self-orientation
- current-body conformance
- post-conformance standing closure
- body-signal recognition, acceptance, or scope
- derivative-vessel relation boundary
- executable source
- tests
- emitted artifacts
- current governing/reference surfaces

This specification does not create authority.

This specification does not create permission.

This specification does not create currentness.

This specification does not define final governance.

This specification does not complete continuity.

This specification does not complete final system identity.

This specification is bounded to the present execution line.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

Current self-orientation v6 mirrors current standing posture across upstream current/governing basis and downstream re-entry, body-signal, derivative-vessel relation, derivative vessel, and operator-facing surfaces.

Current-body conformance proves that selected surfaces cohere as one lawful body when its checks pass and integrated non-claims remain false.

Post-conformance standing closure records `BODY_CONFORMANT` as meaning, not permission, currentness, signal, action, completion, or continuation.

The body now has multiple standing surfaces that may need to be read together without flattening them into one source, one rank, one outcome, or one explanation.

Without a correspondence boundary, surface relation could be misread as authority, equivalence, currentness, explanation ownership, merge, or next work. Therefore one bounded cross-surface correspondence boundary is lawful as a protective seam before any future correspondence resolver or manual correspondence review is recognized.

## 4. Definitions

`standing surface` means an artifact or result surface that already has a preserved identity and outcome/status within the present execution line.

`selected surface` means a standing surface explicitly chosen for a bounded correspondence question.

`source basis` means the upstream surface, path, identity, or lawful basis from which a selected surface derives its local posture.

`downstream surface` means a surface that reads, carries, renders, recognizes, scopes, closes, or audits upstream standing without becoming the source of that upstream standing.

`correspondence` means a bounded reading relation between selected surfaces in which alignment, mismatch, dependency, lineage reference, closure alignment, refusal visibility, or non-correspondence may be recorded without changing any surface's rank, source, outcome, scope, or non-claims.

`correspondence basis` means the declared reason, selected surface identities, selected outcomes, source/downstream postures, and non-claims that permit the selected surfaces to be read together.

`correspondence claim` means the bounded assertion that a specific relation type holds between selected surfaces.

`correspondence refusal` means an explicit refusal to recognize a correspondence claim because the selected surfaces, declared question, basis, or non-claims do not support it.

`non-correspondence` means the bounded outcome that selected surfaces do not correspond for the declared question while remaining preserved in their own local posture.

## 5. What Correspondence Means

Cross-surface correspondence means:

- selected surfaces may be read together under one bounded relation question
- each selected surface preserves its own outcome, rank, basis, and scope
- correspondence may identify alignment, mismatch, dependency, bounded relation, lineage reference, closure alignment, refusal visibility, or no correspondence
- correspondence preserves source/downstream distinction
- correspondence preserves lineage
- correspondence preserves local outcome locality
- correspondence preserves non-claims
- correspondence may support future assessment only if separately admitted by a later bounded surface

Correspondence may help a reader or future implementation see whether two standing surfaces fit one declared relation. It does not command work.

## 6. What Correspondence Does Not Mean

Cross-surface correspondence does not mean:

- selected surfaces are equivalent
- selected surfaces are merged
- one surface explains the other by default
- one surface owns explanation of the other
- one surface replaces source
- one surface becomes currentness basis for the other
- one surface authorizes the other
- one surface upgrades the other
- one surface becomes permission
- one surface becomes signal by default
- one surface establishes presence
- one surface establishes threshold
- one surface creates truth
- one surface authorizes action
- one surface creates consequence
- one surface opens next work
- a self-orientation successor is required
- a conformance successor is required
- a workflow, routing layer, or general coordination surface is created

Similarity is not equivalence. Dependency is not authority. Alignment is not merge.

## 7. Required Correspondence Basis

A future implementation or manual review may recognize correspondence only when:

- each selected surface has an explicit identity or path
- each selected surface has an outcome/status
- each selected surface's source/downstream posture is known or explicitly bounded
- the purpose of reading the surfaces together is declared
- the relation type is explicit
- the correspondence does not contradict any selected surface's non-claims
- the correspondence does not widen scope
- the correspondence does not create currentness
- the correspondence does not create permission
- the correspondence does not create authority
- the correspondence does not create signal by default
- the correspondence does not create presence, threshold, truth, action, or consequence
- the correspondence does not merge selected surfaces

If the selected surfaces cannot preserve identity, outcome, rank, source basis, scope, lineage, and non-claims, correspondence must be refused.

## 10. Relation to Self-Orientation

Self-orientation mirrors current posture. It recognizes current/governing basis from upstream surfaces and downstream posture from downstream surfaces.

Cross-surface correspondence does not replace self-orientation. It does not become self-orientation and does not force a self-orientation successor.

Correspondence may later inform self-orientation only if it materially changes current posture and is separately admitted by a bounded self-orientation successor. Self-orientation should not become the metabolizer of every correspondence.

## 11. Relation to Current-Body Conformance

Current-body conformance proves that selected surfaces cohere as one body under a bounded conformance pass.

Cross-surface correspondence may compare selected surfaces, but it is not conformance. It does not imply `BODY_CONFORMANT`. It cannot override a conformance block. It cannot create conformance closure.

Correspondence may preserve that surfaces align or mismatch for a declared question. It does not prove whole-body conformance.

## 12. Relation to Post-Conformance Closure

Post-conformance standing closure records the meaning of `BODY_CONFORMANT`.

Cross-surface correspondence does not reopen conformance closure. It cannot turn conformance into permission, authority, signal, action, completion, or continuation.

Correspondence cannot treat closure as next-work authorization and cannot use closure as a shortcut around the non-meaning preserved by the post-conformance closure surface.

## 13. Relation to Signal

Correspondence is not signal recognition.

A correspondence result is not automatically a signal. Any future signal use must pass body-signal recognition, acceptance, and scope.

Correspondence cannot bypass signal gates. Signal scope may be one selected surface considered in correspondence, but correspondence does not create signal scope, signal presence, signal threshold, signal truth, signal use, or signal routing.

## 14. Relation to Precursor / Historical Artifacts

Precursor or historical artifacts may be read in correspondence only as historical lineage, pressure evidence, or preserved context where their rank is explicit.

Correspondence with precursor artifacts does not upgrade them to current law.

Correspondence with precursor artifacts does not create source authority.

Correspondence with precursor artifacts does not establish truth, presence, threshold, action, or consequence.

Precursor correspondence must preserve historical rank and non-current status.

## 15. Relation to Future Multi-Carrier Law

This specification is not multi-carrier law.

This specification may later provide a boundary primitive for comparing carrier-held surfaces, but multi-carrier law would require separate pressure, separate basis, and separate bounded specification.

Cross-surface correspondence does not create distributed standing.

Cross-surface correspondence does not authorize multiple carriers to relate by default.

Cross-surface correspondence does not create public release, adoption, privileged standing, or generalized vessel relation lifecycle.

## 20. Closing Boundary Statement

Cross-surface correspondence permits bounded reading across standing surfaces only when rank, source, scope, lineage, and non-claims are preserved.
