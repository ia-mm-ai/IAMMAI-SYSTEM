## 4. Definitions

`portable_verification_packet_artifact` means a future local copyable packet object, if separately resolved and recorded, that can become the basis for later packet emission.

`packet_artifact_specification` means this specification posture. It is not the packet artifact.

`packet_artifact_creation` means future local artifact creation. It does not occur by this spec.

`packet_emission` means future outward emission, write, or copy preparation beyond local artifact standing. It does not occur by this spec.

`packet_transfer` means future movement or copying to another carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`copyable_packet_shape` means an explicitly bounded local artifact shape that identifies what can be carried without relying on hidden repo state.

`packet_manifest` means a future packet-level manifest section if separately admitted. It is not created by this spec.

`packet_contents_index` means a future explicitly selected list of packet-contained or packet-referenced items. It is not created by this spec.

`selected_packet_basis` means the bounded basis selected for a future packet artifact.

`copyable_basis` means future packet basis that is explicit, bounded, and not dependent on hidden repo state.

`non_copyable_basis` means any dependency on unlisted files, current working tree, local path assumptions, account state, vendor state, cache, memory, or unstated lineage.

`hidden_repo_state` means any dependency not declared in the packet artifact basis.

`repo_local_availability` means that a file exists locally. This alone is not packet standing, source standing, authority, currentness, or cross-carrier proof.

`packet_as_source` is blocked.

`packet_as_authority` is blocked.

`packet_as_currentness` is blocked.

`packet_as_cross_carrier_proof` is blocked.

`packet_as_runtime` is blocked.

`second_carrier` means a distinct device, local environment, or carrier from the current repository carrier.

`second_carrier_execution` does not occur by this spec.

`external_result_artifact` does not exist by this spec.

`cross_carrier_evidence_review` does not occur by this spec.

`artifact_existence_as_packet_authority` is blocked.

`artifact_path_as_currentness` is blocked.

`raw_full_prior_artifact_body` means the complete body of a prior artifact. It must not be embedded or returned unless a later packet artifact spec/result explicitly admits a bounded copyable body.

`selected_basis_reference_shape` means packet artifact basis remains reference-shaped unless future packet artifact logic explicitly admits bounded copyable material.

`packet_artifact_non_claim` means explicit false posture preventing packet artifact from becoming emission, transfer, receipt, source, authority, currentness, cross-carrier proof, runtime, final completion, continuation, reusable permission, or follow-on work.
