# Receiver-Side Answerable Basis Candidate Sufficiency Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines one minimum boundary that determines only whether the exact selected receiver-side answerable-basis candidate may enter separate candidate-sufficiency consideration after one completed and exhausted eight-dimension evaluation.

It is boundary-spec-only work. It may record `RECEIVER_SIDE_ANSWERABLE_BASIS_CANDIDATE_SUFFICIENCY_CONSIDERATION_ALLOWED`; it does not record candidate sufficiency, candidate insufficiency, candidate indeterminacy, a candidate-sufficiency decision, receiver attestation, receiver answerable receipt, presence support, or downstream standing.

## 2. Scope

This boundary is additive, repo-local, and subordinate to constitutional/reference authority surfaces and to the completed V3 candidate-evaluation result. It consumes that result as upstream standing only. It creates no resolver, test, artifact, candidate-sufficiency result, candidate-sufficiency operation, reception, re-evaluation, identity, relation, coupling, FIELD machinery, runtime, API, public intake, authority, standing, truth, output authorization, action authorization, synchronization authorization, or follow-on authorization.

Candidate evaluation is not candidate sufficiency. All dimensions satisfied is not candidate sufficiency. A candidate-sufficiency boundary is not a candidate-sufficiency operation. Consideration allowed is not sufficiency established. Evaluation exhaustion is not downstream authorization. A permitted future route is not automatic execution.

## 3. Boundary Question

Given the exact selected candidate was lawfully evaluated; all eight required dimensions were evaluated, established, basis-referenced, and resolved `SATISFIED`; no dimension was `NOT_SATISFIED` or `INDETERMINATE`; and the evaluation operation exhausted without creating candidate sufficiency, may candidate-sufficiency consideration now be admitted?

## 4. Required Answer

Yes, but only as one separately bounded candidate-sufficiency consideration boundary. The allowed boundary result is `RECEIVER_SIDE_ANSWERABLE_BASIS_CANDIDATE_SUFFICIENCY_CONSIDERATION_ALLOWED`; it is not a candidate-sufficiency decision and does not establish candidate sufficient, insufficient, or indeterminate.

## 11. Admission Rule

Candidate-sufficiency consideration may be allowed only when the exact selected candidate identity matches; the exact completed V3 evaluation artifact is selected; the operation completed and exhausted; all eight dimensions are present, evaluated, established, and `SATISFIED`; no dimension is `NOT_SATISFIED` or `INDETERMINATE`; no candidate-sufficiency result or candidate-sufficiency boundary already exists; no prohibited downstream request is present; `failed_check_count` is zero; and all constitutional non-claims remain false.

An allowed boundary records only consideration admission. It does not create or execute a candidate-sufficiency operation, schedule it, authorize it, or select a later receiver-attestation, receiver-answerable-receipt, or presence route.

## 12. Blocking Rule

The boundary must block when required upstream posture is absent, malformed, mismatched, pre-claimed, not satisfied, indeterminate, incomplete, unexhausted, or already converted into candidate sufficiency. It must also block prohibited downstream conversion, repeated or reusable boundary posture, rerun posture, and contaminated-lineage validation.

A blocked boundary is not candidate insufficiency.
