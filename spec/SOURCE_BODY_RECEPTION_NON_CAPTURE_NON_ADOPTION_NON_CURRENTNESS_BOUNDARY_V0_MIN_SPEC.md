## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_non_capture_non_adoption_non_currentness_boundary` means a bounded review surface that checks whether one eligible / admissible source-body reception request preserves the selected source-body surface from capture, adoption, and currentness before any reception recognition boundary is reviewed.

`selected_eligibility_admissibility_result` means one source-body reception eligibility / admissibility result whose outcome is `SOURCE_BODY_RECEPTION_ELIGIBLE_ADMISSIBLE_FOR_REVIEW` and whose failed check count is zero.

`non_capture` means the selected source-body surface remains uncaptured by receiving context, eligibility / admissibility posture, receiving-context role posture, identity preservation posture, request declaration posture, closure artifacts, terminal summaries, latest file posture, carrier possession, registry reference, or narrative convenience.

`non_adoption` means no adoption has been created and no boundary, result, context, role, artifact, or review-readiness posture is treated as adopting the selected source-body surface.

`non_currentness` means no currentness has been created and no boundary, result, context, role, artifact, or review-readiness posture is treated as making the selected source-body surface current or making the receiving context current.

`capture_risk` means any risk that the receiving context or surrounding artifacts are treated as source, source replacement, authority, currentness, receiver, adopter, validator, invalidator, operator, vessel, derivative, governance, operation permission, publication flow, public readiness, final completion, continuation, or follow-on authority.

`adoption_risk` means any risk that review-readiness, eligibility, admissibility, receiving-context role, identity preservation, request declaration, carrier possession, or artifact visibility is treated as adoption of source.

`currentness_risk` means any risk that the selected source-body surface, receiving context, role result, eligibility result, registry reference, carrier context, latest artifact, or surrounding narrative is treated as current by convenience.

`authority_capture` means any risk that a context, role, eligibility result, identity result, declaration result, artifact, carrier, registry, or narrative posture is treated as authority over source-body reception, source validation, source invalidation, adoption, currentness, operation, publication, or follow-on work.

`validation_capture` means any risk that the receiving context or surrounding artifact is treated as validating the selected source-body surface.

`invalidation_capture` means any risk that the receiving context or surrounding artifact is treated as invalidating the selected source-body surface.

`source_replacement_capture` means any risk that the selected source-body surface is replaced by receiving context, request declaration, identity preservation result, receiving-context role result, eligibility / admissibility result, distributed-operation closure artifact, terminal summary, latest artifact, carrier possession, registry reference, or narrative convenience.

`operation_permission_capture` means any risk that non-capture review or an upstream review posture is treated as operation permission, admission, execution, emission, action authorization, consequence creation, or reusable permission.

`governance_capture` means any risk that receiving context or surrounding artifacts acquire governance force by review-readiness, visibility, possession, reference, or convenience.

`publication_flow_capture` means any risk that non-capture review or an upstream review posture opens publication flow, public readiness, public launch readiness, final completion, continuation, or follow-on work.

`recognition_overread` means treating non-capture passed, non-adoption passed, non-currentness passed, or eligibility / admissibility as source-body reception recognition or as proof that recognition has already passed.

`non_capture_passed` means capture risk was checked and refused. It does not mean reception was recognized.

`non_adoption_passed` means adoption risk was checked and refused. It does not create adoption.

`non_currentness_passed` means currentness risk was checked and refused. It does not create currentness.

`non_capture_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that non-capture review did not recognize reception, authorize reception, receive source, claim recognition passed, create receiving-context governance, make receiving context source/authority/current/receiver/adopter/validator/invalidator/operator/vessel/derivative, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, standing propagation, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, mutation, replay, or merge.

Non-capture means the selected source-body surface remains uncaptured by receiving context or surrounding artifacts.

Non-adoption means no adoption has been created.

Non-currentness means no currentness has been created.

Passed means capture, adoption, and currentness risks were checked and refused, not that reception was recognized.

Non-capture passed does not mean reception recognition passed.
