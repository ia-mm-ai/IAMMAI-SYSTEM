# Matter-Bound Selected-Surface Correspondence V0 Minimum V2 Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines one minimum non-executable boundary for reconstructing one declared correspondence relation among explicitly selected, already-standing surfaces for one explicit matter inside one `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` custody.

The minimum reconstruction unit is exactly:

- one explicit matter;
- one explicit closed selected-surface manifest;
- one declared correspondence question;
- one supported declared relation type; and
- one bounded completion or stopping posture.

Correspondence reconstruction is the bounded function. Body self-legibility is only a possible consequence and is not established by this specification.

For this boundary, self-legibility means only that a later bounded reader may be able to inspect the declared relation among the selected surfaces. It is not consciousness, self-awareness, identity, agency, sentience, biological status, Presence, authority, permission, or standing.

This specification permits a future bounded reconstruction to make visible recognized correspondence, mismatch, conflict, refusal, non-correspondence, missing support, unsupported relation type, or blocked reconstruction without collapsing those relation postures into the top-level outcome.

Successful agreement is not required. A lawfully supported mismatch, conflict, refusal, or declared non-correspondence may be a recognized relation and does not invalidate any selected surface.

This file is non-executable and read-only in constitutional effect. It does not implement a resolver, request, result, receipt, test, runtime, live reconstruction, registry, catalogue, current-state surface, workflow, daemon, action, or continuation.

## 5. Definitions

`explicit matter` means one declared matter identity, purpose, scope, and bounded question context. It is not a generic topic, repository-wide theme, or inferred current concern.

`selected-surface manifest` means one ordered, closed list of two or more explicitly selected standing surfaces for the exact matter and reconstruction only.

`selected surface` means one already-standing surface named by exact identity and exact held reference in the manifest.

`exact held reference` means an explicitly supplied stable repository path or other already-supported exact reference. It is not a discovered sibling, latest filename, timestamp choice, sequence-number choice, directory-position inference, or repository-availability inference.

`declared correspondence question` means the one explicit question binding the exact matter, selected surface identities, one supported relation type, direction where relevant, bounded claim, and exact basis to be reconstructed.

`declared relation type` means the one admitted relation the reconstruction asks whether the exact selected basis supports. It is not the top-level outcome.

`correspondence reconstruction` means one bounded reading of the one declared question against the one selected manifest while preserving every selected surface's local standing.

`normalized claim-level posture` means only the minimum referenced claim, outcome, scope, rank, lineage, source/downstream relation, and non-claim values needed for the one declared question. It excludes complete artifact bodies and recursively reproduced ancestry.

`outcome locality` means the finding applies only to the exact matter, selected manifest, declared question, declared relation type, and basis. It does not rewrite any selected surface's outcome.

`missing support` means the selected basis is readable and otherwise sufficient for lawful completion but does not support the exact declared relation. It is not proof that a selected surface is invalid.

`unsupported relation type` means a requested relation type outside the closed family admitted by the governing correspondence boundary.

`bounded completion or stopping posture` means exactly one top-level `CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED`, `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`, or `BLOCKED` posture for the one reconstruction.

## 6. Singular Reconstruction Unit

One reconstruction may contain exactly one declared correspondence question and one declared relation type.

It must not aggregate multiple declared questions, mixed question-level findings, majority logic, precedence logic among questions, or a composite conformance-like conclusion.

If another question is needed, it requires a separate reconstruction with its own exact matter binding, selected manifest, declared question, declared relation type, exact basis, and bounded completion or stopping posture. The earlier reconstruction creates no permission, reusable basis, scheduling, or automatic continuation for another.

This singular unit prevents:

- one successful relation from hiding a different mismatch;
- one blocked question from contaminating or deciding another;
- majority or precedence logic among distinct questions;
- implicit aggregation into conformance, coherence, or self-orientation; and
- a multi-question reconstruction from becoming a miniature whole-body pass.

## 10. Relation-to-Outcome Semantics

Any future implementation must reuse exactly this top-level outcome family:

- `CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED`
- `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`
- `BLOCKED`

`CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED` means the exact readable admitted basis supports the exact declared relation type under the exact matter and selected manifest. It does not mean the surfaces agree, conform, cohere, merge, validate one another, or become equivalent.

A lawfully supported `BASIS_MISMATCH`, `SCOPE_MISMATCH`, `NON_CLAIM_CONFLICT`, `REFUSAL_VISIBLE`, or declared `NO_CORRESPONDENCE` relation therefore completes with top-level `CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED`.

The token `NO_CORRESPONDENCE` has two distinct roles:

- as a declared relation type, it asks whether the basis supports the relation that no correspondence holds for the bounded claim;
- as a top-level outcome, it means the otherwise lawful and sufficient reconstruction basis did not support the exact declared relation being reconstructed.

If the exact readable basis supports declared relation type `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`, that relation was successfully recognized and the top-level outcome is `CORRESPONDENCE_RECOGNIZED`.

Top-level `NO_CORRESPONDENCE` means the readable admitted basis was lawful and sufficient to complete but did not support the exact declared relation. Missing support for the declared relation may produce top-level `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`. It does not delete, reject, downgrade, or invalidate the matter or any selected surface.

`BLOCKED` means the requested reconstruction could not lawfully complete because the relation type was unsupported, required selection or basis was missing or malformed, scope widened, a prohibited conversion was requested, non-claims failed, or the request was otherwise collapse-shaped.

Mismatch is not invalidation. Conflict is not failure. Refusal visibility is not mandatory participation. Non-correspondence is not deletion or rejection.

## 11. Required Reconstruction Checks

A future resolver or manual review may complete one reconstruction only when:

- the matter is explicit and bounded;
- exactly one selected-surface manifest is declared;
- the manifest contains at least two already-standing surfaces;
- every selected surface has exact identity and exact held reference;
- every selected surface's local outcome or posture is present;
- rank, source basis, scope, lineage, and applicable non-claims are preserved;
- manifest order preserves identity and reproducibility only;
- exactly one declared question names the exact matter and selected surfaces it uses;
- exactly one declared relation type is supported;
- the exact basis needed for that declared relation is present and readable;
- source/downstream distinction is preserved where relevant;
- no selected surface is replaced, merged, upgraded, or treated as equivalent by selection alone;
- no scope widens beyond the exact matter, manifest, or declared question;
- no currentness, rank, priority, completeness, or coverage authority is inferred;
- no result is selected by filename, timestamp, sequence number, directory position, availability, or recency;
- no complete artifact body or recursive ancestry is embedded;
- all required non-claims remain false; and
- exactly one bounded completion or stopping posture is recorded.

Detailed selected basis must prevail over summary projection where they differ.

## 12. Refusal and Stopping Conditions

Reconstruction must stop or block when:

- the matter is missing, vague, generic, or unbounded;
- the selected-surface manifest is missing, open-ended, duplicated, or malformed;
- fewer than two surfaces are selected;
- a selected surface is missing, malformed, or not already standing;
- exact identity, exact reference, local outcome, rank, source basis, scope, lineage, or applicable non-claims cannot be preserved;
- no declared question is supplied or more than one declared question is placed in one reconstruction;
- the declared question is not bound to the exact matter and manifest;
- no relation type is declared or more than one relation type is placed in one reconstruction;
- the declared relation type is unsupported;
- a claimed source/downstream relation is unavailable or contradictory;
- reconstruction would widen matter, manifest, surface, or question scope;
- reconstruction would hide mismatch, conflict, refusal, non-correspondence, or missing support;
- reconstruction would treat a negative-sounding supported relation as automatic top-level `NO_CORRESPONDENCE`;
- reconstruction would treat surface similarity as equivalence;
- reconstruction would create source replacement, authority, permission, currentness, standing upgrade, merge, action, consequence, or continuation;
- reconstruction would infer meaning from recency, manifest position, or repository-local availability;
- reconstruction would turn the manifest into a registry, catalogue, inventory, search surface, or coverage authority;
- reconstruction would embed complete selected artifacts or recursively reproduce ancestry;
- reconstruction would create cross-custody relation, answerability, FIELD jurisdiction, Presence, Threshold, Truth, coherence, conformance, self-orientation succession, or conformance succession; or
- required non-claims are missing or flipped.

Blocked reconstruction does not invalidate the matter or any selected surface. It records only that the requested reconstruction could not lawfully complete under the declared basis.
