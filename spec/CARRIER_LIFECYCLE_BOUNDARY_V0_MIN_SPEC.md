# Carrier Lifecycle Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded boundary for carrier lifecycle posture in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

The purpose is to prevent carrier availability, staleness, corruption, replacement, withdrawal, or reintroduction from becoming currentness, source replacement, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, evidence erasure, distributed standing, repository synchronization, carrier registry, full body transfer, second-body creation, continuation, or distributed operation.

Carrier lifecycle is not registry.

Carrier lifecycle is not currentness.

Carrier lifecycle is not hierarchy.

Carrier lifecycle is not standing.

Carrier lifecycle is not distributed standing.

Carrier lifecycle records what may be said about a carrier's bounded posture over time.

Lifecycle status may affect future reliance, refusal, review, reintroduction, or exclusion under a declared basis. It may not mutate prior evidence, erase refusal, replace source, or make a carrier current.

Core rule: carrier lifecycle status may preserve the posture of a carrier for bounded future reliance or refusal. It may not create currentness, source replacement, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, registry authority, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, continuation, or evidence erasure.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization, distributed standing, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, carrier fleet management, network topology, consensus system, leader election, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`carrier lifecycle` means the bounded description of one carrier's posture over time for one declared lifecycle question or transition.

`carrier lifecycle status` means one declared status from the minimal lifecycle set in this specification. It is not currentness, registry authority, carrier hierarchy, or distributed standing.

`lifecycle basis` means the declared lifecycle question, carrier identity, prior status where available, requested status, related evidence where applicable, transition basis where applicable, visible refusal, visible divergence, visible corruption or staleness where applicable, and explicit non-claims.

`lifecycle claim` means the bounded assertion that one carrier has one lifecycle status for one declared lifecycle question.

`lifecycle refusal` means refusal to record lifecycle status because the request is missing, malformed, unsupported, evidence-erasing, source-replacing, currentness-creating, hierarchy-creating, distributed-standing-shaped, continuation-authorizing, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`carrier stale posture` means a carrier holds or refers to an older posture, packet, evidence, or basis. Stale does not mean invalid by default and cannot be used as currentness.

`carrier unavailable posture` means a carrier cannot be reached or used for the declared operation. Unavailable does not mean refusal by default and does not erase prior evidence.

`carrier corrupted posture` means carrier evidence, packet, or integrity basis is malformed, hash-mismatched, visibly damaged, or otherwise integrity-failed. Corruption must remain visible as corruption evidence.

`carrier withdrawn posture` means a carrier is removed from future operation scope under a declared basis. Withdrawn does not erase prior evidence.

`carrier replaced posture` means one carrier is replaced for future operations by another declared carrier under explicit replacement basis. Replacement does not transfer source, currentness, authority, role, evidence, or standing by default.

`carrier reintroduced posture` means a carrier previously stale, unavailable, corrupted, withdrawn, replaced, or retired is considered or admitted again for a declared future role or evidence boundary under explicit basis. Reintroduced does not restore old authority, currentness, or role.

`carrier active-for-operation posture` means a carrier is bounded to one declared operation under a declared role and basis. Active-for-operation does not mean current carrier.

`lifecycle evidence` means selected carrier evidence, carrier status evidence, admission evidence, receipt or refusal evidence, divergence evidence, currentness participation evidence, relation evidence, or integrity evidence used as basis for lifecycle status.

`lifecycle non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that lifecycle did not create source, currentness, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, registry, synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, distributed operation, evidence erasure, hidden refusal, hidden divergence, or repair by overwrite.

Lifecycle participant does not mean body participant.

Lifecycle status does not mean currentness.

Lifecycle status does not erase prior carrier evidence.

## 6. What Lifecycle Status Means

Lifecycle status means:

- one carrier has a bounded posture for a declared lifecycle question
- prior evidence remains preserved
- future reliance may be permitted, excluded, or blocked only under declared basis
- lifecycle state may inform future admission, divergence, currentness, relation, or experiment boundaries
- lifecycle state remains downstream evidence unless separately admitted
- lifecycle status is operation-bound or context-bound unless a future registry boundary says otherwise
- visible refusal remains visible where applicable
- visible divergence remains visible where applicable
- visible corruption remains visible where applicable
- lineage between prior status and requested status remains preserved where applicable
- lifecycle non-claims remain explicit and false

Lifecycle status records bounded posture. It does not convert carrier condition into force.

## 8. Status-Specific Rules

### A. `CARRIER_UNAVAILABLE`

`CARRIER_UNAVAILABLE` means the carrier cannot be reached or used for the declared operation.

Unavailable is not refusal by default.

Unavailable does not erase prior evidence.

Unavailable does not create currentness in another carrier.

Unavailable may block future reliance for the declared operation only when the lifecycle basis says so.

### B. `CARRIER_STALE`

`CARRIER_STALE` means the carrier holds or refers to an older posture, packet, evidence, or basis.

Stale does not mean invalid by default.

Stale cannot be used as currentness.

Stale evidence may require revalidation before reliance.

Stale status must preserve the older basis rather than overwrite it with a newer narrative.

### C. `CARRIER_CORRUPTED`

`CARRIER_CORRUPTED` means carrier evidence, packet, or integrity basis is malformed, hash-mismatched, visibly damaged, or otherwise integrity-failed.

Corrupted evidence must remain visible as corruption evidence.

Corruption does not authorize mutation or repair by overwrite.

Corruption does not erase prior valid evidence.

Corruption may block future reliance on the corrupted evidence without invalidating unrelated evidence by default.

### D. `CARRIER_WITHDRAWN`

`CARRIER_WITHDRAWN` means a carrier is removed from future operation scope by declared basis.

Withdrawn does not erase prior evidence.

Withdrawn does not invalidate prior relation, conformance, or closure by default.

Withdrawn cannot silently transfer role to another carrier.

Withdrawn does not create successor carrier status.

### E. `CARRIER_REPLACED`

`CARRIER_REPLACED` means one carrier is replaced for future operations by another declared carrier.

Replacement does not inherit source, currentness, authority, role, evidence, or standing by default.

Replacement must preserve lineage between old carrier and replacement carrier.

Replacement cannot be treated as the same carrier unless separately bounded.

Replacement does not erase the old carrier's evidence or visible refusal, divergence, staleness, or corruption.

### F. `CARRIER_REINTRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`

`CARRIER_REINTRODUCTION_CANDIDATE` means a stale, unavailable, withdrawn, corrupted, or retired carrier may be considered for reintroduction.

Reintroduction is not automatic.

Reintroduction candidate status must preserve prior status, gap, and basis.

Reintroduction candidate status does not restore role, authority, currentness, or prior operation scope.

### G. `CARRIER_REINTRODUCED`

`CARRIER_REINTRODUCED` means a carrier may be reintroduced only under declared basis and checks.

Reintroduced does not mean restored to old authority, currentness, or role.

Reintroduced may only participate under a newly declared role or evidence boundary.

Reintroduced does not absorb its prior posture without review.

### H. `CARRIER_RETIRED`

`CARRIER_RETIRED` means the carrier is no longer available for future lifecycle use under the present line.

Retired does not erase prior evidence.

Retired does not create successor carrier by default.

Retired does not invalidate prior admitted evidence, divergence evidence, relation posture, conformance, or closure by default.

## 9. Lifecycle Transitions

Future lifecycle transitions may include:

- `CARRIER_CANDIDATE` to `CARRIER_DECLARED_FOR_EXPERIMENT`
- `CARRIER_DECLARED_FOR_EXPERIMENT` to `CARRIER_ACTIVE_FOR_OPERATION`
- `CARRIER_ACTIVE_FOR_OPERATION` to `CARRIER_RETURNED_EVIDENCE`
- `CARRIER_ACTIVE_FOR_OPERATION` to `CARRIER_REFUSED_OR_BLOCKED`
- `CARRIER_ACTIVE_FOR_OPERATION` to `CARRIER_UNAVAILABLE`
- `CARRIER_HOLDING_EVIDENCE` to `CARRIER_STALE`
- `CARRIER_HOLDING_EVIDENCE` to `CARRIER_CORRUPTED`
- `CARRIER_STALE` to `CARRIER_REINTRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`
- `CARRIER_UNAVAILABLE` to `CARRIER_REINTRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`
- `CARRIER_WITHDRAWN` to `CARRIER_REINTRODUCTION_CANDIDATE`
- `CARRIER_REINTRODUCTION_CANDIDATE` to `CARRIER_REINTRODUCED`
- any active or candidate carrier to `CARRIER_WITHDRAWN`
- any carrier to `CARRIER_RETIRED`
- carrier to `CARRIER_REPLACED`, only with explicit replacement basis

Each transition must preserve:

- previous status
- new status
- transition basis
- evidence affected
- visible refusal where applicable
- visible divergence where applicable
- visible corruption where applicable
- visible staleness where applicable
- non-claims
- no source creation or source replacement
- no currentness creation
- no authority creation
- no permission creation
- no evidence erasure
- no distributed standing creation

Transition recording is not carrier registry. It is bounded lifecycle evidence only.

## 11. Carrier Evidence Preservation

Prior carrier evidence remains preserved when lifecycle status changes.

Stale evidence remains stale evidence, not erased evidence.

Unavailable carrier evidence remains prior evidence.

Corrupted evidence remains visible corruption evidence.

Withdrawn carrier evidence remains historical evidence.

Replaced carrier evidence remains linked to the original carrier, not silently moved.

Reintroduced carrier does not absorb its prior posture without review.

Carrier lifecycle may mark evidence as requiring revalidation, blocked for a declared reliance question, or excluded from a declared operation. It may not mutate, overwrite, merge, replay, or erase that evidence.

Successful receipt evidence does not erase blocked receipt evidence.

Blocked receipt evidence does not invalidate successful receipt evidence by default.

Lifecycle status must preserve B/C visible divergence where applicable.

## 12. Relation to Additional Physical Carrier Experiment Boundary

An additional physical carrier experiment may create lifecycle pressure for a new carrier.

Experiment declaration does not create general lifecycle registry.

Experiment success or failure does not create permanent lifecycle role.

Carrier C's blocked receipt evidence may inform future lifecycle status only under this boundary.

Lifecycle review does not retroactively authorize the experiment.

Lifecycle review does not execute any additional physical carrier experiment.

## 13. Relation to Carrier Role and Emission Boundary

Lifecycle status is distinct from carrier role.

Carrier role is operation-local.

Lifecycle status does not grant role rights.

Lifecycle status does not authorize new emissions.

Unsupported role or unsupported emission should block before lifecycle reliance where relevant.

A carrier may be active for a lifecycle question while still lacking any role in a later receipt, admission, divergence, currentness, or relation operation.

## 14. Relation to Carrier-Local Emission Admission

Admitted carrier evidence may inform lifecycle status.

Lifecycle status does not admit evidence by itself.

Returned evidence remains not admitted unless separately admitted.

Admission as evidence does not create lifecycle status automatically.

Lifecycle status may cite admission posture as basis only when the admission identity, outcome, and non-claims are preserved.

## 15. Relation to Cross-Carrier Divergence

Divergence may inform lifecycle status, such as stale, corrupted, refused, or blocked posture.

Lifecycle status does not resolve divergence.

Lifecycle status cannot pick a winning carrier.

Lifecycle status cannot invalidate losing evidence.

Hidden divergence blocks lifecycle status where relevant.

Lifecycle status must preserve visible B/C divergence where selected or applicable.

## 16. Relation to Cross-Carrier Currentness

Lifecycle status does not create currentness.

Active carrier does not mean current carrier.

Stale, unavailable, corrupted, withdrawn, replaced, reintroduced, or retired status cannot make another carrier current.

Currentness participation remains separate.

Carrier C currentness participation remains participation only. It does not become currentness through lifecycle posture.

No lifecycle status may rely on majority, success count, latest file, availability, convenience, carrier label, or physical proximity as currentness.

## 17. Relation to Multi-Carrier Relation

Lifecycle status may be evidence in later relation review.

Relation does not create lifecycle status by itself.

Lifecycle does not create relation by itself.

Lifecycle does not expand relation scope.

Lifecycle does not create carrier hierarchy.

B/C divergence-bounded relation, relation conformance, and relation conformance closure remain preserved as downstream relation posture. Lifecycle status may not convert that posture into distributed standing or currentness.

## 18. Relation to Distributed Standing

Carrier lifecycle boundary does not create distributed standing.

Distributed standing remains open.

Lifecycle status may be prerequisite evidence for future distributed standing only if separately admitted.

Lifecycle does not authorize persistence, registry, synchronization, full body transfer, repository synchronization, second-body creation, or distributed operation.

The body does not stand across carriers because lifecycle status exists.

Carrier lifecycle is narrower than carrier registry and narrower than distributed standing.

## 24. Closing Boundary Statement

Carrier lifecycle status may be recorded only as bounded posture over one carrier or carrier transition. It preserves prior evidence and visible refusal, divergence, and corruption while refusing source replacement, currentness, authority, hierarchy, distributed standing, registry authority, synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, or distributed operation.

It closes the question:

`What may be said about a carrier over time without collapse?`

It does not open the question:

`Does the body now stand across carriers?`
