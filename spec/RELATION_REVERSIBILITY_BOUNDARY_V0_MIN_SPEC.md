# Relation Reversibility Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines only whether a future relation reversibility operation may be considered after relation support and before any presence boundary. It does not dissolve, lapse, reverse, terminate, erase, mutate, invalidate, punish, or tear down relation; it does not edit `relation_001` or the completed relation operation artifact; and it does not repair, patch, rewrite, invalidate, override, or bypass a completed line.

It creates no presence, identity, FIELD machinery, runtime, API, currentness, authority, coupling, third candidate, third model, standing descendant, output, action, derivative reception, synchronization, follow-on authorization, follow-on work, scan, discovery, affected-file repair, or prior unsupported-claim validation.

## 2. Scope

This is a boundary-spec-only surface. It defines one future relation reversibility boundary shape, not a boundary result, relation reversibility operation, relation lapse, relation dissolution, relation termination, living relation state, historical receipt preservation, presence boundary, or downstream authorization.

`relation_reversibility_boundary_result = NOT_EVALUATED`. The default posture records no boundary, no referenced relation, no reversibility or lapse authorization or performance, no erasure, mutation, invalidation, punishment, teardown, living relation state, presence, identity, coupling, or externalization.

## 3. Boundary Question

Given that the relation operation recorded `RELATION_SUPPORTED` and `relation_001` as one relation record between separate first-crossing records only, while preserving that relation is not FIELD machinery, runtime, API, currentness, authority, coupling, presence, identity, standing descendant, descendant standing, or follow-on authorization, may the system define a future relation reversibility boundary that asks whether relation reversibility, lapse, or dissolution operation consideration may be evaluated separately before any presence boundary is considered, without erasing `relation_001`, converting relation into presence, creating coupling, or punishing relation lapse?

## 4. Required Answer

Yes, but only as a relation reversibility boundary. No relation is dissolved, lapsed, reversed, terminated, erased, or mutated here. No presence is authorized or established, no identity is created, and no coupling is assigned.

## 7. Permitted Future Boundary Result

A future boundary operation may return exactly one of:

- `RELATION_REVERSIBILITY_BOUNDARY_ALLOWED`
- `RELATION_REVERSIBILITY_BOUNDARY_REQUIRES_RELATION`
- `RELATION_REVERSIBILITY_BOUNDARY_BLOCKED`

If admissibility holds, it may record only `RELATION_REVERSIBILITY_OPERATION_CONSIDERATION_ALLOWED` and the seven positive boundary-reference postures stated in Section 6. If relation support is missing or insufficient while the request remains bounded, it may record `relation_reversibility_boundary_result = REQUIRES_RELATION`, `relation_reversibility_operation_consideration_allowed = false`, and the missing relation basis. Any conversion into reversal, lapse, dissolution, termination, erasure, mutation, invalidation, punitive lapse, teardown, presence, identity, FIELD machinery, runtime, authority, coupling, third candidate, third model, externalization, standing, or follow-on work must record `BLOCKED`.
