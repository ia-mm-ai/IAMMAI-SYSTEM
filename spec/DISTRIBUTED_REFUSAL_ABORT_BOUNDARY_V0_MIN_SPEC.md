## 4. Definitions

`distributed refusal / abort boundary` means the bounded review surface that defines refusal, abort, stop, and non-admission postures before any future operation admission.

`selected sync/non-sync boundary result` means the distributed synchronization / non-synchronization boundary result selected for this review. It remains boundary posture only and does not authorize synchronization or non-synchronized operation.

`refusal posture` means a declared condition under which admission, continuation, reliance, or operation must be refused in a future admission / transition review. It is not live refusal execution.

`abort posture` means a declared condition under which a future admitted operation would have to stop or fail closed. It is not live abort execution here because no operation has been admitted.

`stop condition` means a bounded condition that must halt future admission, transition, or operation if encountered under a later lawful boundary.

`non-admission condition` means a condition that prevents operation admission unless later separately resolved under a lawful boundary.

`refusal trigger` means a declared event, missing basis, hidden evidence posture, overread, or collapse attempt that would require refusal before future admission.

`abort trigger` means a declared expansion, sync attempt, role activation, authority creation, evidence erasure, consequence creation, or other collapse attempt that would require abort in a later admitted operation context.

`blocked carrier refusal` means the preserved Carrier C block, refusal, or blocked-attempt context. It remains visible evidence and must not be treated as invalidation or cleanup.

`carrier refusal preservation` means the posture that carrier refusal, blocked attempt, divergence, and projection mismatch remain inspectable and cannot be erased by success on another carrier.

`operation overcommit` means treating eligibility, authority basis, role basis, sync/non-sync posture, carrier success, or readiness as if operation has already been admitted, authorized, or made unavoidable.

`refusal / abort awareness` means explicit preservation that future admission and operation must remain refuse-able, abortable, and bounded by non-admission conditions.

`refusal / abort non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that no live refusal, live abort, admission, authorization, execution, synchronization, transfer, second body, consequence, final completion, public readiness, or follow-on work is created here.

Refusal/abort review does not refuse a live operation.

Refusal/abort review does not abort a live operation.

Refusal/abort review does not admit operation.

Refusal/abort review defines conditions that a later admission boundary must preserve.
