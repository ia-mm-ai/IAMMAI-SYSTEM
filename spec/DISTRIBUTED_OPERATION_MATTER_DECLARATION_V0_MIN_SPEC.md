# Distributed Operation Matter Declaration V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines distributed operation matter declaration for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

Distributed operation matter declaration is the first lawful gate before distributed operation eligibility. It may name one candidate distributed operation as a bounded matter for future eligibility review.

The purpose is to prevent distributed standing from turning directly into operation. Before any eligibility boundary can be considered, one declared operation candidate and one declared matter must exist, with a declared question, declared purpose, selected basis, selected carrier context where applicable, proposed affected surfaces, proposed output family, refusal/abort awareness, and explicit non-claims.

This file is a matter-declaration specification only. It does not define distributed operation eligibility. It does not admit, authorize, execute, synchronize, transfer, continue, or produce consequence.

Matter declaration is not eligibility.

Matter declaration is not admission.

Matter declaration is not operation.

Matter declaration is not execution.

Matter declaration is not synchronization.

Matter declaration is not full body transfer.

Matter declaration is not continuation.

Matter declaration is not permission.

Matter declaration only declares a candidate matter so a future eligibility boundary may review it.

## 4. Definitions

`distributed_operation_matter_declaration` means a bounded declaration that names one distributed operation candidate as one matter for future eligibility review. It is not eligibility, admission, authorization, execution, synchronization, transfer, continuation, or consequence.

`distributed_operation_candidate` means the named candidate operation that may later be reviewed by a separate eligibility boundary. The candidate is visible only as a candidate.

`operation_matter` means the bounded matter record that holds the candidate, question, purpose, proposed kind, selected basis, selected carrier context where applicable, proposed affected surfaces, proposed output family, eligibility review request, refusal/abort awareness, and non-claims.

`declared_operation_question` means the explicit question the candidate raises for future eligibility review. It is not an eligibility decision.

`declared_operation_purpose` means the stated reason for declaring the candidate matter. It is not permission to perform the candidate.

`proposed_operation_kind` means the bounded candidate kind assigned to the matter. It is not authorization, admission, or execution.

`selected_operation_basis` means the selected source-body basis, current body orientation/conformance basis, distributed standing basis where applicable, and read-only grounding surfaces used to identify the matter. The basis is not a permission set and not an operation plan.

`selected_carrier_context` means selected carrier evidence or carrier posture context where applicable. It may reference Carrier B success, Carrier C block, B/C divergence, refusal, blocked attempts, and projection mismatch. It does not select current carrier, winning carrier, or losing carrier.

`proposed_affected_surfaces` means the bounded surfaces the candidate proposes to inspect or affect if a future eligibility boundary later admits review. Listing affected surfaces does not authorize any effect.

`proposed_output_family` means the bounded family of outputs the candidate might request if future eligibility, admission, and execution are separately authorized. Naming an output family does not authorize output.

`eligibility_review_request` means the request that a future eligibility boundary may review the declared matter. It does not decide eligibility and does not schedule the review.

`refusal_abort_awareness` means explicit awareness that later eligibility, admission, and operation must preserve refusal and abort conditions before any operation may proceed.

`operation_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that matter declaration does not admit, authorize, execute, synchronize, transfer, continue, select carriers, create currentness, create authority, create permission, create truth/action, produce consequence, claim final completion, create public readiness, or schedule follow-on work.

Matter declaration does not create the operation it names. Matter declaration does not authorize next work by itself. Matter declaration does not mutate prior surfaces.

## 5. Matter Declaration Required Elements

A valid future matter declaration must include:

- declared matter id
- declared operation candidate id
- declared operation question
- declared operation purpose
- proposed operation kind
- selected source-body basis
- selected current body orientation/conformance basis
- selected distributed standing basis where applicable
- selected carrier context where applicable
- proposed affected surfaces
- proposed output family
- requested future eligibility review
- declared refusal/abort awareness
- declared non-claims

Each required element must be declared as a bounded matter-declaration field. None of these elements may be inferred from recency, carrier possession, successful receipt count, majority, availability, registry presence, lifecycle posture, summary projection, or narrative convenience.

## 7. What Matter Declaration May Mean

Matter declaration may mean:

- one distributed operation candidate has been named
- one matter has been declared
- the body may later test eligibility only if separately implemented and bounded
- the proposed operation is visible as a candidate, not admitted
- refusal/abort awareness has been declared
- proposed output family has been named but not authorized
- selected carriers may be referenced as context, not operational participants
- selected basis surfaces may be referenced as basis, not permission

Matter declaration may make a future eligibility question inspectable. It does not answer that question.

## 13. Refusal and Abort Awareness

Matter declaration must include refusal/abort awareness.

At minimum, refusal/abort awareness must declare:

- awareness that operation may later be blocked
- awareness that eligibility may fail
- awareness that carrier refusal must remain visible
- awareness that operation admission, if ever considered, must include abort/refusal conditions
- awareness that no operation may proceed without later refusal/abort boundary
- awareness that abort/refusal is not late cleanup but part of operation law

Refusal/abort awareness is not an abort mechanism by itself. It is not operation admission. It is an early preservation requirement so any future eligibility boundary cannot hide refusal, blocked attempts, or projection mismatch.

## 20. Closing Boundary Statement

Distributed operation matter declaration may record one bounded distributed operation candidate as a matter for future eligibility review. It preserves that declaration does not mean eligibility, admission, authorization, execution, synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, continuation, consequence, public readiness, or follow-on work.

It closes the question:

`Has one distributed operation candidate been declared as a bounded matter for future eligibility review?`

It does not open the question:

`Is distributed operation eligible or authorized?`
