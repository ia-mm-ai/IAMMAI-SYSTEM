# Distributed Operation Eligibility Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the distributed operation eligibility boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

Distributed operation eligibility is the second lawful gate after distributed operation matter declaration.

Eligibility evaluates one declared operation matter only.

The purpose is to prevent a declared operation matter from becoming operation, admission, authorization, repository synchronization, full body transfer, continuation, consequence, final completion, public readiness, or follow-on permission.

Eligibility is not admission.

Eligibility is not authorization.

Eligibility is not execution.

Eligibility is not operation.

Eligibility is not synchronization.

Eligibility is not full body transfer.

Eligibility is not continuation.

Eligibility is not permission.

Eligibility only decides whether one selected declared operation matter may proceed toward a future admission/authority review.

This file is an eligibility boundary specification only.

It is not an implementation, resolver, test, operation admission, operation execution, distributed operation, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, current self-orientation v10, source-body operational authority boundary, carrier operational role boundary, synchronization / non-synchronization boundary, distributed operation transition authority, distributed refusal and abort law, distributed execution / emission boundary, distributed action / consequence boundary, distributed operation receipt / exhaustion, distributed operation conformance, distributed operation closure, final governance, final continuity completion, final system identity, public launch surface, truth law, action law, consequence/action law, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`distributed_operation_eligibility` means a bounded review of whether one selected declared distributed operation matter may proceed toward a future admission/authority review. It is not admission, authorization, execution, operation, synchronization, transfer, continuation, consequence, or permission.

`selected_operation_matter_declaration` means one declared matter declaration result selected as the target of eligibility review. Its outcome must be `DISTRIBUTED_OPERATION_MATTER_DECLARED` before eligibility may proceed.

`selected_operation_candidate` means the candidate named by the selected matter declaration. It remains candidate only.

`eligibility_question` means the declared question asking whether the selected matter is eligible for future admission/authority review. It is not an admission question and not an operation question.

`eligibility_basis` means the selected matter declaration result, selected matter identity, selected operation candidate identity, selected operation question, selected operation purpose, proposed operation kind, selected operation basis, selected carrier context where applicable, proposed affected surfaces, proposed output family, eligibility review request, refusal/abort awareness, current-body conformance v4 closure basis, current self-orientation v9 basis, distributed standing basis where applicable, source-body basis, and explicit eligibility non-claims.

`eligibility_claim` means the bounded assertion that the selected matter is eligible to proceed toward a future admission/authority review.

`eligibility_refusal` means refusal to record eligibility because the selected matter declaration, selected candidate, basis, non-claims, refusal/abort awareness, no-admission posture, no-authority posture, no-operation posture, or no-collapse posture is missing, malformed, failed, too broad, contradiction-shaped, admission-shaped, authority-shaped, operation-shaped, synchronization-shaped, full-body-transfer-shaped, continuation-shaped, consequence-shaped, public-readiness-shaped, final-completion-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`eligibility_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that eligibility did not admit, authorize, execute, synchronize, transfer, continue, define authority, define carrier roles, create permission, create truth/action, create consequence, create public readiness, claim final completion, schedule follow-on work, mutate evidence, replay source, or merge carriers.

`eligible_for_future_admission_authority_review` means the selected matter is sufficiently bounded to be considered by a later source-body operational authority or admission boundary, if that later boundary is separately declared and bounded.

`not_eligible` means the selected matter is readable but cannot proceed toward future admission/authority review under the declared basis.

`requires_additional_basis` means the selected matter is declared and non-collapse-shaped, but the eligibility review cannot yet decide eligible or not eligible because bounded basis is insufficient.

`blocked_eligibility_review` means eligibility cannot lawfully proceed because the request or selected matter is missing, malformed, unreadable, failed, or collapse-shaped.

Eligibility does not admit operation.

Eligibility does not authorize operation.

Eligibility does not execute operation.

Eligibility does not decide authority.

Eligibility does not define carrier roles.

Eligibility does not define synchronization.

Eligibility does not define refusal/abort law.

Eligibility only gates whether the declared matter may proceed to a future admission/authority review.

## 22. Closing Boundary Statement
