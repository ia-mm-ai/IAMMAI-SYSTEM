# Descendant Body Candidate Authored Scope Division Declaration Receipt Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines one receipt-boundary for future audit of an external authored signed candidate scope-division declaration. It is boundary-spec-only. It does not perform receipt, audit, admission, scope declaration, emission, distinctness, standing, relation, runtime, authority, repair, scan, validation enforcement, or follow-on work.

The external declaration exists outside the repository. This boundary permits only a future receipt-then-audit shape for proposed authored audit material. It does not copy, hash, ingest, claim custody over, or treat the external declaration as standing or clean basis.

## 2. Scope

This boundary applies to one external authored declaration about two existing non-standing candidate records:

- Candidate A proposed scope: Motion-side admissible variation.
- Candidate B proposed scope: Regulation-side admissibility bounds.

The external declaration is proposed audit material only. Its signature is authorship attestation only. ## 6. External Authored Declaration Posture

The external authored declaration proposes that Candidate A carries the Motion mandate and governs the variation side of the parent basis: unconstrained admissible variation across frequency, rhythm, phase, amplitude, periodicity, and latency without fixed values, targets, optimization, preferred trajectory, or steering.

It proposes that Candidate B carries the Regulation mandate and governs the admissibility-bound side: coherence, stability, persistence, damping, and modulation may constrain admissibility through thresholds, ranges, and rejection conditions without encoding outcomes, imposing constants, deciding trajectories, or replacing motion with control.

It proposes a non-cosmetic division by responsibility, function, and governed surface. Motion preserves free variation; Regulation preserves admissible bounds. It proposes that neither role may substitute for the other without freezing the system or allowing collapse.

It proposes that coupling is assigned to neither candidate, is not a third candidate, not a third model, not a standing body, and not created by the declaration. It proposes that coupling may appear only later, if separately bounded, as a reversible, non-authoritative, non-persistent effect when Motion-side variation satisfies Regulation-side admissibility conditions.

It proposes lineage requirements: no orphaned state, no silent reset, no overwrite, and later evolution only as additive change referencing prior state. It authorizes audit as a proposed basis-bearing candidate scope division. Every statement in this section remains proposed authored audit material, not a standing finding.
