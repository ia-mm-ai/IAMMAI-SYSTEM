# Artifact Emission Containment Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the artifact emission containment boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

This is the next lawful containment gate before any further live artifact emission in the portable verification / command-boundary line.

The purpose is to prevent future result artifacts from recursively embedding whole prior artifacts.

This boundary may allow the following fields only as bounded artifact-emission containment outcomes:

- `artifact_emission_containment_recorded = true`
- `reference_only_selected_basis_required = true`
- `recursive_full_artifact_embedding_blocked = true`
- `summary_plus_reference_emission_required = true`
- `prior_artifacts_preserved_by_reference = true`

These fields mean future artifact-emission shape containment only.

They do not rewrite old artifacts.

They do not compact old artifacts.

They do not delete old artifacts.

They do not repair old artifacts.

They do not normalize old artifacts.

They do not mark old artifacts invalid only because they are large.

They do not change prior outcomes.

They do not change prior failed check counts.

They do not create a command.

They do not authorize command execution.

They do not implement manifest, checksum, signature, packet, or command mechanics.

They do not create deployment, runtime hosting, public release, source transfer, source migration, source receipt, reception authorization, currentness, authority, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, reusable permission, derivative reception, vessel relation, another reception request, or follow-on work.

Artifact emission containment is not artifact compaction.

Artifact emission containment is not artifact invalidation.

Artifact emission containment is not manifest implementation.

Artifact emission containment is not command implementation.

Artifact emission containment only defines how future artifacts should avoid recursive full-artifact embedding.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

Recent artifacts show recursive full-artifact embedding pressure.

Source-body reception closure artifacts grew substantially when full prior results were embedded.

Portable source-body verification artifacts grew much larger by embedding prior closure and evidence surfaces.

Portable source-body verification evidence-manifest artifacts grew larger again by embedding portable verification basis.

The next live command-boundary artifact would likely grow further if it embeds the full evidence-manifest artifact.

Selected basis sections can become very large when full upstream result objects are embedded.

This creates operational carrier strain.

This strain is a carrier-shape and artifact-emission problem, not automatic law-check failure.

Large artifacts may remain standing heavy evidence where their checks passed.

Large artifacts may also be evidence of artifact-emission shape pressure.

Continuing to emit new live artifacts without containment would likely amplify artifact size.

Containment should be specified before the next live command-boundary artifact is emitted.

The next lawful pressure is artifact emission containment.

The next lawful pressure is not command-boundary live artifact, command implementation, manifest implementation, checksum implementation, signature implementation, packet implementation, artifact cleanup, artifact compaction, artifact deletion, artifact migration, artifact rewriting, artifact normalization, repository cleanup, deployment, runtime hosting, public release, source transfer, source migration, source receipt, or final completion.

## 4. Definitions

`artifact_emission_containment` means a bounded review surface that defines how future artifacts should preserve selected prior results without recursively embedding full upstream artifacts. It is not artifact cleanup, artifact compaction, artifact deletion, artifact migration, command implementation, manifest implementation, deployment, runtime hosting, or public release.

`recursive_full_artifact_embedding` means placing the full body of one prior result artifact inside a later result artifact, especially where that prior artifact already embeds earlier full artifacts. Recursive full-artifact embedding creates compounding artifact-size pressure.

`selected_basis_reference_record` means a bounded record that identifies a selected prior result by reference fields instead of by full raw artifact body.

`summary_plus_reference_emission` means future artifact emission that preserves a selected result by id, path, outcome, check counts where available, selected summary fields, selected non-claims, and basis references instead of embedding the full prior artifact body.

`raw_artifact_embedding` means including the complete upstream artifact JSON or comparable full artifact body inside a future result artifact.

`standing_heavy_artifact` means an existing artifact that may remain valid evidence despite being operationally large. Size alone does not invalidate its recorded law checks.

`heavy_artifact_evidence` means prior large artifacts may be selected as evidence that recursive embedding pressure exists. Heavy artifact evidence does not become source, authority, currentness, cleanup permission, or invalidation basis by size alone.

`artifact_size_pressure` means operational strain caused by large artifact bodies, repeated nested embedding, or cumulative carrier weight. It is not itself a law-check failure.

`carrier_shape_pressure` means strain on filesystem, transport, review, storage, diff, or carrier handling caused by artifact shape. It does not create source, authority, currentness, or final completion.

`reference_only_selected_basis` means future artifacts preserve identifiers and summaries rather than full raw nested artifacts.

`summary_digest` means bounded summary fields selected for readability and review. In this specification, digest does not mean cryptographic digest unless a separate checksum or signature boundary specifies it.

`selected_non_claim_excerpt` means a bounded subset or complete declared set of non-claims necessary to preserve anti-collapse posture without embedding the full prior artifact.

`full_artifact_body` means the complete content of an upstream artifact, including all sections, nested basis objects, checks, statements, summaries, non-claims, and blocks.

`artifact_mutation` means rewriting, replacing, deleting, moving, renaming, truncating, normalizing, compacting, repairing, or otherwise changing an existing artifact.

`artifact_compaction` means reducing an existing artifact's body, replacing it with a shorter version, or emitting a replacement that claims to stand in its place. This boundary does not authorize compaction.

`artifact_invalidation_by_size` means treating an artifact as invalid only because it is large. This boundary prohibits that posture.

`permitted_prior_result_reference` means a future selected result reference that may include id, path, outcome, passed check count, failed check count, selected summary, selected non-claims, declared basis reference, artifact family, and descriptive size class.

`prohibited_prior_result_embedding` means embedding full raw prior artifact bodies into future artifacts without a separate boundary that explicitly permits that embedding.

Standing heavy artifact means an existing artifact that may remain valid evidence despite being operationally large.

Artifact-size pressure does not by itself invalidate prior law checks.

Reference-only selected basis means future artifacts preserve identifiers and summaries rather than full raw nested artifacts.

Summary digest in this specification means bounded summary fields, not cryptographic digest unless separately specified.

Checksum and signature language remains future work unless separately specified.
