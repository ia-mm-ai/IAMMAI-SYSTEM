# Multi-Carrier Relation Conformance V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines bounded conformance criteria for a recognized multi-carrier relation in the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

It defines how a selected recognized relation may be tested for coherence as one bounded relation posture.

The purpose is to prevent `relation recognized` from becoming:

- distributed standing
- currentness
- carrier hierarchy
- source authority
- permission
- repository synchronization
- continuation
- permission to expand carriers
- permission for another physical carrier experiment

Relation conformance is not relation creation.

Relation conformance is not distributed standing.

Relation conformance is not currentness.

Relation conformance is not carrier hierarchy.

Relation conformance is not divergence resolution.

Relation conformance is not permission to continue.

Relation conformance may test only whether the selected recognized relation preserves its basis, selected carriers, selected evidence, local outcomes, visible refusal, visible divergence, downstream posture, currentness non-claims, and relation non-claims.

This file is a specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, distributed standing surface, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization surface, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

The carrier role and emission boundary defines local carrier roles and role-permitted emissions.

Carrier-local emissions may be admitted into the body line only as evidence.

The cross-carrier receipt boundary proves that one carried surface may be received by one physical carrier as carried evidence without creating source, currentness, authority, permission, successor standing, body formation, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The cross-carrier divergence boundary preserves visible mismatch without resolving it into truth, currentness, action, carrier correctness, carrier hierarchy, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The cross-carrier currentness participation boundary prevents carrier evidence from creating currentness by possession, receipt, return, admission, correspondence, divergence, timestamp, majority, success count, availability, label, or convenience.

The cross-surface correspondence boundary permits selected standing surfaces to be read together while preserving rank, source, scope, lineage, local outcome, and non-claims.

The multi-carrier relation boundary now defines when selected carriers or carrier evidence may stand in bounded relation.

A live relation artifact recognizes a Carrier A / Carrier B refusal-success relation under `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_RECOGNIZED`.

Relation recognition is still not conformance.

Conformance is needed to test whether the recognized relation coheres under the body's anti-collapse requirements.

Conformance must not upgrade relation into distributed standing or currentness.

Conformance must precede any closure of relation meaning.

This remains narrower than distributed standing and narrower than any multi-device expansion.

## 4. Definitions

`multi-carrier relation conformance` means a bounded body-side conformance assessment over one selected recognized multi-carrier relation. It tests relation coherence without creating source, currentness, authority, permission, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, truth, action, consequence, or continuation.

`selected relation result` means one declared multi-carrier relation result selected for conformance review. For this boundary, the selected relation result must expose `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_RECOGNIZED`.

`relation conformance basis` means the selected relation result identity, relation outcome, relation type, relation question, selected carriers, selected carrier evidence, relation basis, relation checks, visible refusal where available, visible divergence where available, currentness participation posture where applicable, lineage, and explicit non-claims.

`relation conformance claim` means the bounded assertion that the selected recognized relation coheres under the declared conformance basis.

`relation conformance refusal` means conformance is refused or blocked because the selected relation result, relation basis, selected carriers, selected evidence, checks, visible refusal, visible divergence, or non-claims are missing, malformed, hidden, unsupported, or collapse-shaped.

`relation coherence` means the selected recognized relation preserves its declared basis, selected carrier set, selected evidence set, local outcomes, downstream posture, visible refusal, visible divergence, currentness participation as participation only, and required false non-claims.

`relation non-conformance` means the selected relation is readable but fails one or more bounded conformance requirements without necessarily being malformed or collapse-shaped.

`selected carrier set` means the carriers preserved by the selected relation result, including carrier identities and operation-local carrier roles where exposed.

`selected evidence set` means the carrier-evidence surfaces preserved by the selected relation result, including evidence identities, evidence outcomes, source or carried basis where available, admission status, correspondence status, divergence status, and currentness participation status where exposed.

`conformed relation posture` means a selected recognized relation has passed bounded relation conformance checks. Conformed relation posture does not mean distributed standing.

`relation conformance non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that conformance did not create source, currentness, authority, permission, successor, body, carrier hierarchy, distributed standing, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, continuation, additional carrier permission, or distributed operation.

Conformed relation posture does not mean distributed standing.

Relation coherence does not mean permission.

Relation conformance does not mean currentness.

## 5. What Relation Conformance Means

Relation conformance means a conformance surface verifies that a selected recognized multi-carrier relation preserves:

- relation outcome
- relation basis
- selected carriers
- selected carrier roles
- selected evidence identities
- selected evidence outcomes
- downstream evidence posture
- local outcomes
- visible refusal where available
- visible divergence where available
- currentness participation as participation only
- relation non-claims
- no source/currentness/authority/permission collapse
- no carrier hierarchy
- no distributed standing
- no presence/threshold/truth/action/consequence
- no continuation

Relation conformance may say:

- the selected relation coheres
- the selected relation remains bounded
- the selected relation may later be closed in meaning if separately admitted

Relation conformance records bounded coherence. It does not convert coherence into force.

## 9. Conformance Outcomes for Future Implementation

The minimum future outcome family is:

- `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANT`
- `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_NONCONFORMANT`
- `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANCE_BLOCKED`

`MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANT` means the selected recognized relation coheres under bounded conformance checks.

`MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_NONCONFORMANT` means the selected relation is readable but does not satisfy conformance for the declared conformance question.

`MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_CONFORMANCE_BLOCKED` means the conformance request is malformed, missing selected relation basis, source-replacing, currentness-creating, hierarchy-creating, distributed-standing-shaped, hidden-divergence-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

No conformance outcome may say that a carrier is current, that distributed standing exists, or that continuation is authorized.

## 12. Relation to Multi-Carrier Relation Boundary

The multi-carrier relation boundary recognizes selected relation.

This conformance specification tests whether a selected recognized relation coheres.

Conformance does not create relation by itself.

Conformance cannot repair a blocked relation.

Conformance cannot turn no-relation into relation.

Conformance cannot widen relation scope.

Only a selected `MULTI_CARRIER_RELATION_RECOGNIZED` result may be eligible for relation conformance.

## 13. Relation to Cross-Carrier Currentness

Currentness participation remains participation only.

Conformance does not create currentness.

Conformance cannot select current carrier.

Conformance cannot convert eligible evidence into current evidence.

Conformance must block carrier/currentness shortcuts if present.

Currentness cannot be derived from relation conformance, latest file, newest timestamp, local copy, possession, return, receipt, admission, correspondence, divergence, majority, success count, carrier availability, carrier label, or convenience.

## 14. Relation to Cross-Carrier Divergence

Conformance may pass with visible divergence if divergence remains bounded and non-collapsing.

Conformance must block hidden divergence where it affects selected relation.

Conformance does not resolve divergence.

Conformance cannot select a winner from divergence.

Conformance cannot invalidate losing evidence.

Visible divergence remains preserved as relation evidence, not truth, currentness, action, or carrier correctness.

## 15. Relation to Carrier-Local Emission Admission

Admitted evidence may be part of relation conformance.

Conformance does not admit evidence by itself.

Conformance cannot turn admitted evidence into source, currentness, authority, or permission.

Conformance must preserve admitted evidence as downstream posture.

Admission count does not create conformance, currentness, relation scope expansion, or distributed standing.

## 16. Relation to Carrier Role and Emission Boundary

Carrier roles remain operation-local.

Conformance does not make roles permanent.

Conformance does not grant new roles.

Conformance does not authorize new emissions.

Conformance must block unsupported role or emission collapse if present.

Carrier role and carrier-local emission may be preserved inside conformance only as bounded relation evidence.

## 17. Relation to Cross-Surface Correspondence

Correspondence may be evidence inside relation conformance.

Conformance does not create correspondence.

Conformance cannot use correspondence as currentness, truth, or action.

Correspondence remains a bounded reading relation.

Correspondence alignment does not become relation conformance by itself.

## 18. Relation to Self-Orientation

Self-orientation may later mirror conformed relation posture if current posture materially requires it.

Self-orientation successor is not forced by conformance alone.

Self-orientation must not become carrier selection.

Self-orientation must not turn conformed relation posture into currentness, authority, permission, distributed standing, or continuation.

## 19. Relation to Current-Body Conformance

This specification does not replace current-body conformance v2.

## 20. Relation to Closure

Closure is not scheduled, authorized, or executed by this specification.

## 21. Relation to Distributed Standing

Relation conformance does not create distributed standing.

Distributed standing remains open.

Conformance does not authorize persistence, registry, or synchronization.

Conformance does not mean the body exists across carriers as a distributed body.

Conformance does not authorize distributed operation.

- selected relation result missing
- selected relation result malformed
- selected relation outcome not recognized
- selected relation type unsupported
- selected carriers missing
- selected evidence missing

- selected evidence outcome missing
- relation checks failed or hidden
- visible refusal hidden
- visible divergence hidden
- currentness shortcut present
- carrier hierarchy created
- winning carrier selected
- losing carrier invalidated
- relation replaced source
- relation created currentness
- relation created authority
- relation created permission
- relation created successor standing

- relation created signal by default

- relation established threshold

- required non-claims are missing or flipped

Refusal preserves the selected basis where available. It does not erase the selected relation, selected carriers, selected evidence, visible refusal, or visible divergence.

## 27. Closing Boundary Statement

Multi-carrier relation conformance tests whether a selected recognized relation coheres while preserving carrier roles, evidence identities, local outcomes, visible refusal, visible divergence, downstream posture, currentness non-claims, lineage, and relation non-claims.
