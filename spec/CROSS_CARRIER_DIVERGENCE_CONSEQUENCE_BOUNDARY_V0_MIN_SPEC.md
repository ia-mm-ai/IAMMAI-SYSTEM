# Cross-Carrier Divergence Consequence Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded cross-carrier divergence consequence boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

It defines what lawful consequence visible cross-carrier divergence may have after it has been preserved through admission, currentness participation, relation, relation conformance closure, lifecycle, registry/persistence v2, standing propagation v2, carrier continuity-turn v2, and cross-carrier currentness successor posture.

The purpose is to prevent visible divergence from either:

- being erased as noise
- being overread as truth, currentness, action, invalidation, winner/loser selection, or distributed standing

Divergence consequence is not divergence resolution.

Divergence consequence is not truth.

Divergence consequence is not action.

Divergence consequence is not punishment.

Divergence consequence is not currentness.

Divergence consequence is not distributed standing.

Visible divergence may have lawful consequence by limiting, blocking, deferring, requiring caution, requiring revalidation, or requiring a separate review for a declared reliance/use question.

A consequence of divergence may affect reliance. It may not decide truth.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, divergence resolution, truth law, action law, consequence/action law, punishment or sanction surface, currentness successor implementation, distributed standing, carrier registry implementation, persistence implementation, standing propagation implementation, carrier continuity-turn implementation, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, network topology, consensus system, leader election, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 4. Definitions

`cross-carrier divergence consequence` means a bounded posture recording what visible divergence lawfully requires, limits, blocks, excludes, cautions, or defers for one declared reliance/use question.

`visible divergence` means a preserved, inspectable mismatch, conflict, disagreement, blocked posture, basis mismatch, outcome mismatch, integrity mismatch, role mismatch, emission mismatch, return-path mismatch, or non-claim conflict between selected carrier evidence surfaces. Visible divergence does not decide which evidence is correct.

`divergence consequence posture` means one bounded consequence status for a declared reliance/use question. It may record caution, reliance limitation, specific reliance block, revalidation requirement, separate-review requirement, declared-use exclusion, no consequence for declared use, not-recorded posture, or blocked posture.

`divergence consequence basis` means the declared divergence consequence question, declared reliance/use question, selected divergence evidence, selected divergence evidence outcome, selected carrier evidence where applicable, currentness successor basis where applicable, continuity-turn basis where applicable, standing propagation basis where applicable, registry/persistence basis where applicable, lifecycle basis where applicable, relation/conformance/closure basis where applicable, visible refusal, visible divergence, blocked attempts, projection mismatch, detailed basis, summary projection, and explicit non-claims.

`divergence consequence claim` means the bounded assertion that selected visible divergence has one lawful effect on one declared reliance/use question.

`divergence consequence refusal` means refusal to record divergence consequence posture because required basis is missing, malformed, hidden, summary-overwriting, divergence-resolving, winner-selecting, loser-invalidating, truth-creating, action-authorizing, currentness-creating, distributed-standing-shaped, continuation-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`divergence caution` means a posture that selected visible divergence must remain visible and must be considered before reliance. Caution does not decide truth.

`divergence reliance limitation` means a posture that selected visible divergence limits what may be relied upon for a declared use without generally invalidating the evidence.

`divergence exclusion` means a posture that selected evidence is excluded from one declared use because of visible divergence. Exclusion for one declared use does not erase the evidence or invalidate it for every future use.

`divergence revalidation requirement` means a posture that selected evidence or basis requires bounded revalidation before a declared reliance path may proceed. Revalidation is not scheduled or authorized by this boundary.

`divergence separate-review requirement` means a posture that selected divergence requires a separate review before currentness successor, propagation, standing, registry, or distributed-standing use. Separate review is not scheduled or authorized by this boundary.

`divergence non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that divergence consequence did not resolve divergence, create truth, authorize action, select a winner, invalidate a loser, create currentness, replace source, create authority, create permission, create carrier hierarchy, erase evidence, create distributed standing, authorize synchronization, authorize full body transfer, create a second body, authorize continuation, authorize distributed operation, or perform mutation/replay/merge.

Divergence consequence is not resolving divergence.

Divergence consequence is not selecting a winner.

Divergence consequence is not invalidating a loser.

Divergence consequence is not truth or action.

## 5. Divergence Consequence Postures

The minimum bounded divergence consequence posture set is:

- `DIVERGENCE_CONSEQUENCE_RECORDED`
- `DIVERGENCE_REQUIRES_CAUTION`
- `DIVERGENCE_LIMITS_RELIANCE`
- `DIVERGENCE_BLOCKS_SPECIFIC_RELIANCE`
- `DIVERGENCE_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION`
- `DIVERGENCE_REQUIRES_SEPARATE_REVIEW`
- `DIVERGENCE_EXCLUDES_EVIDENCE_FOR_DECLARED_USE`
- `DIVERGENCE_HAS_NO_CONSEQUENCE_FOR_DECLARED_USE`
- `DIVERGENCE_CONSEQUENCE_NOT_RECORDED`
- `DIVERGENCE_CONSEQUENCE_BLOCKED`

These are bounded consequence postures for a declared reliance/use question.

They are not truth postures.

They are not action postures.

They are not currentness postures.

They are not distributed-standing postures.

`DIVERGENCE_CONSEQUENCE_RECORDED` means one bounded consequence posture has been recorded for selected visible divergence under declared basis and non-claims.

`DIVERGENCE_REQUIRES_CAUTION` means selected visible divergence requires caution before reliance for the declared use.

`DIVERGENCE_LIMITS_RELIANCE` means selected visible divergence limits what may be relied on for the declared use.

`DIVERGENCE_BLOCKS_SPECIFIC_RELIANCE` means selected visible divergence blocks one declared reliance path while preserving the evidence and other possible future uses.

`DIVERGENCE_REQUIRES_REVALIDATION` means selected visible divergence requires bounded revalidation before the declared reliance path may proceed.

`DIVERGENCE_REQUIRES_SEPARATE_REVIEW` means selected visible divergence requires a separate bounded review before the declared use may proceed.

`DIVERGENCE_EXCLUDES_EVIDENCE_FOR_DECLARED_USE` means selected evidence is excluded only for the declared use because of visible divergence.

`DIVERGENCE_HAS_NO_CONSEQUENCE_FOR_DECLARED_USE` means selected visible divergence has no consequence for the declared use only under explicit basis and preserved non-claims.

`DIVERGENCE_CONSEQUENCE_NOT_RECORDED` means the request was readable but no divergence consequence posture was recorded.

`DIVERGENCE_CONSEQUENCE_BLOCKED` means divergence consequence posture was refused under bounded block conditions.

## 6. What Divergence Consequence Means

- a visible divergence affects a declared future reliance question
- selected evidence requires caution before reliance
- selected evidence is excluded from a specific declared use

Divergence consequence records bounded downstream effect on reliance.

Divergence consequence does not convert divergence into truth.

Divergence consequence does not convert caution into action.
