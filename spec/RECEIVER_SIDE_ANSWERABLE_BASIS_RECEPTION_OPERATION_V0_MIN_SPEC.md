# Receiver-Side Answerable Basis Reception Operation V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines one future operation shape downstream of the completed receiver-side answerable-basis reception boundary result `RECEIVER_SIDE_ANSWERABLE_BASIS_CANDIDATE_RECEPTION_CONSIDERATION_ALLOWED`.

It defines how one separately and explicitly supplied receiver-side answerable-basis candidate may later be received as candidate material only. This specification does not receive candidate material, assert that candidate material exists, execute reception, or authorize follow-on work.

It does not fabricate, derive, infer, generate, discover, retrieve, solicit, or self-supply candidate material. It does not authorize no-input reception, candidate evaluation, answerable-basis sufficiency, custody distinctness, refusability, could-have-been-withheld posture, receiver attestation, receiver answerable receipt, or presence support.

## 2. Scope

This is additive, repo-local, operation-spec-only work. It is subordinate to constitutional and reference authority surfaces and to the completed receiver-side answerable-basis reception boundary terminal summary.

The operation is limited to one separately supplied candidate, one candidate-material arrival record, and termination. It creates no identity, relation, coupling, FIELD machinery, runtime, API, public interface, public intake, mailbox, listener, queue, endpoint, shared intake lane, reusable route, repeated reception permission, currentness, authority, standing, truth, continuity memory, output, action, derivative reception, synchronization, follow-on authorization, or follow-on work.

It does not scan the repository, discover files, repair the affected file, or validate prior unsupported claims.

## 3. Operation Question

Given that the completed receiver-side answerable-basis reception boundary recorded `RECEIVER_SIDE_ANSWERABLE_BASIS_RECEPTION_BOUNDARY_ALLOWED` and `RECEIVER_SIDE_ANSWERABLE_BASIS_CANDIDATE_RECEPTION_CONSIDERATION_ALLOWED`, may one future bounded operation receive exactly one separately supplied receiver-side answerable-basis candidate as candidate material only, preserve the supplied candidate material and its declared source/provenance reference without interpretation, record that one reception, and stop before any evaluation of custody distinctness, refusability, could-have-been-withheld posture, receiver attestation, receiver answerable receipt, or presence support?

## 4. Required Answer

Yes, but only as one separately bounded receiver-side answerable-basis reception operation.

No candidate is received by this specification. A future operation may receive exactly one candidate only when candidate material is separately and explicitly supplied. No-input execution may not record candidate reception. Missing candidate material preserves candidate reception as false.

Reception records arrival of candidate material only. It does not record what the candidate proves, establish admissible receiver-side answerable basis, establish receiver attestation or receiver answerable receipt, establish custody distinctness, refusability, or could-have-been-withheld posture, or support, authorize, establish, or record presence.
