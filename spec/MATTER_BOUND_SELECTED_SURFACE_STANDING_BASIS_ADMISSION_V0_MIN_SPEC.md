# Matter-Bound Selected-Surface Standing-Basis Admission V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines one minimum non-executable boundary for deciding whether one exact family-owned standing basis may be admitted for one exact selected surface in one exact bounded downstream matter and use.

The boundary answers only:

> May this exact family-owned standing basis be admitted for this exact selected surface in this exact bounded downstream matter/use?

It does not answer:

> Does this surface stand?

Standing remains established and semantically governed by the source family. This boundary may admit a supplied standing basis for one declared use; it may not establish, renew, generalize, reinterpret, or globally recognize the standing of the selected surface.

The minimum admission unit is exactly:

- one selected surface;
- one explicit downstream matter and use;
- one exact family-owned standing result or assertion;
- one exact family-owned contract defining that result's standing effect;
- the exact evidence, basis, and lineage supporting that result;
- the result's applicable scope, rank, custody, and non-claims; and
- one exact binding among the surface, result or assertion, basis, contract, and downstream use.

This file is a specification only. It does not create a resolver, test, request, result, receipt, artifact, registry, catalogue, inventory, standing-vocabulary allowlist, correspondence successor, runtime, execution, persistence, or lifecycle surface.

## 3. Separate Boundary

Matter-bound selected-surface correspondence requires every selected surface to be already standing. Its current V2 resolver requires exact supplied fields such as surface identity, reference, outcome, rank, source basis, scope, lineage, and non-claims. That strict supplied-posture validation does not independently establish that the selected surface stands or that its standing basis is admissible for the correspondence use.

The repository contains family-local standing contracts and bounded admission ancestry, but no canonical cross-family verifier for arbitrary referenced surfaces. Standing-basis admission must therefore remain separate from correspondence.

This separation is not a defect finding against correspondence V2. Correspondence reconstructs one declared relation among selected surfaces. Standing-basis admission decides only whether one supplied family-owned standing basis may be used for one selected surface in one downstream matter/use.

## 4. Boundary Identity and Scope

The minimum constitutional object is:

- `matter_bound_selected_surface_standing_basis_admission_type = MATTER_BOUND_SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_ADMISSION`
- `matter_bound_selected_surface_standing_basis_admission_version = 0.1.0`
- `matter_bound_selected_surface_standing_basis_admission_scope = ONE_SELECTED_SURFACE_ONE_EXPLICIT_DOWNSTREAM_MATTER_USE_ONE_EXACT_FAMILY_OWNED_STANDING_BASIS_ONLY`

The boundary is matter-bound, use-bound, surface-bound, contract-bound, basis-bound, lineage-preserving, non-generalizing, and read-only in constitutional effect.

One admission decision applies only to the exact selected surface and exact declared downstream matter/use named in that decision. Another surface, matter, scope, use, or downstream operation requires a separate admission basis and separate decision.

## 5. Definitions

`selected surface` means the one exact surface whose family-owned standing basis is proposed for admission. It is identified by exact surface identity, exact reference, source family, source type, and custody.

`explicit downstream matter/use` means one declared matter identity, matter scope, use identity, use class, use purpose, and outside boundary. It is not a generic desire to reuse standing evidence.

`source family` means the family that owns the selected surface's standing semantics, result or assertion, supporting basis, and standing-effect contract. Family ownership is attribution, not authority transfer.

`family-local standing result or assertion` means one exact typed result or assertion whose standing effect is defined by the cited source-family contract. Its outcome string or boolean is not self-interpreting outside that contract.

`family-owned standing contract` means the exact source-family contract that defines what the selected result or assertion means, what evidence establishes it, the scope in which it applies, and whether it remains applicable. This boundary does not replace that contract.

`standing evidence/basis` means the exact bounded evidence and basis references that the family-owned result or assertion cites under its own contract. Standing evidence is not standing.

`standing lineage` means the exact source-to-result references needed to preserve how the family-local result or assertion was derived, reviewed, recorded, or closed under its own family law.

`applicable posture` means the exact scope, rank, custody, non-claims, and contract-defined applicability carried by the family-local result or assertion.

`exact standing-basis binding` means one explicit, internally coherent binding among the selected surface identity and reference, source family and type, family-local result or assertion, family-owned contract, evidence/basis references, lineage, applicable posture, and declared downstream matter/use.

`standing-basis admission` means a bounded decision that the exact supplied basis may be used for the exact selected surface in the exact declared downstream matter/use. Admission is not standing.

`additional basis` means the supplied envelope remains bounded and non-collapse-shaped, but one or more exact family-owned contract, result, evidence, lineage, applicability, or binding facts required for admission are absent or incomplete.

`review blocked` means the admission review cannot lawfully proceed because the supplied envelope is malformed, unreadable, unsupported, contradictory, ambiguous, falsely attributed, scope-widening, authority-transferring, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

## 6. Minimum Supplied Admission Envelope

One admission review requires exactly one declared envelope preserving:

1. one selected surface identity;
2. one exact selected surface reference;
3. one selected surface custody;
4. one source family and source type;
5. one explicit downstream matter identity and scope;
6. one explicit downstream use identity, class, purpose, and outside boundary;
7. one exact family-local standing result or assertion identity, type, version where applicable, reference, and recorded value;
8. one exact family-owned standing contract identity, version where applicable, reference, and standing-effect location;
9. the exact evidence and basis references supporting the result or assertion;
10. the exact lineage references supporting the result or assertion;
11. the result's applicable scope, rank, custody, and non-claims;
12. explicit attribution of the result, basis, and contract to the source family; and
13. one exact standing-basis binding covering every preceding element.

The envelope must be closed and singular. It must not contain alternative surfaces, alternative uses, fallback results, latest candidates, family-spanning outcome maps, or a list of universally accepted standing values.

The standing-effect location must identify the exact contract provision or exact contract-owned field that gives the supplied result or assertion its claimed effect. A contract reference that does not locate the governing effect is incomplete.

## 7. Insufficient Shortcuts

None of the following is sufficient alone or in combination without the complete exact envelope:

- a naked outcome string;
- a naked boolean;
- a filename;
- a directory location;
- a timestamp;
- a sequence number;
- a receipt;
- repository presence;
- repository-local availability;
- a latest-file or newest-result inference;
- adjacency to another standing surface;
- similarity to another family's result;
- a summary without the family-owned result, contract, basis, and binding; or
- a claim that the selected surface is already used elsewhere.

Exact paths and references may participate in identity and binding. They do not create standing or admissibility by themselves.

## 8. Admission Discipline

The boundary may validate only the supplied envelope's:

- completeness;
- exact identity binding;
- attribution;
- source-family ownership;
- contract reference and standing-effect location;
- evidence and basis references;
- lineage;
- rank;
- scope compatibility;
- custody compatibility;
- declared downstream matter and use;
- exact cross-field binding; and
- required non-claims.

The source family's cited contract remains authoritative over:

- what its standing result or assertion means;
- what evidence establishes that result or assertion;
- the scope in which it stands;
- its rank and custody effects;
- its required non-claims; and
- whether it remains applicable to the selected surface.

This boundary must not:

- invent a standing result;
- reinterpret a source-family standing term;
- maintain a universal list of standing outcomes;
- normalize heterogeneous standing vocabularies into one central vocabulary;
- manufacture missing evidence;
- infer standing from adjacent conditions;
- reevaluate the source family's evidence on different semantics;
- override a family-owned contract;
- convert admission into source-family judgment; or
- infer applicability beyond the exact contract-owned effect and supplied binding.

If determining the standing effect requires semantics not explicitly supplied by the family-owned contract and exact envelope, admission cannot be inferred. The review must stop visibly.

## 9. Required Outcome Family

The bounded outcome family is exactly:

- `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_ADMITTED`
- `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_NOT_ADMITTED`
- `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_REQUIRES_ADDITIONAL_BASIS`
- `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_REVIEW_BLOCKED`

`SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_ADMITTED` means only that the exact supplied family-owned basis may be used for the exact selected surface in the exact declared downstream matter/use. It does not mean this boundary established, renewed, generalized, canonicalized, or globally recognized standing.

`SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_NOT_ADMITTED` means the readable and attributable supplied basis cannot be admitted for the exact declared matter/use under its contract-owned scope, rank, custody, applicability, or non-claims. It does not invalidate or downgrade the selected surface or its family-local result.

`SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_REQUIRES_ADDITIONAL_BASIS` means the proposed admission remains bounded and non-collapse-shaped, but an exact required contract, result, evidence, lineage, applicability, or binding fact is missing or incomplete. It creates no request, schedule, debt, obligation, retry, or permission to obtain that basis.

`SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_REVIEW_BLOCKED` means the review could not lawfully complete because the envelope is malformed, unreadable, unsupported, contradictory, ambiguous, falsely attributed, scope-widening, authority-transferring, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

Not admitted, additional basis, and blocked are lawful stopping postures. Refusal or missing basis is not system failure.

## 10. Required Admission Checks

Admission may be recorded only when all of the following are explicit and mutually coherent:

- exactly one selected surface is supplied;
- exactly one downstream matter/use is supplied;
- the surface identity and reference are exact and unique within the envelope;
- the selected surface's family and type are explicit;
- the family-local standing result or assertion is exact, readable, and attributed to that family;
- the family-owned standing contract is exact, readable, and attributed to that family;
- the contract explicitly governs the supplied result or assertion and selected surface type;
- the standing-effect location is exact and readable;
- the evidence and basis references required by the family-owned contract are exact and readable as supplied references;
- the lineage required to bind result, basis, contract, and surface is exact and preserved;
- the result's applicable scope, rank, custody, and non-claims are explicit;
- the downstream matter/use is compatible with the contract-owned scope, rank, custody, applicability, and non-claims;
- the exact standing-basis binding names the same surface, family, result or assertion, contract, basis, lineage, applicability, and downstream matter/use as the envelope;
- no value is inferred from recency, path order, timestamp, sequence number, repository availability, or convenience;
- no rank is upgraded and no custody is silently transferred;
- no source-family semantics are reinterpreted;
- no standing vocabulary is centralized or normalized; and
- all required admission non-claims remain false.

These are envelope checks only. They do not establish standing and do not authorize a future resolver to become the semantic owner of heterogeneous source-family contracts.

## 11. Visible Stopping Conditions

Admission must stop visibly when:

- the selected surface or downstream matter/use is missing, multiple, vague, or unbounded;
- exact identity, reference, family, type, or custody is missing or contradictory;
- the family-local result or assertion is missing, unreadable, malformed, unsupported, ambiguous, or falsely attributed;
- the family-owned contract is missing, unreadable, malformed, unsupported, ambiguous, or does not govern the supplied result or surface type;
- the standing-effect location is missing or cannot be read without inventing semantics;
- required evidence, basis, or lineage is missing, unreadable, contradictory, or manufactured;
- applicable scope, rank, custody, or non-claims are missing or incompatible with the downstream matter/use;
- the exact standing-basis binding is missing, incomplete, contradictory, or binds a different surface, result, contract, basis, or use;
- a naked outcome, boolean, filename, directory, timestamp, sequence number, receipt, repository presence, latest inference, or adjacency is offered as sufficient basis;
- source-family standing vocabulary would have to be reinterpreted or normalized;
- a universal standing outcome list, registry, catalogue, inventory, or coverage authority would be required;
- standing would be inferred from an adjacent condition or another family;
- admission would establish, renew, generalize, upgrade, transfer, canonicalize, or globally recognize standing;
- admission would be reused for another surface, matter, scope, or downstream operation;
- admission would create correspondence, conformance, coherence, currentness, authority, source transfer, receiver standing, execution, or continuation; or
- required non-claims are missing or flipped.

Where a complete readable envelope clearly falls outside contract-owned scope, rank, custody, or use applicability, the outcome is `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_NOT_ADMITTED`.

Where a bounded non-collapse-shaped envelope lacks an exact required fact, the outcome may be `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_REQUIRES_ADDITIONAL_BASIS`.

Where lawful review itself cannot proceed, the outcome is `SELECTED_SURFACE_STANDING_BASIS_REVIEW_BLOCKED`.

No stopping posture repairs, invalidates, downgrades, or reinterprets the selected surface or its source-family standing result.

## 12. Anti-Landlord Boundary

It must not create or maintain:

The boundary reads one supplied family-owned contract only for one exact admission question. It does not decide what standing language another family must use and does not become the source of standing semantics for any family.
