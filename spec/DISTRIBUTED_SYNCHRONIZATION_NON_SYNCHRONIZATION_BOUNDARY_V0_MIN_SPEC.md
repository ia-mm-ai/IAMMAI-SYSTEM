# Distributed Synchronization / Non-Synchronization Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the distributed synchronization / non-synchronization boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

This is the next lawful gate after distributed carrier operational role basis. It exists to prevent carrier role basis from becoming synchronization, shared live state, repository merge, full body transfer, second body, or divergent live operation by implication.

This boundary reviews one eligible declared distributed operation matter only. It may define synchronization and non-synchronization as future boundary postures for that matter after source-body operational authority basis and distributed carrier operational role basis have been recorded.

This boundary does not authorize synchronization.

This boundary does not authorize non-synchronized distributed operation.

This boundary does not admit, authorize, execute, merge, replay, transfer, continue, or produce consequence.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

The distributed operation matter has been declared. Distributed operation eligibility has recorded the matter eligible for future admission / authority review. Source-body operational authority basis has been recorded. Distributed carrier operational role basis has been recorded.

Carrier role basis did not activate roles. Carrier role basis did not admit or authorize operation. Carrier role basis preserved no synchronization, no full body transfer, no second body, no continuation, and no distributed operation.

Before refusal/abort or admission can be considered, synchronization and non-synchronization must be bounded.

Synchronization language can smuggle repository synchronization, shared live state, state merge, latest-carrier wins, majority/quorum fraud, copy-equals-current, full body transfer, or second-body creation.

Non-synchronization language can smuggle divergent live operation, unsupervised carrier autonomy, stale carrier operation, or split-body behavior.

This boundary prevents both synchronization and non-synchronization from becoming hidden operation.

## 4. Definitions

`distributed synchronization / non-synchronization boundary` means the bounded review surface that defines synchronization and non-synchronization postures for one eligible declared matter before any future operation admission.

`selected carrier role basis` means the distributed carrier operational role basis selected for this review. It remains role basis only and does not activate carriers.

`synchronization posture` means a declared posture about repository synchronization, shared live state, state merge, replay, body transfer, second body, and evidence merge behavior. It is not synchronization authorization.

`non-synchronization posture` means a declared posture about refusing synchronization defaults and preserving unsynchronized evidence/context. It is not authorization for carriers to operate independently.

`synchronization candidate` means a proposed synchronization posture that may be reviewed as boundary posture only.

`non-synchronization candidate` means a proposed non-synchronization posture that may be reviewed as boundary posture only.

`synchronized state` means a state claimed to be shared, merged, current, or operationally common across carriers. This boundary does not create synchronized state.

`unsynchronized evidence` means carrier evidence that remains visible and unmerged. It does not become shared live state.

`non-synchronized context` means carrier context preserved without synchronization and without independent operational authority.

`shared live state` means a state that would be treated as jointly current or operational across carriers. This boundary must not create it.

`state merge` means combining carrier or repository states into one asserted standing state. This boundary must not authorize it.

`repository synchronization` means synchronization of repositories, records, or standing surfaces. This boundary must not authorize it.

`full body transfer` means transferring the full body into another carrier, repository, or execution line. This boundary must not authorize it.

`second body` means a separate body created or treated as live from carrier context, synchronization, or non-synchronization. This boundary must not create it.

`split-body risk` means the risk that synchronization or non-synchronization language creates more than one live body or divergent live operation.

`sync refusal` means explicit preservation that synchronization, merge, transfer, replay, second body, and non-synchronized operation are not authorized here.

`sync non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that no synchronization, non-synchronized operation, merge, transfer, second body, operation, consequence, or follow-on work has been authorized.

Synchronization review does not authorize synchronization.

Non-synchronization review does not authorize non-synchronized operation.

No-sync does not mean carriers may operate independently.

Sync does not mean repositories may merge.

Both synchronization and non-synchronization remain boundary postures only.

## 22. Closing Boundary Statement

Distributed synchronization / non-synchronization boundary may define and review synchronization and non-synchronization postures for one eligible distributed operation matter with source-body authority basis and carrier role basis recorded. It preserves that sync/non-sync review does not authorize repository synchronization, create shared live state, merge state, authorize replay, transfer the body, create a second body, authorize non-synchronized operation, activate carrier roles, admit operation, authorize operation, execute operation, create consequence, create public readiness, claim final completion, or schedule follow-on work.

`What synchronization and non-synchronization postures may be recognized before any future operation admission?`

It does not open the question:

`May repositories synchronize or carriers operate without synchronization?`
