## 4. Definitions

`source_body_reception_eligibility_admissibility_boundary` means a bounded review surface that determines whether one declared source-body reception request is eligible and admissible for later reception-family review after request declaration, identity preservation, and receiving-context role have been recorded. It is eligibility / admissibility only.

`selected_receiving_context_role_result` means one source-body reception receiving-context role result whose outcome is `SOURCE_BODY_RECEPTION_RECEIVING_CONTEXT_ROLE_RECORDED` and whose failed check count is zero.

`reception_eligibility` means the bounded posture that a declared request may proceed to later reception-family review if that later review is separately created and bounded. It is not reception, recognition, authorization, source receipt, authority, currentness, or standing.

`reception_admissibility` means the bounded posture that the declared request has enough basis to be reviewed later without immediate capture. It is not authorization and does not grant permission.

`eligible_for_later_reception_review` means the request may be considered by later reception-family boundaries only if those future boundaries are separately declared, bounded, and checked.

`admissible_for_later_reception_review` means the request is sufficiently bounded for later review without converting eligibility into reception, admissibility into authorization, or review-readiness into source receipt.

`review_readiness_posture` means the limited state of being ready for later review. It does not create standing state, operation permission, source-body reception, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, or follow-on authority.

`eligibility_not_reception` means eligibility does not recognize reception, receive source, adopt source, validate source, or create source-body reception.

`admissibility_not_authorization` means admissibility does not authorize reception, source receipt, adoption, validation, invalidation, operation, publication, continuation, or follow-on work.

`eligibility_admissibility_non_claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that eligibility / admissibility did not recognize reception, authorize reception, receive source, create receiving-context governance, make receiving context source/authority/current/receiver/adopter/validator/invalidator/operator, validate source, invalidate source, replace source, create adoption, authority, currentness, standing, standing propagation, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, non-capture passed posture, recognition passed posture, mutation, replay, or merge.

`inadmissible_request` means a readable declared reception request whose eligibility / admissibility basis fails review-readiness or cannot be separated from capture, authorization, recognition, source receipt, operation permission, governance, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, or follow-on authority.

`insufficient_eligibility_basis` means the declared request is readable and non-collapse-shaped, but the eligibility basis is not specific enough to record eligibility for later review.

`capture_shaped_eligibility` means an eligibility / admissibility posture that attempts to become reception, authorization, source receipt, adoption, authority, currentness, validation, invalidation, operation permission, receiving-context governance, publication flow, public readiness, final completion, continuation, follow-on work, non-capture passed posture, or recognition passed posture.

`recognition_risk_posture` means any posture where eligibility / admissibility could be mistaken for source-body reception recognition or used as evidence that recognition has already passed.

Eligible means a request may proceed to later reception-family review if separately created.

Admissible means the request has enough bounded basis to be reviewed later without immediate capture.

Even when eligibility / admissibility is recorded, no adoption, authority, currentness, standing, vessel relation, derivative relation, operation permission, receiving-context governance, public readiness, final completion, continuation, publication flow, or follow-on work is created.

## 21. Closing Boundary Statement

Source-body reception eligibility / admissibility boundary may record that one declared source-body reception request is eligible and admissible for later reception-family review after request declaration, identity preservation, and receiving-context role have been recorded. It preserves that eligibility / admissibility is not reception, not authorization, not source receipt, not adoption, not authority, not currentness, not validation, not invalidation, not operation permission, not governance, not publication flow, not public readiness, not final completion, not continuation, and not follow-on work. Non-capture and reception recognition remain separate future boundaries.

It closes the question:

`Is this declared source-body reception request eligible and admissible for later reception-family review?`

It does not open the question:

`Is source-body reception recognized, authorized, received, adopted, current, operative, or public-ready now?`
