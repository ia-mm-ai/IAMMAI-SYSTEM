Current self-orientation is an internal derived recognition surface. It asks:

- given the preserved artifacts currently standing, what can the body lawfully recognize about its own current, governing, derivative, open, blocked/refused, touchable, continuity, vessel-facing, and operator-facing posture
- does that recognition remain lawfully proportioned to the source surfaces it derives from

This is not external reader orientation. It is not a README, repo map, onboarding layer, public explanation layer, or hand-maintained summary seam.

## 7. Recognition Categories

Current self-orientation may recognize the following bounded categories.

### 7.1 Current Executable / Core Line

Recognition is derived from standing execution-authority and governing/effective surfaces. It is not authority creation. It must not be guessed from file recency alone.

### 7.2 Current Governing / Effective Basis

Recognition is derived from explicit current-governing, governing-transition, governing re-resolution, successor-adoption, effective-family, and effective-family-consumption surfaces. It must not be replaced by latest-file inference.

### 7.3 Current-State Surfaces

Recognition may include readout, handoff, export, delivery, application, answer/read, query, what-stands-now, and what-remains-open surfaces where they stand. Recognition must preserve their explicit outcomes and lineage.

### 7.4 Continuity / Transfer Surfaces

Recognition may include touch permission, continuity transfer, transfer receipt, continuity memory seam, and body-pass surfaces. Recognition must preserve transfer, receipt, seam, and body-pass as distinct functions rather than flattening them into one memory label.

### 7.5 Derivative Participation / Action-Permission Surfaces

Recognition may include received derivative participation and received derivative action permission. Recognition must preserve source, transfer, receipt, participation, and action-permission distinction.

### 7.6 API / Vessel / Operator-Facing Derivative Surfaces

Recognition may include bounded derivative vessel outputs and operator-facing terminal brief outputs only as downstream derivative posture. They may not determine current or governing basis.

### 7.7 Open Surfaces

Recognition may include what-remains-open surfaces and carried false non-claims. Open posture must not be silently converted into completed posture.

### 7.8 Blocked / Refused Surfaces

Recognition may include blocked or refused surfaces where they are part of the standing line. They must remain visible and must not be hidden as noise.

### 7.9 Touch / Admissibility Surfaces

Recognition may include admissibility and touch-permission posture where present. Recognition must not upgrade touchability into general permission or authority.

### 7.10 Carried Non-Claims

Recognition may carry visible false non-claims forward. Recognition must not flip them, drop them, or convert them into implied completion.

## 8. Bounded Correspondence / Proportion Checks

Continuity requires declared bounds of correspondence, not exact duplication and not arbitrary drift.

Current self-orientation must not only recognize posture. It must also remain proportioned to the source surfaces it derives from.

These correspondence checks are an internal check family inside self-orientation, not a new standalone proportion doctrine.

At minimum, the future implementation must verify:

- every source surface named by self-orientation is readable
- recognized current surfaces match the outcome and posture of the source artifacts they derive from

- recognized derivative surfaces remain marked as derivative

- what-remains-open posture is not converted into completed posture
- blocked or refused artifacts are not hidden as noise
- non-claims remain false and are carried forward
- self-orientation does not claim more than its source artifacts support
- self-orientation does not omit required current surfaces when they are standing and relevant
- self-orientation does not flatten current, governing, derivative, open, blocked/refused, and touchable posture into one undifferentiated overview blob
- self-orientation does not over-mirror by trying to become the whole body
- self-orientation does not under-mirror by thinning required current posture into vague summary
