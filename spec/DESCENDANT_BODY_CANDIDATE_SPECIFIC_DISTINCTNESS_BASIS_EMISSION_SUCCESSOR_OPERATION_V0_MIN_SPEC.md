# Descendant Body Candidate Specific Distinctness Basis Emission Successor Operation V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This specification defines one future candidate-specific distinctness basis emission successor operation shape. It asks whether, after the prior additional-basis gap has been lawfully closed, a future operation may emit bounded, separate, candidate-specific basis material for Candidate A and Candidate B.

It does not emit material, create candidate records, mark records distinct, rerun distinctness, record `DISTINCTNESS_SUPPORTED`, authorize candidate standing, create descendant bodies or standing descendants, authorize crossing, create relation, FIELD machinery, runtime, API, currentness, authority, output, action, derivative reception, synchronization, or follow-on work. It does not assign or create coupling, admit a third candidate or model, establish presence, create identity, scan the repository, discover files, repair the affected file, validate prior unsupported claims, or override or bypass an upstream completed line.

## 2. Scope

This specification is limited to `EMIT_CANDIDATE_SPECIFIC_BASIS_MATERIAL_AFTER_SUCCESSOR_CLOSURE_ONLY`.

It defines a future successor emission operation shape only. It is not a successor emission operation result, candidate-specific content emission, distinctness support, candidate records distinctness, candidate standing, descendant-body creation, crossing, relation, runtime, currentness, authority, coupling, presence, identity, or follow-on authorization.

## 3. Operation Question

Given that the earlier candidate-specific distinctness basis emission operation recorded `REQUIRES_ADDITIONAL_BASIS` because non-cosmetic candidate A scope declaration, non-cosmetic candidate B scope declaration, and basis-bearing scope division declaration were missing, and that the authored V2 declaration was later received, audited, digest/custody sealed, and the prior additional-basis gap was closed by successor closure, may the system define one future candidate-specific distinctness basis emission successor operation that may emit bounded, separate, candidate-specific basis material for Candidate A and Candidate B without marking candidate records distinct, recording `DISTINCTNESS_SUPPORTED`, authorizing candidate standing, creating descendant bodies or relation, assigning coupling, establishing presence or identity, or authorizing follow-on work?

## 4. Required Answer

Yes, but only as a successor emission operation specification. No material is emitted here. No distinctness support is recorded here. No candidate records are marked distinct here. No standing is created here.
