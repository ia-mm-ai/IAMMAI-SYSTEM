It defines how body-side current posture may lawfully account for plural carrier evidence after carrier lifecycle, registry/persistence, standing propagation, and continuity-turn posture have been bounded.

The purpose is to prevent plural carrier evidence, registry reference, lifecycle status, standing propagation, or continuity-turn lineage from becoming currentness by:

- possession
- receipt
- return
- admission
- divergence
- relation
- lifecycle
- registry
- propagation
- recency
- majority
- success count
- availability

Currentness participation is not currentness.

Currentness successor is not distributed standing.

Carrier evidence may inform body-side current posture without any carrier becoming current.

Plural carrier evidence is not majority currentness.

Successful receipt is not currentness.

Blocked receipt is not exclusion by default.

Lifecycle status is not currentness.

Registry presence is not currentness.

Standing propagation is not currentness.

## 4. Definitions

`cross-carrier currentness successor` means a bounded body-side successor posture that may account for selected plural carrier evidence in current posture without making any carrier current.

`body-side current posture` means the current posture recognized by the body line under body-side basis. It may account for evidence. It is not held by a carrier and is not transferred by a carrier.

`carrier currentness` means the forbidden posture that a carrier, carrier copy, carrier receipt, carrier status, carrier registry reference, carrier lifecycle status, carrier propagation status, carrier relation, or carrier continuity turn has become current by itself.

`current carrier` means a carrier selected as current. This boundary does not select a current carrier.

`currentness participation` means selected carrier evidence may participate in later currentness review under explicit basis. Participation is not currentness, does not force accounting, and does not select a carrier.

`currentness account` means the body-side accounting of selected plural carrier evidence for one declared currentness successor question. Accounting evidence does not make that evidence current.

`currentness successor basis` means the declared currentness successor question, selected body-side current posture, selected carrier evidence identities, selected evidence outcomes, prior participation basis where applicable, lifecycle basis where applicable, registry/persistence basis where applicable, standing propagation basis where applicable, continuity-turn basis where applicable, relation/conformance/closure basis where applicable, visible refusal, visible divergence, blocked attempts, projection mismatch, detailed basis, summary projection, lineage, and explicit non-claims.

`currentness successor claim` means the bounded assertion that selected plural carrier evidence has been accounted for in body-side current posture under declared basis and non-claims.

`currentness successor refusal` means refusal to record currentness successor posture because required basis is missing, malformed, hidden, summary-overwriting, carrier-currentness-shaped, current-carrier-selection-shaped, source-replacing, authority-creating, permission-creating, distributed-standing-shaped, continuation-shaped, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`plural carrier evidence` means selected evidence from more than one carrier, carrier line, carrier role, carrier lifecycle posture, registry/persistence reference, standing propagation posture, continuity-turn posture, relation posture, conformance posture, closure posture, receipt, refusal, admission, or divergence surface.

`currentness-relevant evidence` means selected evidence that may be accounted for in a declared body-side current posture question under explicit basis. It is not currentness by itself.

`currentness-ineligible evidence` means selected evidence preserved as evidence but not eligible for accounting in the declared currentness successor question.

`currentness non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that currentness successor did not create carrier currentness, select a current carrier, select a winning carrier, invalidate a losing carrier, replace source, create authority, create permission, create carrier hierarchy, resolve divergence, erase evidence, create distributed standing, authorize synchronization, authorize full body transfer, create a second body, authorize continuation, or authorize distributed operation.

Body-side current posture may account for evidence.

Carrier evidence does not become currentness.

No carrier becomes current by being accounted for.
