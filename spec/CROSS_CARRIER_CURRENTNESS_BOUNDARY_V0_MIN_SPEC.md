# Cross-Carrier Currentness Boundary V0 Minimum Specification

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the bounded cross-carrier currentness boundary for the present `IAMMAI-SYSTEM` execution line.

It defines what may participate in body-side current posture across carrier-produced, carrier-returned, carrier-admitted, corresponded, or divergent evidence.

The purpose is to prevent carrier possession, carrier emission, carrier return, receipt, admission, correspondence, divergence, timestamp, majority, success count, carrier availability, carrier label, carrier count, or convenience selection from becoming currentness.

Carrier-held does not mean current.

Carrier-emitted does not mean current.

Carrier-received does not mean current.

Carrier-returned does not mean current.

Carrier-admitted-as-evidence does not mean current.

Carrier-corresponded does not mean current.

Carrier-divergent does not mean current.

This file defines currentness participation conditions and currentness refusal conditions only. It does not create currentness by itself.

This file is a boundary specification only. It is not an implementation, resolver, test, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, carrier relation law, carrier registry, persistence layer, repository synchronization, full body transfer, second-body creation, successor carrier law, presence surface, threshold surface, truth surface, action surface, consequence surface, signal series logic, body relevance medium, workflow, router, roadmap, README, or manifesto.

## 3. Why This Spec Is Needed Now

The carrier role and emission boundary defines local carrier roles and role-permitted emissions.

The carrier-local emission admission boundary defines how carrier-local emissions may enter the body line as evidence only.

The cross-carrier surface receipt boundary proves that one carried surface may be received by one physical carrier as carried evidence without creating source, currentness, authority, permission, successor standing, body formation, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

Returned receipt evidence and returned refusal evidence now stand as downstream evidence.

The cross-carrier divergence boundary preserves visible mismatch between returned blocked receipt evidence and returned successful receipt evidence without resolving that mismatch into truth, currentness, action, carrier correctness, carrier hierarchy, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The cross-surface correspondence boundary permits selected standing surfaces to be read together while preserving rank, source, scope, lineage, local outcome, and non-claims.

Current self-orientation v7 mirrors carrier evidence and refusal visibility as downstream posture.

Current-body conformance v2 verifies that the v7-integrated posture coheres as one current body line without opening carrier relation, authority, permission, signal, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, multi-carrier law, distributed standing, or continuation.

The body has not yet bounded what can and cannot participate in current posture across carriers.

Cross-carrier currentness must be bounded before multi-carrier relation law can be considered.

This boundary remains narrower than multi-carrier law. It defines currentness participation and refusal posture only.

## 4. Definitions

`carrier-held evidence` means evidence present on, held by, or available through a carrier. Carrier-held evidence is not current by possession, availability, location, recency, or carrier label.

`carrier-produced evidence` means an operation-local output emitted by a carrier under a declared carrier role, including receipt, refusal, manifest, integrity evidence, transfer declaration, returned evidence packet, or bounded passive storage status where applicable.

`carrier-returned evidence` means carrier-produced evidence brought back or made available to the body line. Returned evidence is not admitted by return and is not current by return.

`carrier-admitted evidence` means carrier-local or returned evidence admitted into the body line as downstream evidence by a bounded body-side admission surface. Admission as evidence does not create currentness.

`carrier-divergent evidence` means selected carrier-produced, carrier-returned, carrier-admitted, or corresponded evidence that visibly mismatches another selected carrier evidence surface under a declared divergence basis.

`currentness basis` means an explicit body-side basis that states the currentness question, selected body-side current posture, selected carrier evidence identities, carrier identities where required, evidence outcomes, source or carried basis where available, admission status where applicable, correspondence status where applicable, divergence status where applicable, lineage, and non-claims.

`cross-carrier currentness boundary` means this boundary for deciding what selected carrier evidence may participate in body-side current posture assessment without letting carrier possession, carrier status, carrier count, receipt, return, admission, correspondence, divergence, recency, majority, success count, or convenience create currentness.

`currentness participation` means selected carrier evidence may be considered in a later body-side current posture assessment under explicit currentness basis and non-claims. Participation is not currentness creation.

`currentness exclusion` means selected carrier evidence remains preserved but may not participate in current posture for the declared question.

`body-side current posture` means a current posture recognized by the body line under body-side basis. It is not held by a carrier, created by a carrier, or transferred by a carrier.

`carrier-local currentness claim` means any claim that a carrier, local copy, returned artifact, receipt, admission, correspondence, divergence, timestamp, success count, majority, availability, convenience path, or carrier label creates currentness.

`currentness refusal` means a requested currentness participation posture is refused because basis is missing, malformed, unsupported, hidden-divergence-shaped, recency-shaped, majority-shaped, success-count-shaped, carrier-sovereignty-shaped, source-replacing, or otherwise collapse-shaped.

`currentness non-claim` means an explicit preserved false posture stating that carrier evidence did not create currentness, replace source, create authority, create permission, create successor standing, create body formation, hide divergence, hide refusal, select a winning carrier, invalidate a losing carrier, or create multi-carrier law, distributed standing, presence, threshold, truth, action, consequence, or continuation.

## 5. What Cross-Carrier Currentness Means

Cross-carrier currentness means a body-side posture may consider selected carrier evidence in relation to current body posture only when:

- evidence was admitted or otherwise selected under a bounded basis
- carrier role and emission basis are preserved
- return basis is preserved where applicable
- admission basis is preserved where applicable
- correspondence state is visible where applicable
- divergence state is visible where applicable
- selected evidence remains downstream
- lineage is preserved
- currentness basis is explicit
- non-claims remain false

Cross-carrier currentness is not held by a carrier.

Cross-carrier currentness is not created by a carrier.

Cross-carrier currentness is not transferred by a carrier.

Cross-carrier currentness is recognized only by body-side currentness basis.

This boundary permits only the possibility that selected carrier evidence may participate in a later body-side current posture assessment. It does not say that the carrier is current. It does not say that the selected evidence is current by itself.

- `CURRENTNESS_PARTICIPATION_ELIGIBLE`

`CURRENTNESS_PARTICIPATION_ELIGIBLE` means selected evidence may participate in a later body-side current posture assessment under explicit basis and non-claims. It does not mean the carrier is current. It does not mean the evidence became current by itself.

`CURRENTNESS_PARTICIPATION_EXCLUDED` means selected evidence remains preserved but may not participate in current posture for the declared question.

## 10. Relation to Carrier Role and Emission Boundary

## 13. Relation to Cross-Carrier Divergence

Divergence does not create currentness.
