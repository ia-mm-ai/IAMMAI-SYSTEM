## 4. Definitions

`packet_transfer` means one future bounded transfer/copy posture, if separately resolved and recorded. Packet transfer specification is not packet transfer.

`bounded_packet_transfer` means one transfer/copy action posture only, not receipt, execution, cross-carrier proof, source, authority, currentness, runtime, final completion, continuation, reusable permission, or follow-on work.

`copy_to_another_device` means making packet material available to a distinct carrier. By itself it is not second-carrier receipt.

`transfer_artifact` means a future artifact recording bounded transfer/copy posture, if separately resolved and recorded.

`copy_artifact` means a future artifact recording bounded copy posture, if separately resolved and recorded.

`second_carrier_receipt` means future receipt acknowledgment by second carrier and does not occur by this spec.

`second_carrier_execution` means future execution by second carrier and does not occur by this spec.

`source_transfer` does not occur by this spec.

`source_receipt` does not occur by this spec.

`reception_authorization` does not occur by this spec.

`external_result_artifact` does not exist by this spec.

`cross_carrier_evidence_review` does not occur by this spec.

`packet_transfer_boundary_basis` means the clean transfer-boundary artifact and terminal summary.

`packet_emission_basis` means the clean packet-emission artifact and terminal summary.

`emitted_packet_basis` remains bounded local emission posture basis.

`transfer_as_receipt` is blocked.

`transfer_as_execution` is blocked.

`transfer_as_source_transfer` is blocked.

`transfer_as_source_receipt` is blocked.

`transfer_as_reception_authorization` is blocked.

`transfer_as_cross_carrier_proof` is blocked.

`transfer_as_source` is blocked.

`transfer_as_authority` is blocked.

`transfer_as_currentness` is blocked.

`transfer_as_runtime` is blocked.

`transfer_as_final_completion` is blocked.

`transfer_as_follow_on_work` is blocked.

`copy_presence` means a file or packet copy is visible somewhere. Copy presence alone is not receipt, authority, currentness, or proof.

`carrier_possession` means a carrier has a copy or path. Possession alone is not receipt, source, authority, currentness, cross-carrier evidence, or runtime.

`hidden_repo_state` means any dependency not declared in selected transfer basis.

`repo_local_availability` means repository-local presence, path reachability, cache state, current working tree state, or carrier-local artifact visibility. Repo-local availability is not transfer authority.

`selected_basis_reference_shape` means selected transfer basis remains bounded and reference-shaped unless a later transfer resolver explicitly admits bounded transfer/copy material.

`raw_full_prior_artifact_body` means an unbounded body from an earlier artifact or source. It must not be returned unless a later transfer resolver explicitly admits bounded transfer material.

`packet_transfer_non_claim` means explicit false posture preventing transfer from becoming receipt, execution, source, authority, currentness, cross-carrier proof, runtime, final completion, continuation, reusable permission, or follow-on work.
