## 4. Definitions

`distributed standing boundary conformance closure` means bounded recording of what one selected distributed standing boundary conformance result means and does not mean.

`selected conformance result` means one declared distributed standing boundary conformance artifact whose outcome is `DISTRIBUTED_STANDING_BOUNDARY_CONFORMANT`.

`closure basis` means the declared closure question, selected conformance result identity, selected conformance result outcome, parseability where supplied, conformance checks, conformance statement, conformance summary, selected distributed standing boundary result identity, prerequisite basis conformance, refusal/divergence/lineage conformance, non-claim conformance, summary/detail correspondence, and explicit non-claims that permit closure of meaning.

`closure claim` means the bounded assertion that the meaning and non-meaning of the selected conformant distributed standing boundary conformance result have been recorded.

`closure refusal` means refusal to close meaning because the selected conformance result, selected result identity, outcome, parseability, conformance checks, conformance statement, non-claims, or no-expansion posture are missing, malformed, failed, hidden, contradictory, or collapse-shaped.

`closure meaning` means the bounded meaning that the selected conformance result verified conformance of the selected distributed standing boundary result to its declared basis and non-claims.

`closure non-meaning` means the preserved posture that closure did not create permission, continuation, operation, synchronization, full body transfer, second body, carrier currentness, carrier selection, authority, truth, action, implementation, final completion, or self-orientation successor by default.

`conformance closure non-claim` means an explicit false posture preserving that closure did not mutate the selected conformance result, mutate the selected distributed standing boundary result, expand conformance, authorize operation, create permission, claim final completion, or schedule a self-orientation successor.

Closure of conformance does not create new distributed standing.

Closure of conformance does not mutate the conformance result.
