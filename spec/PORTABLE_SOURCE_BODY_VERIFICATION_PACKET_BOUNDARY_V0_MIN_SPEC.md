## 4. Definitions

`portable_verification_packet_boundary` means a boundary over local command-success basis for one future packet step.

Packet boundary is not the packet.

Packet boundary is not packet emission.

Packet boundary is not transfer.

Packet boundary is not second-carrier execution.

Packet boundary is not cross-carrier evidence.

`portable_verification_packet` means a future copyable packet artifact, if separately specified and lawfully emitted.

`packet_emission` means future creation/write of the packet artifact. It does not occur by this spec.

`packet_artifact` means a future bounded packet artifact, if separately specified and lawfully emitted. It does not exist by this spec.

`packet_transfer` means moving or copying packet to another carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`second_carrier` means another device, local environment, or carrier distinct from the current repository carrier.

`second_carrier_execution` means future execution/checking on that second carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`second_carrier_receipt` means future bounded receipt posture on a second carrier. It does not occur by this spec.

`external_result_artifact` means a future result artifact from a second carrier or external execution context. It does not exist by this spec.

`cross_carrier_evidence_review` means future review of external result artifacts after second-carrier execution. It does not occur by this spec.

`local_command_success_basis` means the clean command success artifact and terminal summary, preserving that local command success is not packet emission, not source, not authority, not currentness, not final completion, and not cross-carrier proof.

`command_success_as_packet_permission` means treating command success as authority to create, emit, transfer, or execute a packet. This is blocked.

`packet_as_source` means treating a packet or packet boundary as source or source-body standing. This is blocked.

`packet_as_authority` means treating a packet or packet boundary as authority. This is blocked.

`packet_as_currentness` means treating a packet, packet path, packet timestamp, or packet possession as currentness. This is blocked.

`packet_as_cross_carrier_proof` means treating packet boundary, packet existence, packet path, or packet possession as proof that another carrier received, executed, or verified anything. This is blocked.

`packet_as_runtime` means treating a packet or packet boundary as runtime hosting or runtime permission. This is blocked.

`packet_boundary_non_claim` means an explicit false posture that prevents packet-boundary review from becoming packet creation, packet emission, source transfer, source receipt, cross-carrier evidence, source, authority, currentness, final completion, runtime, continuation, reusable permission, or follow-on work.

`copyable_basis` means future packet contents must be selected and bounded explicitly, not silently inherited from local repo state.

`hidden_repo_state` means any dependency on unlisted files, current working tree, local path assumptions, environment state, account, vendor, cache, memory, or unstated lineage. Hidden repo state cannot be used as packet authority.

`selected_basis_reference_shape` means selected basis is reference-shaped and bounded, not full raw prior body unless a later packet spec explicitly admits a bounded copyable body.

`raw_full_prior_artifact_body_not_returned` means raw full prior artifact body is not returned, emitted, embedded, or treated as authority by the packet boundary.
