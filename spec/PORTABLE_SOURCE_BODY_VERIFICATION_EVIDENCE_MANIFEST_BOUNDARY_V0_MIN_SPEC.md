## 4. Definitions

`evidence_manifest_boundary` means the bounded review surface that defines evidence classes and evidence-only posture for future portable source-body verification. It is not a manifest file, command, checksum, signature, packet, runtime, deployment, public release, or implementation.

`evidence_manifest_basis` means a declared list or structure of evidence classes for future verification. It is not source, currentness, command execution, implementation, permission, deployment, public release, final completion, or continuation.

`evidence_class` means a named category of checkable evidence, such as source surfaces, spec surfaces, resolver surfaces, test surfaces, artifact roots, closure artifacts, terminal summaries, corrected closure evidence, failed-input evidence posture, or future manifest / checksum / signature / packet / command candidates.

`required_source_surfaces` means declared source-side surfaces selected as evidence for future verification. They do not become source by being listed.

`required_spec_surfaces` means declared specification surfaces selected as evidence for future verification. They do not become authority by being listed.

`required_resolver_surfaces` means declared resolver source files selected as evidence for future verification. They do not become command execution or runtime hosting by being listed.

`required_test_surfaces` means declared test surfaces selected as evidence for future verification. They do not create final completion, operation permission, deployment, runtime hosting, or public release by being listed.

`required_artifact_roots` means declared artifact directories selected as evidence roots. Their existence does not create currentness, source standing, authority, or permission.

`required_closure_artifacts` means declared closure artifacts selected as evidence that a prior boundary line closed. They do not authorize next work.

`required_terminal_summaries` means declared terminal-summary surfaces selected as readability evidence. They do not replace underlying specs, resolvers, tests, or artifacts.

`corrected_closure_artifact_evidence` means a declared posture that a corrected closure artifact may be selected where relevant while failed-input evidence remains visible. It does not repair, delete, normalize, overwrite, or mutate any artifact.

`failed_input_evidence_posture` means a declared posture preserving failed-input artifacts as visible evidence only. Failed-input evidence does not become authority, source, currentness, permission, or repair authorization.

`manifest_candidate` means future evidence language for a possible manifest surface. It is not a manifest implementation.

`checksum_candidate` means future evidence language for possible checksum evidence. It is not a checksum implementation.

`signature_candidate` means future evidence language for possible signature evidence. It is not a signature implementation.

`source_body_packet_candidate` means future evidence language for a possible source-body packet. It is not packet implementation, source transfer, source migration, carrier expansion, or multi-carrier standing.

`command_candidate` means future evidence language for a possible verification command. It is not command implementation, command invocation, command output, runtime hosting, deployment, public release, or operation permission.

`evidence_only_posture` means evidence may be named for later checking without becoming source, authority, currentness, permission, deployment, runtime hosting, public release, final completion, continuation, or follow-on authority.

`evidence_overread` means treating evidence, manifest candidates, checksum candidates, signature candidates, packet candidates, command candidates, repository copies, paths, artifact existence, carrier possession, or narration as source, authority, currentness, permission, implementation, deployment, public release, or continuation.

`artifact_existence_currentness` means the invalid posture that an artifact exists and therefore creates currentness.

`repository_copy_as_body` means the invalid posture that a repository copy becomes the body by possession or locality.

`path_currentness` means the invalid posture that a filesystem path creates currentness.

`latest_file_currentness` means the invalid posture that latest-file posture or timestamp creates currentness.

`carrier_possession_currentness` means the invalid posture that carrier possession, archive possession, or local custody creates currentness.

Manifest, checksum, signature, packet, and command language in this spec is future evidence language only.
